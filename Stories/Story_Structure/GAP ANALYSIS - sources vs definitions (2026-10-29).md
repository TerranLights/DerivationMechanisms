## John Truby, *The Anatomy of Story* (PDF on disk)
**The 22 steps** (his list, PDF page 119): 1 Self-revelation, need and desire · 2 Ghost and story world · 3 Weakness and need · 4 Inciting event · 5 Desire · 6 Ally or allies · 7 Opponent and/or mystery · 8 Fake-ally opponent · 9 First revelation and decision · 10 Plan · 11 Opponent's plan and main counterattack · 12 Drive · 13 Attack by ally · 14 Apparent defeat · 15 Second revelation and decision · 16 Audience revelation · 17 Third revelation and decision · 18 Gate, gauntlet, visit to death · 19 Battle · 20 Self-revelation · 21 Moral decision · 22 New equilibrium. His 7 key steps are a subset: weakness and need, desire, opponent, plan, battle, self-revelation, new equilibrium.

- **In the master:**
  - ✅ Self-revelation (20), Moral decision (21), Fake-ally (8), Ally (6), Opponent (7, as "Opposition", only "self-explanatory").
  - ≈ Inciting event (4) as Catalyst; Apparent defeat (14) as All Is Lost.
- **❌ Not in the master:** Ghost and story world, Weakness and need (as a structural step), Desire, the three revelations and decisions (9, 15, 17), Plan, Opponent's plan and main counterattack, Drive, Attack by ally, Audience revelation, Gate/gauntlet/visit to death, Battle, New equilibrium.
- **The arc template already uses** Plan, Opposition's Main Counter-Attack (11), Fake-Ally Attack (13), Battle and New Equilibrium without definitions. Need and desire appear as fields in the character templates.
