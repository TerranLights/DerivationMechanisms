## 2. Settled so far (from the files already read; do not redo)

- **Deuterium is not scarce.** Seawater holds about 33 to 34 mg/L, about 4.6 × 10¹³ t in the oceans. The national D-T need for Tepenia is about 11 t a year (GDD `Tepenia_Fusion_and_Uranium_Discussion_2026-11-02.md` §2), about 55 t of D₂O, about 3.3 × 10⁵ m³ of seawater a year at 100% recovery. (Note §2, DERIVED.)
- **Tritium stays scarce.** World commercial stock 20 to 30 kg; a D-T plant must breed its own tritium from lithium; Li-6 enrichment is the other bottleneck (GDD `Fusion_F2_Fuel_Cycle_Research_2026-11-02.md`, SOURCED there).
- **Fusion is unproven.** No net-energy D-³He result; magnetic fusion had not reached Q = 1 (`benefits_of_mining_the_sun.md` §F2; GDD `Fusion_F1_...`).
- **Canon base:** the Census's non-city maritime fleet (49,991 in Census I; 17,938 in Census II) is defined as "fishing vessels and radio-relay points on the waters around Antarctica (the Drake Passage excluded), crews on rotating tours" (GDD `Official_Population_Census.md`, `DR-68`).
- **A link worth testing:** heavy-water reactors are the only commercial tritium source (about 130 to 260 g per reactor-GWe-year, SOURCED in F2). A heavy-water fleet fed by a Tepenian deuterium supply might help start a first fusion plant.

## 3. Research questions

Physics, engineering and economics only; no story or canon assumed. Mark each finding DEMONSTRATED, ENGINEERING-STUDIED or SPECULATIVE, as the tranche-9 rules do.

**Q1. Which deuterium-from-seawater method suits a floating plant?**
- Girdler-sulfide, ammonia-hydrogen exchange, electrolysis-based (CECE) routes, distillation, laser or other.
- For each: energy per kilogram of deuterium, feed volume, recovery, hazards (hydrogen sulfide, ammonia), footprint and mass, crew, maturity.
- The earlier F2 file found no sourced Girdler-sulfide energy per kilogram.

**Q2. Where does the plant sit?** On a relay platform; on a separate ship of the same fleet; on the seabed; or ashore with a sea intake. Compare mass, power, motion, sea-ice and iceberg risk, storm loads, resupply and crew for a Southern Ocean site. Include what is known about floating or offshore process plants (offshore oil and gas, floating LNG, ocean thermal energy conversion platforms, seawater uranium and lithium recovery trials) as precedent.

**Q3. Where does the heat come from?** Platform power, a reactor, solar, hydrothermal vents or geothermal heat reaching a coast. Which Southern Ocean vent fields and geothermal sites exist.

**Q4. What do the seabed and the crust beneath it hold?** Check my unresearched claims in note §5:
- D/H in pore water, clays, serpentinites, gas hydrates and vent fluids against seawater (claimed lower, not higher, in the solid Earth);
- lithium in seafloor brines, deposits and nodules, and in seawater (recovery methods and rates);
- uranium in seawater (adsorbent recovery studies) and in seafloor phosphates;
- helium-3 in ridge vent fluids (a speculative lead);
- which of these exist in the waters the fleet works, and at what concentration.

**Q5. Heavy-water reactors in the setting.**
- What a heavy-water reactor needs: heavy-water inventory, natural-uranium fuel, the tritium it makes and recovers.
- How many reactors and what tritium yield would start a first fusion plant; ITER's lifetime need is about 12.3 kg, and a demonstration plant's start-up inventory is about 5 to 11 kg per 3 GW (F2, SOURCED).
- Whether the Tepenian nuclear files (GDD `Tepenia_Nuclear_*`) already assume them.

**Q6. Crew and the census.** How many people each option needs, against the fleet's fixed census number. Whether radio relay, fishing and extraction can share crews on rotating tours.

**Q7. Cold and the Southern Ocean.** Effect of near-freezing feed water and sea ice on each process; effect of the polar surface water's lower D/H (GENERAL: meltwater lowers it a little near the ice).

**Q8. The video's open claims** (note §4):
- escape and export velocities for Jupiter and Saturn, and the delivered cost of exporting bulk hydrogen;
- buoyant-city engineering (hot-hydrogen lift, hull materials, storm loads);
- the video's cost and revenue figures (uncited).

Q8 is separate from the maritime idea; run it only if the author wants the gas-giant side.
