# Regional frameworks: where each region's units come from

STATUS: index, 2026-11-02.

DCI uses the same frameworks as the nation maps. These define the **units** (regional cultures, or tiered ethnic identities) that Stage 0 finds inside each target.

**The general method:** `../../Nation_Drawing_Method/METHOD - How Nations Get Drawn.md`.
- Founding culture first, by the Doctrine of First Effective Settlement.
- Where there was no founding settlement, tiered ethnic identity: tier 1 the major grouping, tier 2 the identities within it.
- Present-day demographics corroborate; they don't decide.
- Zones (core, domain, sphere), not lines; seams are marked, not resolved.
- Indigenous peoples are cultures in their own right.

**Where the surveys are:** CurrentNovelDocs `worldbuilding/extractions/` (the survey files named below), with the regional map research under `y-files/Map Files/<region>/`.
