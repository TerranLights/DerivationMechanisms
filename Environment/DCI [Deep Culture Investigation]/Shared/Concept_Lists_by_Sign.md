# DCI concept lists by sign

STATUS: Tier 1 drafted 2026-11-02. Each sign's list is settled with the author just before that sign's pass (RUNBOOK, Stage 4).

**Source of the concepts:** the Robot Fundamental Concepts research in CurrentNovelDocs `worldbuilding/Robot_Fundamental_Concepts/`. The per-sign concept tables hold every concept with how that sign's material frames it; the merged index (`02 - Concepts/00 - Concept index (2026-10-30).md`) holds each sign's ten most central concepts and the near-synonym groups.

**The hierarchy** (author, 2026-11-02): the twelve signs are the top-level categories; the concepts are their subcategories. **Each concept has one home sign,** the one it most naturally associates with.
