# Fischer's folkways, crosswalked to the twelve signs

STATUS: PROPOSED 2026-11-02 (author adopted the folkways as a second checklist; the placements below are proposals, settled sign by sign).

**Source:** David Hackett Fischer, *Albion's Seed: Four British Folkways in America* (1989). Fischer describes each of four founding British cultures through 24 folkways. His treatment of freedom (four regional meanings of one concept) is the closest existing precedent for DCI.

At Stage 4, each sign's pass covers its folkways after its concepts. The runner-up sign keeps a cross-reference.
