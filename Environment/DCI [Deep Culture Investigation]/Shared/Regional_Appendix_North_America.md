# Regional appendix: North America (the pilot region)

STATUS: draft v0 (2026-11-10). Access not yet checked for most items; marked *(check)* where even the existence or coverage is uncertain. Moved out of `Shared/Investigation_Sheet.md` so that the sheet stays the same for every culture; each world region gets an appendix like this when its first target begins.

## Languages and voices (sheet A5)
- **Settler languages:** regional varieties of English, Spanish (Mexican, northern and Baja varieties) and French.
- **Indigenous languages:** each nation's own; the nations' published dictionaries and language programs come first (FirstVoices; the *Lushootseed Dictionary*; the Confederated Tribes of Grand Ronde's Chinuk Wawa dictionary *check*).
- **Trade and contact languages:** Chinuk Wawa (Chinook Jargon), and its loans into Pacific Northwest English (*skookum*, "strong, brave"; *tyee*, "chief, boss"; *potlatch*).
- **Immigrant languages:** Chinese, Japanese, Korean, Tagalog, Vietnamese, Persian, Russian (the Doukhobors) and others. **No Subcontinentals:** British Columbia's South Asian communities and their history (including the *Komagata Maru*, 1914) get one exclusion line, flagged, and nothing further.

## Dictionaries, corpora, proverbs
- **Regional dictionaries:** *Dictionary of American Regional English*; *Dictionary of Canadianisms on Historical Principles* (DCHP-2); *Diccionario del español de México* (El Colegio de México); Academia Mexicana's *Diccionario de mexicanismos*.
- **Corpora with a regional field:** the Strathy Corpus of Canadian English; GloWbE (by country); the *Corpus del Español* and CORPES XXI (by country). COCA has no region field. A county-level Twitter corpus (Grieve) *check access*.
- **Proverbs:** Mieder et al., *A Dictionary of American Proverbs*; a Mexican *refranero* (Pérez Martínez *check*).

## Archives and collections
- **Folklore:** the Library of Congress American Folklife Center; Utah State University's Fife Folklore Archives; the Berkeley Folklore Archives.
- **Life histories and self-portraits:** the Federal Writers' Project life histories and the American Guide Series (each state's 1930s self-portrait); pioneer diaries (Schlissel, *Women's Diaries of the Westward Journey*).
- **Monuments and markers:** the National Monument Audit (2021); the Historical Marker Database (HMdb); Parks Canada's Directory of Federal Heritage Designations.
- **Regional encyclopedias** (for leads): the Oregon Encyclopedia; HistoryLink.org (Washington); The Canadian Encyclopedia; INAFED's *Enciclopedia de los Municipios y Delegaciones de México*.
- **Voters' own arguments:** Oregon's Voters' Pamphlet (arguments paid for by citizens); California's Official Voter Information Guide; state party platforms.
- **Official self-portraits:** governors' State of the State addresses; provincial Speeches from the Throne; Mexican governors' *informes*; citizenship study guides and tests (the U.S. civics test; *Discover Canada*; Mexico's naturalization exam).

## The nations' own sources
Tribal and First Nations constitutions and codes (the National Indian Law Library; the Tribal Court Clearinghouse); Indigenous newspapers (*Ha-Shilth-Sa*); cultural centers (the U'mista Cultural Centre); oral histories entered as court evidence (*Delgamuukw*, 1997); INPI's *Atlas de los Pueblos Indígenas de México*. First Nations data follow OCAP; the First Nations Regional Health Survey is used only as its governing body allows.

## Counted exemplars
The Carnegie Hero Fund awards (U.S. and Canada, with places); Canada's Decorations for Bravery; Medal of Honor recipients by state; the NEA National Heritage Fellowships; CBC's *The Greatest Canadian* (2004).

## Baselines and classic accounts
- **National self-portraits, read as baselines, not as the unit's reading:** Tocqueville; Bellah et al., *Habits of the Heart*; Paz, *El laberinto de la soledad*; Ramos; Portilla on *relajo*; Bartra, *La jaula de la melancolía* (the critique); Frye's "garrison mentality"; Atwood, *Survival*; Lipset, *Continental Divide*; Adams, *Fire and Ice*; Anzaldúa, *Borderlands/La Frontera*.
- **Outsider accounts, read critically:** Dana, *Two Years Before the Mast*; Calderón de la Barca, *Life in Mexico*; Boas; Curtis.
- **Regional:** Killen and Silk, eds., *Religion and Public Life in the Pacific Northwest: The None Zone* (2004).

## Datasets
**United States**
- American Community Survey: living alone, young adults with parents, multigenerational households, self-employment, veterans, intermarriage.
- Business and migration: IRS county-to-county migration flows; Nonemployer Statistics (county); the Kauffman Indicators of Entrepreneurship; Business Formation Statistics (county coverage *check*).
- Health and risk (CDC): BRFSS; PLACES (county and tract); YRBSS; WONDER mortality; NSDUH substate estimates; SchoolVaxView exemptions. Crashes: NHTSA's Fatality Analysis Reporting System (speeding, alcohol, unbelted, by county).
- Civic life: 2020 Census self-response rates (tract); Nationscape (2019-2021); the CPS Civic Engagement and Volunteering Supplement; IRS charitable deductions by ZIP code; the IRS Exempt Organizations Business Master File.
- Religion: the U.S. Religion Census 2020 (county); Pew's Religious Landscape Study (2023-2024).
- Social ties: Meta's Social Connectedness Index.
- Guns and hunting: RAND household gun-ownership estimates; FBI NICS checks; U.S. Fish and Wildlife hunting and fishing licenses.
- Military and sport: the Defense Department's *Population Representation in the Military Services*; the National Federation of State High School Associations' participation survey.
- Arts, recreation, libraries: BEA's outdoor-recreation and arts satellite accounts; the IMLS Public Libraries Survey; Seattle Public Library checkout data.
- Money: bankruptcy filings by district; the Urban Institute's "Debt in America" (county); SAHIE uninsured rates (county).
- Time and household: the American Time Use Survey; HUD Point-in-Time homeless counts; AFCARS foster care.
- Death, fear, friendship, protest: the Cremation Association of North America's rates; the Chapman Survey of American Fears; the American Perspectives Survey (friendship); the Crowd Counting Consortium; ACLED.
- Values: GSS (region); PRRI's American Values Atlas (state); the Cooperative Election Study (state, district).

**Canada**
- The Canadian Community Health Survey (health region).
- General Social Survey cycles: Giving, Volunteering and Participating; Time Use; Victimization; Social Identity.
- The Canadian Social Survey (loneliness); the Canadian Election Study.
- Indigenous data: the Aboriginal Peoples Survey; census Indigenous population profiles by community; the First Nations Regional Health Survey (under OCAP).
- The CRA's list of charities; Health Canada's annual reports on medical assistance in dying; PISA's provincial samples.

**Mexico** (INEGI unless noted)
- ENVIPE (victimization, fear of crime); ENOE (informality, self-employment); ENIGH (household spending); ENDIREH (violence in the home); ENUT (time use); ENADIS (discrimination); ENCUCI (civic culture, 2020; ENCUP 2001-2012 before it); ENBIARE (well-being); ENCODAT (drugs and alcohol); ENSANUT (health).
- Not INEGI: the Mexican Family Life Survey; UNAM's *Los mexicanos vistos por sí mismos* (2015); Alduncin's *Los valores de los mexicanos*; ESRU-EMOVI (social mobility); Banxico remittances by state; INPI's atlas (above).
