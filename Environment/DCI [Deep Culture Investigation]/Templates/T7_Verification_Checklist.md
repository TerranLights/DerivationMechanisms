# DCI Stage 7: verification checklist

Run on every output file before its STATUS says done. Record the result as `STATUS: done <date>; VERIFIED <date>` or list what failed.

## Completeness
- [ ] Every section of the template is filled, or carries an honest null with one line on what was looked for.
- [ ] Every concept entry has a Variation answer.
- [ ] Founders' understanding and later layers are kept apart.
- [ ] Every claim carries a tag: measured, official, scholarly, synthesis, classic, practitioner or claim.
- [ ] Source disagreements are recorded, not resolved.

## Sources
- [ ] Every claim has a citation, and every citation is listed under Sources.
- [ ] No South Asian works. Any study held back only because of an author's name is in a `held_for_review_*.md` file.
- [ ] Quotations are a sentence or less.
- [ ] Measured values give the level, year and sample; no country value is passed off as a unit's.
- [ ] Anything read only from a summary or abstract says so.
- [ ] Measures from think tanks with a declared orientation are tagged practitioner and checked against academic measures.

## Rules
- [ ] No national stereotypes: no claim treats a people as one voice.
- [ ] Nothing zodiac-derived is written as if it were in-world.
- [ ] Stage 6 material is marked PROPOSED and was drafted between 05:00 and 14:59.
- [ ] American English throughout.
- [ ] No copyrighted text, raw datasets or third-party images added to git.

## Spot check
- [ ] Three claims chosen at random have been checked against their sources.
