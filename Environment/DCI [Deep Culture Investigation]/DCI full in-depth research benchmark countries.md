# Full-Research Benchmarks

## Summary

The example to calibrate the full, in-depth research methodology is the Republic of Cascadia. However, it will also be necessary to use several different culture-nations as examples for calibrating the weekly model allotment resource-usage of the full, in-depth DCI.

These will need to be culture-nations whose overall general historical and cultural breadth and depth are wide enough and deep enough to where they are approximately average- to above-average complexity in order to measure how much of the weekly resources one [1] full, complete DCI run actually uses.

Once average resource-usage has been accurately assessed, it will then be possible to plan for the future in terms of scheduling full, in-depth DCI runs for all countries over the course of the following months.
