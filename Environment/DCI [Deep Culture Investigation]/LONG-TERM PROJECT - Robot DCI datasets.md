# Long-term project: Robot DCI datasets

**Status: noted, not started (author, 2026-11-21).** A long-term project to run alongside the current work. Expected length: **at least a year, most likely a year and a half to two years.** It is highly theoretical, and will need an extreme amount of calculation, data processing and thinking, from both the author and Claude. Slow and arduous by nature; there is no credit for speed.

## The aim
Work out the mechanisms and methodologies to:
1. **analyze** the results of the DCI for real-world human societies (each culture-nation: how it understands every concept), and
2. **translate** them into equivalent **robot counterparts**: how robots *from* those societies would understand the same concepts.

The product is a set of synthesized **Robot DCI datasets**, each comparably equivalent to its human counterpart.

## The two starting points
- **Human side:** Donald E. Brown's *Human Universals* (1991) is the floor of the *analysis*. **But the DCI research itself never refers to it** (author, 2026-11-21, binding): each culture-nation is researched on its own terms first; only once its research is complete are its parallels and connections to Brown's universals analyzed. This project starts from those completed datasets and their after-the-fact analysis against the universals, never from universals used to frame the research. (Brown's book and the Human Universals Culture Framework are in `Environment/Neo-Races_and_Cultures_Method/`.)
- **Robot side:** the **Robot Universals** synthesized for this universe are the floor (`Characters/Robot-specific/Robot_Universals/`: ground-level fundamentals, the differentiation method, robot cultural universals, applying the universals). From them, extrapolate out to what the theoretical DCI datasets for robot societies and robot culture-nations would be.

So the translation pairs, on the human side, each **completed human culture-nation dataset with its after-the-fact analysis against Brown's universals**, and builds, on the robot side, from the **robot universals out to a robot culture-nation dataset**, with the human dataset as the guide for what the robot one must cover and how it must differ.

## Depends on
- The DCI's real research for each culture-nation (the springboards come first; the full research after).
- The Robot Universals as they stand.

## Where the results would go
The finished Robot DCI datasets belong with the other culture results in the [Culture Library](https://github.com/TerranLights/CultureLibrary). Its layout for this is not yet decided (for example, a robot layer beside each culture-nation's human entry); settle it when the first dataset exists.
