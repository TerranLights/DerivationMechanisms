# DCI agent brief template

Copy this into the results root's `_dispatch/` as `<target>_<unit>_S<stage>[_<sign>].md`, fill the angle brackets, and save it **before** launching the agent. One job = one unit × one stage (or one unit × one sign at Stage 4).

---

**Job:** DCI Stage <N> (<stage name>) for the unit **<unit>** in the target **<target>**<, sign NN <Sign>>.

**Read first:**
1. `<DM>/Environment/DCI [Deep Culture Investigation]/RUNBOOK.md` (the stage's section and the standing rules).
2. The template: `<DM>/Environment/DCI [Deep Culture Investigation]/Templates/<template>`.
3. `<results root>/Targets/<target>/00 - Scope.md` (the unit's founders, layers and zone).
4. For Stage 4: `Shared/Concept_Lists_by_Sign.md` (the settled list for this sign), `Shared/Folkways_Crosswalk.md`, and this unit's Stage 1-3 files.
5. `Shared/Sources_and_Datasets.md` and `Shared/Regional_Frameworks.md`.

**Write to:** `<results root>/<output path>`. Touch no other file.

**Checkpoints:** the first line of the output (above the title) is `STATUS: in progress, section 0 of <M>`. Update it after every section. When finished: `STATUS: done <date>, not yet verified`.

**Search budget:** about <N> web searches. Fetch primary sources where possible. If a source is read only from its abstract or summary, say so. **The session's search cap (1,000 by default) is shared by every agent running at once;** if it runs out, carry on by fetching known primary pages directly, and say so in the report.

**Scratch space:** work in a subfolder named for this job in any shared scratch folder; other agents are using it too.

**Rules:**
- American English.
- Native, Indigenous or Aboriginal, never "Indian" (in Canada also First Nations, Inuit or Métis). Published titles and names, a nation's own name, quotations and census category names stay as published.
- No South Asian works as sources. A modern study that would be set aside only because of an author's name goes in `held_for_review_<name>.md` beside the output, not into the text.
- Short quotations only (a sentence or less), with citations. No copyrighted text, raw datasets or images.
- Tag every claim: measured, official, scholarly, synthesis, classic, practitioner or claim.
- Founders first, then later layers. Variation is required. Honest nulls, not forced matches. Record disagreements between sources; don't resolve them.
- No national stereotypes: a people is never one voice.
- The signs are research classifiers; nothing zodiac-derived is written as in-world.
- Research only. Don't propose divergence or in-world content (that is Stage 6, the author's).

**Report back:** what was found, what was thin, what was held for review, and questions for the author.
