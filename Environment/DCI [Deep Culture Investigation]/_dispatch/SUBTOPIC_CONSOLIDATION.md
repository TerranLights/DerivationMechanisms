# Candidate subtopics: consolidated across signs

**Why:** the sign-completion run of 2026-11-11 proposed 650 candidate subtopics. Some ideas were proposed in more than one sign. The author asked that each such idea get one home (2026-11-11, scheduled; confirmed 2026-11-15, "that is important to take care of").

**What was done:**
1. **True duplicates, 16 of them, get one home each.** The same idea had been proposed in two signs.
   - The home entry keeps its own text and gains the other entry's **full text, word for word**, under "Merged here".
   - The other entry keeps its number and title and becomes a one-line pointer to the home.
   - Nothing was cut or reworded.
2. **Related ideas, 84 clusters, stay where they are.** Each candidate in a cluster gains a "See also" line naming the others: 294 entries in all, and some carry more than one line. These are neighbors, opposite poles or narrower cases, not the same idea. Splits stay split, per the author's ruling of 2026-11-11.
3. **Checked:** every non-empty line of the twelve files before the change still exists afterward, verbatim or indented under "Merged here" (0 lines lost).

**How to undo:**
- The twelve files as they were before are in `consolidation_logs/before/`.
- The script, with the full lists of merges and clusters, is `consolidation_logs/consolidate.py`. Its MERGES and CLUSTERS tables are the record of every decision.
