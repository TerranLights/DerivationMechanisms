**The author's request** (2026-11-10): "go through each Zodiac sign, one 'meta-category' at a time, and go through each subtopic, and dedicate all your thinking efforts towards identifying every possible avenue of investigation that you'd be able to learn about details of that particular aspect of some particular given culture." DCI is a full, in-depth cultural investigation, not a documentation of laws: law is one angle among many.

**Write to:** `<DM>/Shared/Avenues/NN - <Sign>.md`. First line `STATUS: in progress, concept N of M` while writing; at the end `STATUS: draft v0 (2026-11-10), for the author to review.` Touch no other file. No git commands.

**Format** (as in the Aries file):
- Header: what the file is; the marks (*check*; Indigenous material only as the nations share it); the concept list, marked **not yet settled** (each sign's list is settled with the author just before its pass), with any near-synonym or duplicate questions named.
- **Section 0, the domain:** traditional areas; the domain question; avenues for the domain as a whole and for the traditional areas the concepts don't cover; each of the sign's folkways (runner-up folkways get one line pointing to their home sign).
- **One section per concept:** working definition; facets; neighbors and opposites, excess and deficiency; then avenues under these seventeen headings, every one filled for every concept: **Words; Beliefs and standing; Feeling and the body; Everyday practice; Life course; Ritual; Institutions; Rewards and sanctions; Expression; Exemplars; History; Measures; Variation; Relations; Who it applies to; Silences; Change**; then **Key questions** (the questions that most separate cultures on this concept).
- **Duplicates whose proposed home is another sign:** a one-line entry pointing there ("security: proposed home Taurus; see `02 - Taurus.md`").

**Depth and quality:**
- Every heading, every concept. Concrete and researchable from published sources and data: name the kind of source, a dataset, a word, a practice, an institution, an event.
- **Words:** real terms in the pilot's languages (English and Spanish, including Mexican Spanish); Indigenous-language terms only where the nations publish them, and never invented. Mark anything uncertain *(check)*.
- **Measures:** real datasets, at the finest level available: U.S. (Census and ACS, BEA, BLS and its American Time Use Survey, CDC surveys, FBI, Pew, Gallup, GSS, PRRI), Canada (Statistics Canada), Mexico (INEGI), and international (World Values Survey, Global Preferences Survey, GLOBE, Hofstede, ISSP, regional barometers). Mark any you are not sure of *(check)*.
- Examples come mostly from the pilot (Cascadia: the Left Coast, the British Columbia coast, the Far West, El Norte, Baja California Sur, and the Northwest Coast, Plateau, California, Yuman, Great Basin and Subarctic Dene peoples), but every avenue must work for any culture in the world; add examples from elsewhere where they show an avenue better.
- Group differences are framed as questions to research, never as findings.

**Rules:**
- American English. Plain, short sentences.
- **Native, Indigenous or Aboriginal, never "Indian"** in your own words (in Canada also First Nations, Inuit, Métis). Titles of laws, cases, agencies and books, a nation's own name, quotations and census category names stay as published.
- **No South Asian scholars, works or datasets** as sources. **No Subcontinentals:** nothing about South Asian communities.
- Indigenous ceremonies, stories and practices: only as the nations themselves share them; owned and restricted knowledge is not retold.
- No national stereotypes: a people is never one voice.
- **Research only, up to the present day.** Nothing in-world, nothing after 2026, no projections. Where an avenue concerns machines, robots or AI, it is present-day human attitudes only.
- The signs are research classifiers. No astrology content as evidence or avenue.
- Short quotations only (a sentence or less). No copyrighted text.

**Report back:** concepts covered; questions for the author (near-synonyms, duplicates, concepts that seem misplaced in this sign, concepts the domain needs that the list lacks); how many items are marked *(check)*; anything the format didn't fit.
