### Bat semi-domestication
- **Category:** culture and society
- **Definition:** Over hundreds of years, bats become half-tame "wild pets" that live just outside people's homes, without ever being truly domesticated. ### Battery
- **Category:** robot biology
- **Also called:** heart (a Doll's battery)
- **Definition:** A robot's energy-source architecture, its equivalent of a heart, which supplies its Engine's power; a Doll's battery is called her "heart". Capacity and output vary by build, and a battery can become irreplaceable and burn out. Irreversible damage to it, as to the gel brain, counts as death for a robot, so battery failure is typically terminal.
- **Status:** project canon
- **Used in:** InnerTepeniaGDD; SouthernLights
- **Sources:** InnerTepeniaGDD `Worldspace/Robot_Biology_and_Culture/Robot_Physiology_and_Cultural_Practices.md` (What Counts as Death) and `Worldspace/Characters/Dolls/Doll_Visual_Design_Canon.md`; SouthernLights `Worldspace/Robot-Biology-and-Culture.md`

### Belgrano
- **Category:** place
- **Definition:** A Halley-subnet city founded by Argentina on the Belgrano II station site, on flat coastal ice in Coats Land deep on the Weddell Sea coast; it is the subnet's southernmost major city. Its identity is "the Airbase That Never Stood Down": an aviation and frontier city run like an airbase, the subnet's aviation hub with one of Tepenia's few confirmed runways, home of the Belgrano Institute of Medicine and the Halley end of the Weddell radio crossing. It built las Arrastradoras and sent the all-robot crew that found Byrd Station, survived the Long Night War intact, and declined into ruin afterward (the Boneyard Times); its ruins are in DLC 5.
- **Status:** Timeline canon (name, subnet, site, the Arrastradoras, the later ruin); project canon for the founders (`DR-19`, `DR-22`) and the detail
- **Used in:** InnerTepeniaGDD (DLC 5); TepenianUniverseTimeline; CurrentNovelDocs (reference copy; worldbuilding; doll-music extraction); SouthernLights; TorontoFellOutGDD (basis copy); DerivationMechanisms
- **Sources:** InnerTepeniaGDD `Worldspace/Locations-and-Levels/Outside-World/Tepenian-Federation/Locations/Cities/Specs/Halley subnet/Belgrano.md`, `Worldspace/Locations-and-Levels/Outside-World/Tepenian-Federation/Locations/Cities/Founding_Register.md` and `Worldspace/Locations-and-Levels/Outside-World/Tepenian-Federation/Locations/Cities/City_Master_Reference/Halley_Subnet_Reference.md`; TepenianUniverseTimeline `Reference/World_History_Reference.md` (city table), `Reference/Amundsen_Station_Archive_and_Trucking_Network.md` and `Timeline Eras/2 The Second Interwar Period/Timeline.md` (Shape D, END)
- **Open:**
  - **War status.** Its spec (corrected 2026-07-03) says it survived the war and was ruined afterward, matching the Timeline's "Belgrano's later ruin"; the Timeline's 2812 summary nonetheless lists it among destroyed cities, and GDD `Overview.md` and `Station_to_City_Map.md` say damaged, partially operational.
  - **The Byrd maps.** The spec says both that maps recovered from Belgrano's ruins helped the Byrd founding expedition and that the maps were a gift from the then-thriving airbase, generations before its decline; it leaves the map archive open.
  - **Language.** Radio Tepenia's "A Todo Motor" speaks for the whole city in Argentinian Spanish, while the GDD makes English and German primary and Spanish a heritage language.
  - GDD `Airports.md` was found listing it as highway-only as well as a confirmed airfield (see Belgrano Airfield).
  - SouthernLights says the Argentine Air Force founded it and flags its founding-nation material for the No National Stereotypes rule.

### Bond and Grief
- **Category:** game term
- **Definition:** In Inner Tepenia, the two axes (0 to 3 each) of the Fragmentation Matrix. Bond is how far a companion or district has accepted the player's current self; Grief is unresolved feeling about earlier selves it knew, kept as a hidden ledger with one entry per past self (relationship depth × the personality's Grief-Multiplier). Grief entries never shrink and Bond drops only through specific events; an honest confrontation raises Bond but does not lower Grief.
- **Status:** project canon (decisions 2026-10-21); the visible-tier rule is deferred
- **Used in:** InnerTepeniaGDD; TorontoFellOutGDD; DerivationMechanisms (copied methodology)
- **Sources:** InnerTepeniaGDD `Game-Mechanics/Core-Mechanics/Fragmentation_Matrix.md`; `Game-Mechanics/Core-Mechanics/Fragmentation_Decisions_2026-10-18.md`
- **Open:** companions who discover past selves get Secondhand Grief entries at a reduced rate with no ceiling; Calethina has an extra marker, the Direct Participation Count.

### Byrd Expedition
- **Category:** era or event
- **Definition:** The founding-era expedition that discovered the buried ruins of Byrd Station. Belgrano organized it, built its vehicles (las Arrastradoras) and sent an all-robot crew, while Palmer City and Rothera shipped supporting historical records across the Weddell Sea.
- **Status:** Timeline canon for the event; project canon for the name, which appears only in the GDD (2026-07-03)
- **Used in:** InnerTepeniaGDD; TepenianUniverseTimeline; CurrentNovelDocs (reference copy)
- **Sources:** InnerTepeniaGDD `Storyline/DLC_Overview.md` (DLC 2, DLC 5) and `Neo-Races-and-Cultures/Halley_Subnet/Belgrano/Belgrano_Catalog.md` (Arts and Crafts); TepenianUniverseTimeline `Reference/Amundsen_Station_Archive_and_Trucking_Network.md`
- **Open:**
  - **Which vehicle made the crossing.** The Byrd spec and the Halley subnet reference say Neumayer re-engineered Belgrano's rough first-generation hauler and the rebuilt design completed the crossing; Belgrano's culture file and the Timeline quote credit the originals, driven by an all-robot crew. Belgrano's Expedition Day commemorates the crossing.
  - It shares its name with the real 20th-century Byrd expeditions.

### Byrd subnet
- **Category:** place
- **Also called:** Pacific (informal nickname)
- **Definition:** The Arcanet subnet of Marie Byrd Land and the Ross Ice Shelf side of West Antarctica, whose only city is Byrd; it is the region of DLC 2, and Byrd's own megasheet stands in for a subnet ultra-megasheet. Its broad-scale culture label is Anglo-American, though Byrd was founded by first-wave Tepenians, not by any Upper Earth country. Before Amundsen Tower it had no subnet-mate, so Background-Lore treats pre-Tower Byrd as having no digital contact with anyone.
- **Status:** Timeline canon
- **Used in:** InnerTepeniaGDD; TepenianUniverseTimeline; SouthernLights; CurrentNovelDocs (reference copy); TorontoFellOutGDD (basis copy); DerivationMechanisms
- **Sources:** TepenianUniverseTimeline `Worldspace/Locations/README.md`; InnerTepeniaGDD `Worldspace/Locations-and-Levels/Outside-World/Tepenian-Federation/Locations/Cities/Overview.md` (Reference Notes), `Worldspace/Locations-and-Levels/Outside-World/Tepenian-Federation/Locations/Per-Subnet_Overview_Reference_Data.md` and `Worldspace/Locations-and-Levels/Outside-World/Tepenian-Federation/Locations/Per-Subnet_Metacategories.md`
