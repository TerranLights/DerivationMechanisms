# Glossary: A

### Abramentes
- **Category:** robot-fabrication megacorporation
- **Definition:** Industrias Abramentes, one of the post-war megacorporations that design, fabricate, produce, program and distribute sentient robots during the First Interwar Period. It is based primarily in the Republic of Sonora (La República de Sonora).
- **Status:** Timeline canon for the name, base and role. The Timeline's megacorp records are only a few lines long; size, products and reach are undeveloped.
- **Used in:** TepenianUniverseTimeline; InnerTepeniaGDD (Imelda Sánchez was built in Mexico by a surviving enclave of the company; the location methodology's corporation roster); CurrentNovelDocs worldbuilding.
- **Sources:** TepenianUniverseTimeline `Megacorps/README.md` and `Megacorps/Abramentes/README.md`; InnerTepeniaGDD `Worldspace/Characters/Dolls/Still-Present_-_In-Game/recruitable/Imelda Sánchez/README.md`.
- **Open:**
  - ⚠️ **Naming variants.** The Timeline uses "Industrias Abramentes." Imelda Sánchez's files use "Abramentes Industries" and "Las Indústrias de Abramentes"; standard Spanish has no accent on *industrias*.
  - Whether the company exists in the Second Interwar Period is not stated.
