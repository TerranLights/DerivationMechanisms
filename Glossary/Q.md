# Glossary: Q

### QA gates (ULM)
- **Category:** method term
- **Definition:** Out of fiction. The seventeen checks run at ULM Step 7: numbered Gates 0 to 11 (completion claim, coverage, general population, internal contradiction, swap test, cross-location consistency, duplicate institutions, research accounting, standout, asymmetry, Review Panel, plausibility), plus lettered Gates C (canon, federated), F (frame integrity), I (inheritance classification), P (parent reconciliation) and G (generator honesty).
- **Status:** project canon (count settled at seventeen, 2026-09-07).
- **Used in:** InnerTepeniaGDD; DerivationMechanisms.
- **Sources:** InnerTepeniaGDD `Worldspace/Locations-and-Levels/Universal_Location_Methodology/04_QA_Gates_and_Differentiation.md`; `Universal_Location_Methodology/Stepwise_Execution/02_Gates/`; `Universal_Location_Methodology/Corpus_Reference_Sheets/QA_Gates_Full_Text_Quick_Reference.md`.
- **Open:** some files once said sixteen gates, because of a mislabeled heading fixed on 2026-09-07. Gate 6's against-siblings half is terminal, never run in-pass.

### Qin
- **Category:** polity
- **Definition:** One of the nine Han successor states of the Sinian Federation. It absorbed Qinghai (2131 to 2178) and kept its name; Qinghai is part of Qin, not of Tibet and not its own region.
- **Status:** Timeline canon.
- **Used in:** TepenianUniverseTimeline; InnerTepeniaGDD; CurrentNovelDocs.
- **Sources:** TepenianUniverseTimeline `Worldspace/Locations/Earth/Upper_Earth/Asia_East/README.md`.

### Quarantine perimeter
- **Category:** place
- **Definition:** In Toronto Fell Out, the guarded boundary sealing off Toronto, still enforced 14 years after the 2083 war. It is also the open world's map edge: there are no invisible walls, and a player who tries to leave meets checkpoints, patrols and gunfire.
- **Status:** project canon for the boundary mechanic; working for the enforcing quarantine authority and its motive. Toronto Fell Out is set in the Tepenian universe, in the Great Lakes DMZ shortly after the 2083 war (author, 2026-07-29 and 2026-10-30).
- **Used in:** TorontoFellOutGDD.
- **Sources:** TorontoFellOutGDD `Reference/Main-Storyline-Candidates.md` (opening notes, section 1); `TODO.md`.
- **Open:** The enforcer shortlist is Quebec and the People's Democratic Republic of New England, possibly the Midwest Republic.

### Quarry Return Toasts
- **Category:** culture and society
- **Definition:** Mirny's informal custom of recognizing a quarry crew that makes it back safely from the harsher southern routes.
- **Status:** working (megasheet proposal).
- **Used in:** InnerTepeniaGDD.
- **Sources:** InnerTepeniaGDD `Worldspace/Locations-and-Levels/Outside-World/Tepenian-Federation/Locations/Cities/City_Megasheets/Mirny_Subnet/Mirny/Mirny_Community_Infrastructure.md`.

### Quebec (post-war nation)
- **Category:** polity
- **Also called:** Quebec
- **Definition:** One of the twelve regions of the New North America map: a post-2083 nation of Quebec, the Atlantic provinces and eastern Ontario, with its capital at Quebec City. It appears on the Falkland Treaty era maps but not on the treaty's signatory list. Toronto Fell Out names it, with the People's Democratic Republic of New England, as one of the most obvious candidates to be enforcing the Toronto quarantine.
- **Status:** Timeline canon as a map region; project canon for the territory and capital (CurrentNovelDocs, 2026-09-26).
- **Used in:** TepenianUniverseTimeline (map mention); CurrentNovelDocs (worldbuilding); InnerTepeniaGDD (the Post-War Nations Founder Reference); TorontoFellOutGDD; SouthernLights.
- **Sources:** CurrentNovelDocs `worldbuilding/nations/New North America - Territory Definitions (read from map).md`; `worldbuilding/nations/World-Nations-Roster.md`; TepenianUniverseTimeline `Reference/Falkland_Treaty/Real_World_Influences.md`.
- **Open:** the Timeline leaves its treaty and signatory status open.

### Quest and Choice Perks
- **Category:** game term
- **Definition:** In Inner Tepenia, earned perks (Category C) for finishing a quest or for taking one branch of a major choice, the branches being mutually exclusive and equally valuable. The first six are Faith and Belief perks unlocked by questlines, such as Resonant Witness, Gilded Sight and Faithful Skeptic.
- **Status:** working (6 of a target of 50 to 70).
- **Used in:** InnerTepeniaGDD; Outer Tepenia 1 (fork).
- **Sources:** InnerTepeniaGDD `Game-Mechanics/Perks/Quest_and_Choice_Perks.md`; `Game-Mechanics/Perks/Perk_Framework.md`.
- **Open:** `Perks.md` still says this file does not exist (stale).

### Quest Pools
- **Category:** game term
- **Definition:** In Plot Hole, every request combines one passenger, one item and one complication, each drawn from a small pool of about 8 to 10 entries, so the absurdity comes from the combination.
- **Status:** working (draft pools).
- **Used in:** PlotHoleGDD.
- **Sources:** PlotHoleGDD `quick-rundown.md` (Quest Generation; Quest Pools).

### The Question of the Unbroken Name
- **Category:** group or people
- **Definition:** A post-war faction possibility built on the identity question each destroyed-city refugee community faces: when does someone from Zukelli, Belgrano, Palmer City or Casey stop being "from" that city and become simply Tepenian?
- **Status:** working (GDD faction possibility).
- **Used in:** InnerTepeniaGDD (factions; Palmer City vision notes; megasheets); SouthernLights; CurrentNovelDocs.
- **Sources:** InnerTepeniaGDD `Worldspace/Factions/City_Origin_Factions_PostWar_Refugee.md`; SouthernLights `Worldspace/Factions-and-Religions.md`.
- **Open:** Sinheung was removed from the list on 2026-07-05.

### Quick Capture Seed
- **Category:** method term
- **Definition:** Out of fiction. The character pipeline's short intake template (00c): a handful of seed fields (name, type, robot or human, personal vision, Enneagram type and Undercurrent if known, origin, location, timeframe, brief personality and backstory, weakness and goal) that the Derivation Protocol expands into the full character field set.
- **Status:** project canon (part of the pipeline).
- **Used in:** InnerTepeniaGDD; TepenianUniverseTimeline; CurrentNovelDocs; DerivationMechanisms.
- **Sources:** DerivationMechanisms `Characters/Character_Development_Methodology/Pipeline/00c_Quick_Capture_Seed_Template.txt`; `00a_Initial_Input.md` (The Minimal Seed Fields; The Derivation Protocol); InnerTepeniaGDD `Worldspace/Characters/Dolls/Methodology/00c_Quick_Capture_Seed_Template.txt`.
- **Open:** the GDD also has ready-made partial seeds (00d) and an inbox for batch input (00e), which CurrentNovelDocs relocated as Inner Tepenia roster data. The Timeline's `seed-template-init.txt` adds Original Doll fields.

### Quickhack
- **Category:** game term
- **Definition:** In Inner Tepenia, a planned category of short, cheap hacking effects delivered by jack-in against robots or humans and limited by cooldowns; the term comes from Cyberpunk 2077. It is where Baldur's Gate 3 cantrips are to be converted.
- **Status:** working (method only; none listed yet).
- **Used in:** InnerTepeniaGDD.
- **Sources:** InnerTepeniaGDD `Game-Mechanics/Perks/BG3_Conversion_Basis_of_Translation.md`; `Game-Mechanics/Perks/CP2077_Conversion_Basis_of_Translation.md`.

### The Quiet City
- **Category:** culture and society; place
- **Definition:** Zhongshan's civic personality, valuing autonomy, presence and simply being rather than analyzing or performing; outsiders also use it as a colloquial name for Zhongshan.
- **Status:** project canon for the civic personality; the nickname is working (a local-culture identity label with no explicit author ruling).
- **Used in:** InnerTepeniaGDD; CurrentNovelDocs (reference copy); DerivationMechanisms.
- **Sources:** InnerTepeniaGDD `Worldspace/Locations-and-Levels/Outside-World/Tepenian-Federation/Locations/Cities/City_Vision_Notes/Zhongshan.md`; `Cities/Local_Cultures/Mirny_Subnet/Zhongshan.md` §5; `Cities/Local_Cultures/Mirny_Subnet/Tri-Cities_Region.md`; `Neo-Races-and-Cultures/Mirny_Subnet/Zhongshan/Zhongshan_Catalog.md`.
