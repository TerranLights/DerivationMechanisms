### Official Population Census
- **Category:** method term
- **Definition:** Out of fiction. Inner Tepenia's master population file for every chartered Tepenian city, framed in-world as compiled by the Office of the Federal Registry. It holds each city's national-origin tier table (Section A), Census I (Section B), Census II (Section C, strictly orbital-era: after Von Braun Wheel colonization began and before the Long Night War), the Held Overflow Ledger, and a note on lost cities (Section D). There is no Census III and no present-day post-war population table.
- **Status:** project canon (Census II); working (a true post-war Census III is missing).
- **Used in:** InnerTepeniaGDD (cities; ULM; Division of Industry; Concordia districts); TepenianUniverseTimeline; CurrentNovelDocs (reference copy); TorontoFellOutGDD (`Original-Basis-Materials/`); SouthernLights (figures); DerivationMechanisms.

- **Open:**
  - The sections were renamed from I-IV to A-D on 2026-09-04, so that readers stop inferring a Census III. Census II's table carried post-war labels until 2026-07-12.
  - DR-23: no census edits, heritage tags included, until all 38 cities finish the ULM. It is a held file during the Mirny pass, so Section A still uses old names (Juan Carlos, Port Lockroy, Sejong).
  - Standing rule: the census is a record, and its editing history is not in-world events.

### Off-World Evacuees
- **Category:** group or people
- **Definition:** The Tepenians, robot and human, who left Antarctica for orbit through Amundsen Tower before it was destroyed. By the Inner Tepenia game present they are alive and prospering, with orbital installations around Earth, infrastructure at Phobos and Deimos, early Mars settlements or plans for them, a presence toward Venus and data infrastructure between Venus and Earth, outgrowing Tepenia; Concordia does not know this. Their descendants become the solar-system civilization of the Cryptograph Helix era.
- **Status:** Timeline canon.
- **Used in:** TepenianUniverseTimeline; InnerTepeniaGDD; CurrentNovelDocs (`reference/`; OOLSD); SouthernLights; TorontoFellOutGDD; TheCryptographHelixDD; DerivationMechanisms.
- **Sources:** TepenianUniverseTimeline `Reference/World_History_Reference.md` (The Off-World Evacuees); `to-be-integrated/universe-timeline.md`; TheCryptographHelixDD `world/orbital-origins.md`; CurrentNovelDocs `worldbuilding/Object_Oriented_Location_Synthesis/README.md`; `research/_canon_extract_interplanetary.md` (K-18 to K-22).
- **Open:**
  - **Numbers.** The novels' roughly 12 million late-Second-Interwar emigrants do not match Census II's orbital population of 10,104,964; the GDD's `TODO.md` still has an older figure of about 11.36 million.
  - **Who counts.** The pre-war orbital population counted in Census II is treated by the Timeline as migration, not loss; OOLSD's phrasing rule is that only a proportion of Tepenia left, most of them in peacetime.
  - **Mars.** The Timeline has Mars colonization still planned at the game present; a note derived from the Cryptograph Helix says it began in the Second Interwar Period (OOLSD AP-5 asks the author to pick).
  - The Cryptograph Helix frames them as witnesses of the war rather than survivors, and leaves open whether Tepenia is orbit's parent or sister civilization.
  - Named evacuees in Concordia are TBD.

### O'Neill Cylinder
- **Category:** technology
- **Definition:** A rotating cylindrical space habitat, based on Gerard O'Neill's real design. In the Tepenian universe it is stage 2 of the orbital build-out: huge, slow-moving, floor-space-efficient habitats that were the first orbital structures humans occupied alongside robots, and that remain the main long-term home for both in orbit for the rest of orbital history. Tepenians who escaped via Amundsen Tower live in them, and the Solarnet's data is held there.
- **Status:** project canon for the role and build order (GDD, 2026-07-02 and 2026-07-04). The GDD's mass calculations are plausibility bounds, not locked canon; the OOLSD class is not yet drafted.
- **Used in:** InnerTepeniaGDD (census; Theoretical-Calculations); TepenianUniverseTimeline (character files, including Zhuldyz Serikbolatkyzy and Besifa); CurrentNovelDocs (`reference/`; character files; OOLSD); SouthernLights; DerivationMechanisms.
- **Sources:** InnerTepeniaGDD `TODO.md` (Orbital infrastructure: logistics, mathematics, and dimensions); `Worldspace/Locations-and-Levels/Outside-World/Orbital-Infrastructure/README.md`; `Theoretical-Calculations/Orbital_Infrastructure_Mass_Budget.md`; `Theoretical-Calculations/Design_Efficiency_Comparison.md`; `Worldspace/Locations-and-Levels/Outside-World/Tepenian-Federation/Locations/Cities/Official_Population_Census.md` (Section C note); CurrentNovelDocs `worldbuilding/Object_Oriented_Location_Synthesis/02_Design_Brief.md` §4.
- **Open:**

  - Song prompts add rented cylinders in Earth and Mars orbit and a neutral micronation cylinder at Jupiter; Zhuldyz's cylinder orbits Earth in two songs and Mars in a third.

### OOLSD
- **Category:** method term
- **Also called:** Object-Oriented Location Synthesis and Derivation; Object_Oriented_Location_Synthesis (folder name); the OOLSD library; the off-world library
- **Definition:** Out of fiction. The author's off-world location class library, which hands the ULM, CST and RWBEM over to the repos that will build Tepenian locations in low Earth orbit, on Mars and Venus, in the Asteroid Belt, at Jupiter and Saturn, and later at New Centauri. Each class declares what physics, function and network position force on any place of its kind, and leaves named slots open for what is not yet known, chiefly the neo-cultures the 38 Antarctic cities will produce. - **Used in:** CurrentNovelDocs (worldbuilding; its home since 2026-10-23; Plan of Action); DerivationMechanisms (copy, 2026-10-29); InnerTepeniaGDD (a moved-away pointer; trackers); Hybrid-Anthro_Culture (references).

### OOLSD object model (class, instance, slot, late binding)
- **Category:** method term
- **Definition:** Out of fiction. OOLSD's borrowing of object-oriented terms, each mapped onto existing ULM machinery. A class is a kind of location, holding everything true of every instance; a subclass inherits from a parent class; an invariant is fixed by the class (the early-binding generators G2, G3 and G5); a slot is a named open field; an instance is one specific place run through a ULM pass; late binding fills slots once their data exists.
- **Status:** working.
- **Used in:** CurrentNovelDocs; DerivationMechanisms.
- **Sources:** CurrentNovelDocs `worldbuilding/Object_Oriented_Location_Synthesis/README.md` (The model; Slots, and when each binds).

### Operating-hours law
- **Category:** method term
- **Also called:** hours law; deep-synthesis hours law; worldbuilding hours; the 05:00 to 14:59 window
- **Definition:** Out of fiction. The author's standing rule that deep-synthesis work (derivation and worldbuilding: ULM, CST, RWBEM, district culture, character backstory, questlines) runs only from 05:00 to 14:59, with wrap-up from 13:59, after which no new step, phase or city is opened. Fact research, data collection, logistics and method findings may run at any hour; the test is whether the work derives a claim about a place, people or culture. It applies by type of work, not by repository.
- **Status:** project canon (author rule; GDD standing law since 2026-09-13), recorded across repos; its scope is stated differently in different places.
- **Used in:** InnerTepeniaGDD (ULM tracker; city passes; research logs; rulings log); CurrentNovelDocs (Plan of Action; OOLSD; Robot Fundamental Concepts; Europe intake; the Gilgamesh film; the doll-music extraction; Maritza); DerivationMechanisms; TepenianUniverseTimeline; Textbooks.
- **Sources:** InnerTepeniaGDD `Worldspace/Locations-and-Levels/Universal_Location_Methodology/MASTER_Process_Tracker.md` (Session boot; standing law, 2026-09-13); `Universal_Location_Methodology/DEVELOPER_RULINGS_LOG.md`; `Cities/Research_Logs/Davis_Research_Log.md` (M-244); CurrentNovelDocs `worldbuilding/PLAN_OF_ACTION/00_MASTER_PLAN_DRAFT.md` §2; `worldbuilding/Object_Oriented_Location_Synthesis/README.md`; `worldbuilding/Robot_Fundamental_Concepts/README.md`; `worldbuilding/nations/Europe - GDD hand-over intake (Santa Luce, the two Italys, the CIN; 2026-10-23).md` §6.
- **Open:**
  - **Scope.** The master plan (2026-10-23) limits it to ULM, CST and RWBEM; the Europe intake answer (2026-10-23) applies it to derivation and worldbuilding generally; the OOLSD README (2026-10-06) to deriving any specific off-world location; Robot Fundamental Concepts (2026-10-30) to robot-understanding derivation; the GDD rulings log (2026-10-03) splits data collection (any hour) from town character (window only).
  - **Exemptions.** Recording developer rulings and the Founding Register arithmetic are exempt (DR-33, DR-53 onward). Each research-log entry is stamped with the clock read at its start.
  - **Other repos differ.** slang-synthesis limits autonomous work to 15:00 to 04:59 (its grammar-logic work is exempt); TepenianHawaiian has no hours restriction until the ULM resumes (D-5).

### Orbital tier
- **Category:** place
- **Also called:** the ORBITAL TIER; Orbital infrastructure (the GDD folder's name)
- **Definition:** Tepenia's off-world settlement layer in low Earth orbit, opened by Amundsen Tower around its completion (about 2688) and built in three stages: robot-only staging stations, then O'Neill Cylinders as the main homes, then Von Braun Wheels for mobile and expansion crews. People who went up the Tower before the war count as migration to orbit, not loss. The GDD's Census II puts about 10.1 million of Tepenia's 32 million people in orbit.
- **Status:** Timeline canon (as a crowded milestone of about 2688). The structure types and figures are project canon in the GDD; no settlements are named yet, and the GDD's orbital folder is scaffolding.
- **Used in:** TepenianUniverseTimeline; InnerTepeniaGDD; CurrentNovelDocs; SouthernLights; DerivationMechanisms.
