# Glossary: K

### Kaliningrad (post-war country)
- **Category:** polity
- **Definition:** After the 2083 war Kaliningrad becomes its own country, majority Russian, with its capital at Kaliningrad. It joins the Confederacy of Intermarium Nations alongside Lithuania while staying independent within it.
- **Status:** Project canon (author, 2026-09-29 and 2026-09-30).
- **Used in:** CurrentNovelDocs (worldbuilding); InnerTepeniaGDD (Russia-split research logs).
- **Sources:** CurrentNovelDocs `worldbuilding/STATUS - Cross-Session Progress Tracker.md` §4.10.
- **Open:** The Russia notes deferred its status to the Europe work.

### Kamikaze
- **Category:** game term
- **Definition:** Inner Tepenia: a trait ported from Fallout: New Vegas, giving +10 AP at the cost of −15% armor effectiveness.
- **Status:** Working.
- **Used in:** InnerTepeniaGDD; TorontoFellOutGDD.
- **Sources:** InnerTepeniaGDD `Game-Mechanics/Character-Creation/Traits.md`.

### Karakalpakstan (Autonomous Zone)
- **Category:** polity
- **Definition:** In the setting, Uzbekistan's Karakalpakstan becomes an officially recognized Autonomous Zone within Uzbekistan, neither independent nor ordinary territory.
- **Status:** Project canon (2026-09-28).
- **Used in:** CurrentNovelDocs (worldbuilding).
- **Sources:** CurrentNovelDocs `worldbuilding/STATUS - Cross-Session Progress Tracker.md` §4.8.

### Karelia (post-war proto-country)
- **Category:** polity
- **Definition:** A semi-stable proto-country of Karelia and Murmansk that splits from Russia after the 2083 war. Later the Republic of Karelia joins Finland and Murmansk goes to Idelsk-Uralia. Its capital was never set.
- **Status:** Project canon for the name; the timing of its end is open.
- **Used in:** CurrentNovelDocs (worldbuilding); InnerTepeniaGDD (Russia-split research logs; the Post-War Nations Founder Reference, used to pick Tepenian city founders).
- **Sources:** CurrentNovelDocs `worldbuilding/nations/World-Nations-Roster.md` (Russia); InnerTepeniaGDD `Cities/Research_Logs/Russia_Split_Proportions_RS1_Inventory_and_Method_2026-10-29.md`.

### Khuzestan (Unstable Territory)
- **Category:** place
- **Definition:** Persia's Khuzestan province, historically the Arab emirate of Muhammara, marked Unstable Territory. Once post-war Persia turns secular, both reunion with Iraq's Shia south and an independent Arabistan become possible.

- **Definition:** The post-war successor state to Saudi Arabia, which also absorbs Kuwait and Bahrain; Qatar, Oman and the United Arab Emirates stay independent. The name revives the dynasty's own 1926 to 1932 name for its state.
- **Status:** Project canon (2026-09-29).
- **Used in:** CurrentNovelDocs (worldbuilding); InnerTepeniaGDD (the Post-War Nations Founder Reference, used to pick Tepenian city founders).
- **Sources:** CurrentNovelDocs `worldbuilding/STATUS - Cross-Session Progress Tracker.md` §2, §4.8.

### Künnarantaiga
- **Category:** polity
- **Also called:** Kunnarantaiga (ASCII spelling, the map's primary label line); Country A (former placeholder)
- **Definition:** The post-war Russian Far Eastern successor state on the author's maps, explicitly not Russia itself, with its capital at Vladivostok. It covers Sakha, Amur, Buryatia, Chita, Chukotka, Kamchatka, Khabarovsk, Magadan, Primorye, Sakhalin with the Kurils, the Jewish Autonomous Oblast and Irkutsk. With Mongolia it jointly founded the Tepenian city Ariun Nuur, whose stock also founded Kunlun (DR-35); its name is built from real vocabulary but is not historically attested.
- **Status:** Project canon for the name (2026-09-27) and as Ariun Nuur's co-founder (DR-35). It is not in the Timeline; the state is map-derived, and the maps are still malleable.
- **Used in:** CurrentNovelDocs (worldbuilding nations roster, status tracker); InnerTepeniaGDD (Ariun Nuur, Kunlun; Russia-split research logs); DerivationMechanisms.
- **Sources:** CurrentNovelDocs `worldbuilding/nations/World-Nations-Roster.md` (Russia); `worldbuilding/STATUS - Cross-Session Progress Tracker.md` §3; InnerTepeniaGDD `Cities/Founding_Register.md` (Ariun Nuur); `Cities/Post-War_Nations_Founder_Reference.md`.
- **Open:** A GDD research brief of 2026-10-29 (`Cities/Research_Logs/Russia_Split_Proportions_RS1_Inventory_and_Method_2026-10-29.md`) still calls the Far Eastern composite "Country A".

### Kunlun
- **Category:** place
- **Definition:** A Mirny-subnet city at Dome A (Dome Argus), about 4,093 m up at the highest, coldest point of the ice sheet: the highest city in Tepenia, and robot-only (123,449), with humans forbidden. Its founding stock is Ariun Nuur's (Künnarantaiga and Mongolia) plus the Sinian Federation, curated as a space, astronomy and communications-heritage population; it grew into Tepenia's astronomy and communications outpost, with a great observatory. It is Ice-Cold Buddhism's holiest site and half of the Mountain Pass venture, linked by Hwy 37, and it survived the Long Night War, barely.
- **Status:** Timeline canon for the name, the subnet and the Mountain Pass venture. Project canon for the founders (DR-34, DR-35).
- **Used in:** InnerTepeniaGDD (DLC 7); TepenianUniverseTimeline; SouthernLights; CurrentNovelDocs (reference copy); TorontoFellOutGDD (basis copy); DerivationMechanisms.
- **Sources:** InnerTepeniaGDD `Cities/Specs/Mirny subnet/Kunlun.md`; `Cities/Founding_Register.md`; TepenianUniverseTimeline `Reference/World_History_Reference.md`; `Reference/Amundsen_Station_Archive_and_Trucking_Network.md`; SouthernLights `Worldspace/Environments-Federation-Cities.md` (Kunlun).
- **Open:**
  - An earlier premise of an exclusively Sinian (Chinese) population was replaced on 2026-07-06; some older files still call it single-nation Chinese.
  - The Timeline proposes that its observatory tracked the Tower's falling debris.
  - The Timeline's `to-be-integrated/Calethina's history.txt` writes "Kuslun", apparently a misspelling.
  - SouthernLights and the Timeline name its Mountain Pass partner Vostok, the old name of Ariun Nuur.

### Kurdistan
- **Category:** polity
- **Also called:** The Kurdish Nation (earlier analytical label)
- **Definition:** A post-war Kurdish state whose border with Turkey follows the Taurus-Zagros mountain belt. It has a core and a domain reaching into Turkey's southeastern provinces, Iraq and Persia's Kurdish provinces; Ilam was later moved to Persia.
- **Status:** Project canon (renamed, and borders settled, 2026-09-28 to 2026-09-29).
- **Used in:** CurrentNovelDocs (worldbuilding).
- **Sources:** CurrentNovelDocs `worldbuilding/STATUS - Cross-Session Progress Tracker.md` §4.8.
