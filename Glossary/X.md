# Glossary: X

### Xcast
- **Category:** technology
- **Definition:** In *Streetside Harmonies*, an in-world broadcast medium: Rebecca's father, Mr. Whitley, watches one, and the binder glosses it as the news. Mentioned, not defined.
- **Status:** project canon (the hand-written binder).
- **Used in:** CurrentNovelDocs (*Streetside Harmonies*).
- **Sources:** CurrentNovelDocs `Streetside Harmonies/structure/main story/stanza scene and chapter breakdowns/stanza breakdowns (transcribed)/Chapter 03.md` (stanzas 6-7); `Streetside Harmonies/structure/plot-snag brainstorms (2026-10-20)/Snag 3 - HyperHedral's dark office (hints only).md`.
- **Open:** the older arc file has him listening to internet radio instead.

### Xenobiology First-Principles Method (XFP)
- **Category:** method term
- **Also called:** XFP
- **Definition:** (out of fiction) Hybrid-Anthro method M-5: it derives a physiology layer for races with no Earth-animal base (aliens, and possibly orcs or elves) from the conditions they evolved under.
- **Status:** working (proposed draft, v0.1).
- **Used in:** Hybrid-Anthro_Culture.
- **Sources:** Hybrid-Anthro_Culture `methods/M-5_Xenobiology_First_Principles.md`.

### XP Banking
- **Category:** game term
- **Definition:** (Inner Tepenia) XP earned past the current level cap is kept and applied as soon as a DLC raises the cap, up to the final cap of 100.
- **Status:** project canon (2026-07-22).
- **Used in:** InnerTepeniaGDD.
- **Sources:** InnerTepeniaGDD `Game-Mechanics/Core-Mechanics/Experience_and_Leveling_System.md`.
