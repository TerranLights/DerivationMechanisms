### War of Upper Earth
- **Category:** era or event
- **Definition:** The first anti-robot war, about 2563 to 2564: a global war of humans against robots and the humans who sided with them, fought across the non-Antarctic world. Upper Earth won, and the war ended with the Falkland Treaty and the exile of the robots and their human allies to Antarctica. It is separate from the later Long Night War and is not to be confused with the place name Upper Earth.
- **Status:** Timeline canon. Developer ruling, 2026-08-30: no single nation caused it.
- **Used in:** TepenianUniverseTimeline; InnerTepeniaGDD; CurrentNovelDocs (worldbuilding; *The Saga of Maggie Aarden*, which ends with it; the doll-music lyrics); SouthernLights; TheCryptographHelixDD; TorontoFellOutGDD; DerivationMechanisms.
- **Sources:** TepenianUniverseTimeline `Reference/World_History_Reference.md` (The War of Upper Earth); CurrentNovelDocs `worldbuilding/history/World-History-Excerpt.md`.
- **Open:**
  - The Sinian Federation is a confirmed participant and persecutor of robots; the other combatants are unnamed. An older wording saying the Sinian Federation "drove" the war was corrected 2026-08-30 (the CurrentNovelDocs reference copy of Zhongshan's culture sheet still has it).
  - Song lyrics use other wordings: "War For Upper Earth" (Garcelle's song, which merges it with the Long Night War), "the global anti-robot war" (Kendra) and "the great human-robot war" (Eira).

### Warm run
- **Category:** method term
- **Definition:** (out of fiction) The ULM's production mode: everything about the subject location is open in a mandated read order, its own earlier culture material is read last and only as a check, and conclusions about every other city stay closed until Step 6. It drops the cold run's quarantine apparatus but keeps the anti-convergence rule.
- **Status:** project canon (developer ruling 2026-09-04, ratified 2026-09-05 as the standing mode of the 38-city run).
- **Used in:** InnerTepeniaGDD; DerivationMechanisms.
- **Sources:** ULM `Run_Modes_Warm_and_Cold.md`.
- **Open:** there is no third, semi-cold mode; a cold run (own entry) that takes a spine-level leak is relabeled warm.

### Weapons and Tools Philosophy
- **Category:** method term
- **Also called:** Weapons & Tools Philosophy
- **Definition:** (out of fiction) The cross-media principle that almost every Tepenian weapon category began as a civilian survival, building, extraction or caretaking tool, because Antarctica rather than people was the threat, and was weaponized only by circumstance. Guns are the one exception, kept for institutional defense against Upper Earth. SouthernLights shows the tools in their original use before Inner Tepenia shows them weaponized.
- **Status:** working: both the GDD and SouthernLights state it as a standing design principle, but no explicit author ruling on it was found.
- **Used in:** InnerTepeniaGDD; SouthernLights; TorontoFellOutGDD (basis copy only); DerivationMechanisms.
- **Sources:** InnerTepeniaGDD `Worldspace/Weapons_and_Tools_Philosophy.md`; `Worldspace/Weapon_Item_Catalog.md`; SouthernLights `Storyline/Weapons-and-Tools-Philosophy.md`.
- **Open:**
  - The developer ruled on 2026-09-14 (GDD `Universal_Location_Methodology/WHY_CITIES_ARE_THE_ROOT.md`) that weapon and armor specs wait until all 38 cities are developed; the item catalog (2026-08-16) predates that ruling.
  - Tepenia's outward-facing defensive institution is unresolved in both repos.

### Whole-subdivision rule
- **Category:** method term
- **Definition:** (out of fiction) The absolute rule that national borders follow whole first-order subdivisions (states, provinces, departments) and never split one. It was adopted 2026-09-23 when Washington and Oregon were made wholly Cascadian, and it governs hard borders, not sphere overlays.
- **Status:** project canon (method).
- **Used in:** CurrentNovelDocs (worldbuilding).
- **Sources:** CurrentNovelDocs `worldbuilding/METHOD - How Nations Get Drawn.md` (Rule 1); `worldbuilding/STATUS - Cross-Session Progress Tracker.md` §0.
- **Open:** a few later cases needed district-level exceptions.

### World Trigger Law
- **Category:** method term
- **Also called:** World Trigger
- **Definition:** (out of fiction) A design law: every secret ending's access point is a physical, discoverable object or place, never a hidden stat check, a popup or an NPC who approaches first. Meeting the ending's real requirement is what makes the trigger reachable or recognizable.
