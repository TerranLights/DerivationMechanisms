### Vegas

- **Definition:** In *Streetside Harmonies*, the city across Cascadia's border where the hitchhike driver's brother runs his Tesla Coil Orchestra; the robot tariff keeps Valerie from going. The author ruled that she wants both Vegas and the Jazz cats' tour.
- **Status:** working: the in-world name is uncertain.
- **Used in:** CurrentNovelDocs (*Streetside Harmonies*).
- **Sources:** CurrentNovelDocs `Streetside Harmonies/.../Chapter 11.md` (stanzas 5-6); `Streetside Harmonies/structure/character arcs/Valerie - working copy for arc adjustment.md` (P1); `worldbuilding/nations/New North America - Territory Definitions (read from map).md`.
- **Open:**
  - The hand-written binder reads "[New] Vegas", the bracket marking an uncertain word.
  - Elsewhere in the repos "New Vegas" means the Fallout game, not an in-world city.
  - The map reading puts Nevada in the Colorado Republic.

### The Veilkeepers
- **Category:** group or people
- **Also called:** Veilkeeper (singular)
- **Definition:** A proposed Concordia faction concerned with suppressed pre-war information about the Falkland Treaty, especially why the evacuation happened, which it holds is too dangerous to make public. In the GDD it runs encrypted Arcanet black archives and acts as an information gatekeeper.
- **Status:** the Timeline names the group under the treaty's in-world presence. As a faction it is working: the GDD's tentative preliminary faction FD-3 (first suggested by Grok), awaiting design.
- **Used in:** TepenianUniverseTimeline; InnerTepeniaGDD; CurrentNovelDocs; SouthernLights; TorontoFellOutGDD.
- **Sources:** TepenianUniverseTimeline `Reference/World_History_Reference.md` (The Falkland Treaty, In-world presence); InnerTepeniaGDD `Worldspace/Factions/preliminary_faction_possibilities.md`; `Dev-Road-Map/04-Phase-2-Factions-and-Spirituality.md`; `Storyline/Endings/Secret-Endings/Faction_Devotion_Endings.md` (FD-3); SouthernLights `Worldspace/Factions-and-Religions.md`.
- **Open:**
  - The Timeline says only that they are concerned with the suppressed information; SouthernLights says they guard and suppress it.
  - The GDD sketch places their archives in the "Janbogo Undergrid", an older layout name for the Undergrid.

### Verb-vs-Noun Skill Audit
- **Category:** method term
- **Definition:** (out of fiction) A test for skill lists: a skill must be a noun, a domain someone can build a career on, not a verb or a one-off action. Verb-shaped candidates become perks or are cut.
- **Status:** project canon (design law).
- **Used in:** TorontoFellOutGDD; InnerTepeniaGDD.
- **Sources:** TorontoFellOutGDD `General-Overview-Notes/Design-Philosophy.md`.

### The Vigil
- **Category:** group or people
- **Also called:** The Echoes of Amundsen (earlier name; now mainly the DLC 1 working title); the Echoes (in `Faction_Devotion_Endings.md`)
- **Definition:** A tentative Concordia faction of longing for the off-world evacuees and the destroyed Amundsen Tower: it keeps memorials, archives evacuation stories and runs signal towers trying to contact the evacuees in orbit. Its name refers to Amundsen Station, and repurposing Tower scrap is an Infamy trigger for it.
- **Status:** working: tentative in both the Timeline and the GDD. The developer has not decided whether to keep, redesign or replace it, and its name is TBD. It was downgraded in June 2026 from a Devotion faction (FD-7) to a significant minor group (Blocking Decision 1).
- **Used in:** TepenianUniverseTimeline; InnerTepeniaGDD; CurrentNovelDocs; SouthernLights; TorontoFellOutGDD; DerivationMechanisms.
- **Sources:** TepenianUniverseTimeline `Reference/World_History_Reference.md` (The Vigil [NAME TBD]); InnerTepeniaGDD `Worldspace/Factions/preliminary_faction_possibilities.md`; `Dev-Road-Map/02-Blocking-Decisions.md` (Decision 1); `TODO.md` (Tentative factions); `Worldspace/City_Logistics.md`.
- **Open:**
  - Always written "The Vigil [NAME TBD]"; a find-and-replace left "The The Vigil" in several files.
  - `Faction_Devotion_Endings.md` still calls it "the Echoes" and still gives it the FD-7 ending, which Blocking Decision 1 had vacated.
  - The earlier name collides with the working title of DLC 1, Echoes of Amundsen (own entry).
  - The GDD sketch bases it at Janbogo's Central Hub memorial plaza.

### Vision Notes
- **Category:** method term
- **Also called:** District Vision Notes; Developer Vision Notes (file titles)
- **Definition:** (out of fiction) The developer's own direct creative input for an Inner Tepenia district or city, recorded when the developer is asked to picture being there. In the Megasheet process they are the highest-authority creative source (Step 0). The per-city set is City Vision Notes (own entry).
- **Status:** project canon (method).
- **Used in:** InnerTepeniaGDD; CurrentNovelDocs; SouthernLights; DerivationMechanisms; copies.
- **Sources:** InnerTepeniaGDD `Concordia-City/Districts/District_Vision_Notes/`; `Cities/City_Megasheets/City_Megasheet_Compilation_Guide.md` (Step 0).
- **Open:** for cities, ruling DR-10 places City Vision Notes in an annotation stage after the ULM, never as an input to a pass, while the Megasheet guide uses Vision Notes as its Step 0 input.

### Von Braun Wheel
- **Category:** technology
- **Definition:** A rotating, wheel-shaped orbital station based on the real von Braun wheel. In the GDD's orbital build order it is Stage 3, alongside continuing O'Neill Cylinders: light, easily moved stations that trade floor space for mobility and carry robots and humans together over long distances to build more orbital infrastructure. It is a mobile construction-and-transit type, not a permanent residence; Census II is dated by it.
- **Status:** project canon (GDD build order, 2026-07-02/04; census correction, 2026-07-04). The mass figures are plausibility estimates.
- **Used in:** InnerTepeniaGDD (the census; Theoretical-Calculations; orbital infrastructure); TepenianUniverseTimeline (character files); CurrentNovelDocs (`reference/`; the Garcelle film notes; worldbuilding characters; Object-Oriented Location Synthesis); SouthernLights; DerivationMechanisms.
- **Sources:** InnerTepeniaGDD `TODO.md` (orbital infrastructure entry; Census III); `Theoretical-Calculations/Von_Braun_Wheel_Mass_Budget.md`; `Worldspace/Locations-and-Levels/Outside-World/Orbital-Infrastructure/README.md`; `Cities/Official_Population_Census.md` (Section C note); `General-Overview-Notes/aspects_of_consideration.md`.
- **Open:**
  - **Home or not:** the census correction says wheels are not where people live permanently (O'Neill Cylinders are). `Upper_Earth_Immigration_Composition.md`, which marks itself out of date, still says the first wheel let Tepenians move to orbit permanently. Character material also treats wheels as homes: the Garcelle film's older note has her living aboard a small wheel, and Besifa's Saturn residence (Timeline) is "O'Neill Cylinder or Von Braun Wheel, undecided".
  - **Mass efficiency:** the GDD makes a wheel about 5.4 times less mass-efficient per resident than a cylinder; the Object-Oriented Location Synthesis sources give about 10 times.

### Vostok (Ariun Nuur district)
- **Category:** place
- **Definition:** The central scientific district of Ariun Nuur, the Mirny-subnet city formerly called Vostok. When the city was renamed (DR-42, 2026-10-03), this district kept the old name to honor the scientists of the past.
- **Status:** project canon.
- **Used in:** InnerTepeniaGDD; DerivationMechanisms (ULM).
- **Sources:** InnerTepeniaGDD rulings log DR-42; `Cities/Specs/Mirny subnet/Ariun_Nuur.md`; `Cities/City_Renames_Alias_Table_2026-10-03.md`; `Neo-Races-and-Cultures/Mirny_Subnet/Ariun_Nuur/Ariun_Nuur_Catalog.md` (rename notice).
- **Open:**
  - The district's boundaries are not set.
  - "Vostok" alone is ambiguous. Older GDD files and Megasheet folders, the Timeline (`Calethina/README.md`, `Amundsen_Station_Archive_and_Trucking_Network.md`) and SouthernLights still use it for the whole city (see Ariun Nuur), and it also names the real Vostok Station and Lake Vostok, which keep the name.
