- **Tepenia's currency** is absolutely unnamed and unsettled. - **Majyao's Concordia teahouse** is in Taurus, "without a doubt."
- **Robot religions** have no fixed count: the number is fluid and will change as the author researches and has ideas for more.
- **Hawaii and En.Ki.Du in 2083:** En.Ki.Du was built in roughly the half year before the war, while Hawaii was still part of the United States; Hawaii became independent following the war.
- **Hall of Archives and International Archive** are both true names (how they divide is still open; see below).
