### The Ice Road Season
- **Category:** culture and society
- **Definition:** The seasonal link between Temirötkel (formerly Sayowa) and its port on East Ongul Island: an ice road in winter and a sea crossing in summer, with no permanent bridge or causeway.
- **Status:** Working: from a non-canon vignette (GDD `TODO.md` ruling, 2026-09-30/10-01), repeated in the city's Mega_Init.
- **Used in:** InnerTepeniaGDD.
- **Sources:** InnerTepeniaGDD `Background-Lore/Cities/Mawson_Subnet/Sayowa/Sayowa_Course_of_Events_Suggestions.md` (#3); `Cities/City_Megasheets/Mawson_Subnet/Sayowa/Sayowa_Mega_Init.md`.
- **Open:** The vignette predates the 2026-09-02 ruling that puts the city on the mainland with East Ongul as its port.

### Ice-Cold Buddhism
- **Category:** culture and society
- **Also called:** Ice Cold Buddhism (spelling in the city specs); Cold Buddhism (shorthand)
- **Definition:** A robot religion built on the physics of extreme cold: as computing matter nears superconductivity, a robot reaches a state likened to meditative nirvana. Its two sacred sites are Kunlun, the holiest, and Dome Fuji, a major pilgrimage destination whose robot population is a community of devotees. Adherents are called Ice Cold Buddhists.
- **Status:** Project canon for the concept and the two sacred sites. Working: the name is a placeholder, and the doctrine has not been reviewed by the author.
- **Used in:** InnerTepeniaGDD (city specs, factions, `TODO.md`, the Dev-Road-Map, DLC 4 and DLC 7); SouthernLights; CurrentNovelDocs (reference copy; doll-music extraction); TorontoFellOutGDD; DerivationMechanisms.
- **Sources:** InnerTepeniaGDD `Worldspace/Factions/robot_religions_-_original_ideas.md`; `TODO.md` ("Robot religion design"); `Cities/Specs/Mirny subnet/Kunlun.md` and `Cities/Specs/Mawson subnet/Dome_Fuji.md` (Religious significance); `Worldspace/Factions/City_Origin_Factions_Second_Interwar.md` ("The Coldest Devotion"); SouthernLights `Worldspace/Factions-and-Religions.md`.
- **Open:**
  - **The two sites.** Their relationship is open. SouthernLights makes them rival holy sites: at Kunlun devotion is earned through purpose, at Dome Fuji arrival alone is enough. The GDD's `Neo-Races-and-Cultures/_Method/City_Types_Reference.md` calls each of them arguably the primary sacred site.
  - **Humans at the sites.** Polydimensional Animism's Rituals file says the religion forbids humans entirely at Kunlun and Dome Fuji; the robot physiology file says humans are kept out for their safety, not from hostility.
  - The `Worldspace/Factions/Robot_Religions/Ice-Cold_Buddhism/` folder is empty. Draft doctrine in `Reference/Real-World/Ice-Cold_Buddhism_Research/` (file 08) is marked not canon.
  - Background-Lore keeps the Kunlun–Dome Fuji link out of pre-Tower batches, because it postdates the Tower's completion.

### Idelsk-Uralia
- **Category:** polity
- **Definition:** One of the successor states of the old Russia on the author's maps, spanning the Volga, the Urals and the European north (Tatarstan, Bashkortostan, Sverdlovsk, Arkhangelsk and others; about UTC+2 to +5), with its capital at Nizhny Novgorod. It later gains Murmansk. It founded the Tepenian city Mirny, replacing Russia in that role (DR-46).
- **Status:** Project canon as Mirny's founder (DR-46). Working: the state is map-derived and the maps are still malleable.
- **Used in:** CurrentNovelDocs (worldbuilding nations roster, status tracker); InnerTepeniaGDD (Mirny; Founding Register; Russia-split research logs); DerivationMechanisms.
- **Sources:** CurrentNovelDocs `worldbuilding/nations/World-Nations-Roster.md` (Russia); InnerTepeniaGDD `Cities/Founding_Register.md`; `Cities/Post-War_Nations_Founder_Reference.md`; `Cities/Research_Logs/Russia_Split_Proportions_RS1_Inventory_and_Method_2026-10-29.md`.
- **Open:** ⚠️ **Name status.** The CurrentNovelDocs tracker records the name as set on 2026-09-27, but the map package still treats it as a placeholder (the same conflict as Donska-Kubania). The Russia map's own progress tracker, in `y-files/Map Files/Russia/` outside the repos, is the authority.

### The Identity Collapse Epidemic
- **Category:** era or event
- **Definition:** A wave of identity fracture among Concordia's robots from about 2812 to 2820, in units worn down by trauma, reconfiguration or forced reprogramming. The Scorpio district became the emergency center and improvised, with uneven success, the techniques that became modern rebirth practice. Units that came out stable but changed are called the Altered.
- **Status:** Timeline canon.
- **Used in:** TepenianUniverseTimeline; InnerTepeniaGDD; SouthernLights; CurrentNovelDocs.
- **Sources:** TepenianUniverseTimeline `Timeline Eras/2 The Second Interwar Period/Timeline.md`; InnerTepeniaGDD `Districts/District_Canon_Reference.md` (District 4).

### Identity Fragmentation
- **Category:** game term
- **Also called:** IF (abbreviation); IF meter
- **Definition:** Inner Tepenia: the visible, irreversible cost of re-speccing, which only ever grows. The re-spec design gives it five states: Whole, Fractured (1 to 2 re-specs), Fragmenting (3 to 4, when the Rebuilt marker appears), Unstable (5 to 6, with echo events) and Critical (7 or more, which unlocks a failsafe ending). Extreme methods can skip states.
- **Status:** Project canon.
- **Used in:** InnerTepeniaGDD; Outer Tepenia 1 (forked copy); TorontoFellOutGDD; CurrentNovelDocs (reference copy); DerivationMechanisms.
- **Sources:** InnerTepeniaGDD `Game-Mechanics/Core-Mechanics/Player_Re-Spec_-_Complete_Design.md`; `Storyline/Endings/Secret-Endings/Identity_Fragmentation_Endings.md`.
- **Open:**
  - ⚠️ **Two scales.** The endings file describes a meter whose bands (75 to 89, 90 to 99, and 100) unlock three endings: The Unmoored, The Ghost in the Machine and The Hollow. The re-spec design describes five states with a single failsafe ending at Critical. How the two fit together is not stated.
  - The IF meter belongs to the player; the Fragmentation Matrix tracks what others feel about it.

### Idolized
- **Category:** game term
- **Also called:** Idolization (reaching the tier)
- **Definition:** Inner Tepenia: the top reputation tier with a district or group (Fame 3, Infamy 0), named after Fallout: New Vegas. It gives a 20% merchant discount, opens the district's capstone questline and its highest-tier perks, and counts 50 accomplishment points for a companion who cares about that district. A district's first Infamy threshold sets how much misbehavior it tolerates before the tier is lost.
- **Status:** Project canon (the New Vegas tier name is kept for now).
- **Used in:** InnerTepeniaGDD; Outer Tepenia 1 (forked copy); TorontoFellOutGDD; CurrentNovelDocs (reference copy); DerivationMechanisms.
- **Sources:** InnerTepeniaGDD `Game-Mechanics/Core-Mechanics/Reputation_System.md`; `Game-Mechanics/Perks/post-Idolization_Questline_Perks.md`; `Game-Mechanics/Perks/Perk_Framework.md`.

### Illusion of Agency
- **Category:** method term
- **Definition:** Plot Hole (out of fiction): the game's design pillar. Every system that looks like a meaningful choice, such as character creation and dialogue, is cosmetic, and the game openly treats that as the joke; only tray carrying and earned body upgrades have a real mechanical effect.

### Independent Lattice
- **Category:** game term
- **Definition:** Inner Tepenia: a secret alternative endgame in which the player builds a decentralized power network of district or neighborhood micro-grids that makes the failing central grid under the Hub irrelevant. It is discovered only through scattered environmental clues across all 13 districts, with no quest markers or notifications. Its resolutions are Lattice Concordance, Frontier Bloom and Quiet Independence.
- **Status:** Working: guidelines only (2026-07-21). The name, suggested by Grok, is approved by the developer.
- **Used in:** InnerTepeniaGDD; Outer Tepenia 1 (forked perk files); TorontoFellOutGDD (copy); CurrentNovelDocs (reference copy).
- **Sources:** InnerTepeniaGDD `Worldspace/Locations-and-Levels/Concordia-City/Districts/Independent_Lattice_Guidelines.md`; `Storyline/Endings/Secret-Endings/Hidden_Paths_And_Secret_Endings.md`; `Storyline/Main-Story/Major_Stabilization_Paths.md`.
- **Open:**
  - ⚠️ **Gating.** The guidelines say it has no stat, skill or perk checks at all. `Hidden_Paths_And_Secret_Endings.md` and `Character_Creation_Overview.md` gate it on stats (broad investment, five MACHINE stats at 6 or 7; Calculation 7+, Engine 7+ and more), perks and skills.
  - Outer Tepenia 1's copied perk file (`Game-Mechanics/Perks/Special_Unique_Perks.md`) calls one version the "Lone Lattice" path; in the GDD, the Lone Lattice is a separate hidden path.
  - It is not the Bonded Lattice faction, and not the existing power network that the logistics file credits to that faction (see The Lattice (power network)).

### Inhabitation status (ULM)
- **Category:** method term
- **Definition:** (Out of fiction) The Universal Location Methodology's declared state of a location, separate from its type and band: Living, Growing, Declining, Dying, Dead or ruined, Never inhabited, Seasonal or rotational, Transit-only, Contested, or Resettled. Each status carries a question the pass must answer. A population drop caused by migration to a known destination stays Living, not Declining (added 2026-08-31).
- **Status:** Project canon.
- **Used in:** InnerTepeniaGDD; DerivationMechanisms.
- **Sources:** InnerTepeniaGDD `Universal_Location_Methodology/01_Frame_Typology_and_Inheritance.md` §3.

### Inheritance classes
- **Category:** method term
- **Definition:** (Out of fiction) The ULM's classification of every element of a location's culture by where it comes from. Determined: fixed by the parent. Inflected: the parent supplies the form and the place its own version; this is called the workhorse class. Originated: comes from nothing above. Aggregated: the parent's character as the sum or tension of its children.
- **Status:** Project canon.
- **Used in:** InnerTepeniaGDD; DerivationMechanisms.
- **Sources:** InnerTepeniaGDD `Universal_Location_Methodology/01_Frame_Typology_and_Inheritance.md` §5.1.
- **Open:** Flagged act-blind (finding M-157): the Determined class widens from Act 1 to Act 2, so every call must say which Act it is made for.

### Inner Tepenia

It is set in Concordia in the years after the Long Night War, about 2822 to 2827, and its main quest is stabilizing Concordia's failing power grid.

- **Used in:** InnerTepeniaGDD; TepenianUniverseTimeline; CurrentNovelDocs; TheCryptographHelixDD; SouthernLights; the Outer Tepenia series; DerivationMechanisms.

### Input Contract
- **Category:** method term
- **Definition:** (Out of fiction) ULM Step −1 and file `05`: what must be supplied before a pass and what the pass produces. Inputs are tiered: Tier 0 is blocking (designation, position, population, parent), 0b is the temporal frame, 1 is spine-critical (the eight generators), 2 is enriching and 3 is optional particulars. Every item is marked PROVIDED, RESERVED (authority lies elsewhere; a location's official in-fiction name is always reserved to the developer), PRODUCED (always marked Proposed) or REQUESTED (asked for rather than invented).
- **Status:** Project canon.
- **Used in:** InnerTepeniaGDD; DerivationMechanisms; Hybrid-Anthro_Culture.
- **Sources:** InnerTepeniaGDD `Universal_Location_Methodology/05_The_Input_Contract.md`; `Universal_Location_Methodology/ULM_Input_Required_Reference.md`; `Universal_Location_Methodology/Stepwise_Execution/01_Spine/S01_Step_MINUS-1_The_input_contract_before_the_frame.md`.

### Inspirational-Influences
- **Category:** method term
- **Also called:** Inspirational Influences; Inspirational-Influences picks
- **Definition:** (Out of fiction) The developer's hand-picked real-world cities (and, for Concordia's districts, real places) used as research inputs and inspiration for each Tepenian city or district, later researched and folded into its vision. Picks are judged only on structural and thematic parallels (industry, economy, arts scene, work and life culture), never on sharing a nation with the city's station or founders. They are the raw input of the RWBEM and step 6 of the nine-step city pipeline; ULM Phase 10's Process B researches them.
- **Status:** Project canon (developer rulings 2026-08-31 and 2026-10-01).
- **Used in:** InnerTepeniaGDD (city megasheets, research logs, ULM Phase 10, Pre-Trip sheets, Neo-Races catalogs); CurrentNovelDocs (reference copy); TorontoFellOutGDD; DerivationMechanisms.
- **Sources:** InnerTepeniaGDD `Cities/Inspirational-Influences.md`; `Districts/District-Inspirational-Influences.md`.
- **Open:**
  - **Tiers.** The city file marks picks PRIMARY or SECONDARY; the method summary also lists a Supporting tier.
  - Highways and corridors deliberately have no entries (CGRM-015, ruling 2026-08-31).

### International Court of Diplomacy
- **Category:** organization
- **Also called:** International Court of Diplomacy at Jeju-do (full form); ICD (abbreviation in CurrentNovelDocs); Jeju-do court; Court of Jeju-Do (developer quote in DR-44); Jeju-do International Court of Diplomacy; International Court of Jeju-Do (a doll-music song prompt)
- **Definition:** An Upper Earth court founded in 2267 on Jeju-do, which Unified Korea submitted as neutral ground between Korea, Japan and the Sinian Federation, for nations genuinely trying to settle disputes without violence. Its best-known ruling, of April 27, 2318 in the Gyeong-ja Yun case, gave robots legal personhood (see Jeju-do ruling). Before the exile it also allocated founding populations for Antarctic sites: the Bharati site (Shirayuki) to Japan and, in the GDD, Sinheung to Korea, confirming China's claim to Zhongshan (see Jeju-do allocation).
- **Status:** Timeline canon for the court, its founding, the 2318 ruling and Shirayuki's allocation to Japan. Project canon for the other Larsemann Hills allocations (Founding Register, DR-44). Its fate during and after the War of Upper Earth is TBD.
- **Used in:** TepenianUniverseTimeline; InnerTepeniaGDD (Shirayuki, Sinheung, Zhongshan, the Tri-Cities Region, the Founding Register, the Maggie Aarden files); CurrentNovelDocs (worldbuilding history, nations maps, Falkland Treaty copy, doll-music extraction); TheCryptographHelixDD (series timeline); SouthernLights; TorontoFellOutGDD; DerivationMechanisms.
- **Sources:** TepenianUniverseTimeline `Reference/World_History_Reference.md` ("The International Court of Diplomacy at Jeju-do"; "Unified Korea"); `Reference/No_Subcontinentals.md`; `Worldspace/Locations/Earth/Upper_Earth/Asia_East/README.md`; InnerTepeniaGDD `Cities/Founding_Register.md` (Shirayuki, Sinheung); `Cities/Specs/Mirny subnet/Shirayuki.md`; GDD rulings log DR-44; CurrentNovelDocs `worldbuilding/history/Jeju-do - Neutral Ground and Language Precedence.md`.
- **Open:**
  - ⚠️ **Timing of the Antarctic allocations.** The GDD says they were settled before the exile. The Timeline's draft Falkland Treaty (Art. II.3) says station assignments would be agreed after signing and need not follow a station's former nation. DR-44 adds geography as a basis on top of the allocation.
  - **A song prompt's version.** A doll-music prompt says "International Court of Jeju-Do" and puts Jeju under Korean dominion, against canon's neutral condominium; the same character's English lyric file says "International Court of Justice" (CurrentNovelDocs `worldbuilding/characters/doll-music extraction (2026-10-23)/`).

### International Data Syndicate
- **Category:** organization
- **Also called:** IDS (abbreviation)
- **Definition:** A loose network that locates, secures and retrieves datashards, old hard drives and other records of the pre-war internet to preserve human history. The work is dangerous because robots are illegal on Upper Earth after the Falkland Treaty. It formed about one human generation after 2564, around 2584 to 2594.

### Island Population Balancing
- **Category:** method term
- **Also called:** island cap
- **Definition:** (Out of fiction) The cap on each island city's population at a semi-comfortable level for its real land area, with the overflow sent to the nearest eligible mainland or ice-shelf coastal cities within ±3 solar zones. - **Status:** Project canon.
