### The Narrow Door
- **Category:** era or event
- **Also called:** Merit Board Audit (earlier name, still in the Timeline TODO and SouthernLights); meritocracy-integrity review (the cultural sheet's wording)
- **Definition:** The c. 2761 independent review of Capricorn's promotion system. It found that review windows calibrated to founding-crisis conditions, and a fixed, never-expanded number of master workstations, quietly favor the familiar, especially disadvantaging robots whose maintenance cycles do not fit a human shift calendar. Leadership disputed it, made minor changes and buried it; it is the Recalibration Underground's main exhibit, and the main quest plants it in Beat 1.
- **Status:** project canon in the GDD (date moved from 2861 to c. 2761 on 2026-07-04; mechanism confirmed 2026-07-29). The Timeline and SouthernLights still list it as tentative.
- **Used in:** InnerTepeniaGDD (Capricorn district; main quest); TepenianUniverseTimeline; SouthernLights; CurrentNovelDocs; TorontoFellOutGDD.
- **Sources:** InnerTepeniaGDD `Worldspace/Locations-and-Levels/Concordia-City/Districts/District_Canon_Reference.md` (District 6, Capricorn); `Districts/Deep_Dives/06b_Capricorn_Alternative_Conditions.md`; `Storyline/Main-Story/Main_Quest_Revised_Beat_Structure_TENTATIVE.md`; SouthernLights `Storyline/Timeline.md` (as the Merit Board Audit); TepenianUniverseTimeline `TODO.md` (as the Merit Board Audit).
- **Open:**
  - The TepenianUniverseTimeline `TODO.md` still calls it "the Merit Board Audit (c. 2761)" and flags it as shaky, "explicitly deferred until all cities are developed"; the GDD confirmed the mechanism later (2026-07-29). It is not in the Timeline's dated index, and the Timeline only mentions it.
  - SouthernLights' `Storyline/Timeline.md` (older) describes the Merit Board Audit as finding that Capricorn's merit system had undervalued robot workers against humans for decades, marks it tentative ("not sure yet whether to keep this one"), and places it at about 79.2%, the only named content in the Second Interwar Period's Dark Night of the Soul span.
  - Capricorn's district main questline is called "The Audit," a separate name.

### National currency (Tepenia)
- **Category:** unit or measure
- **Also called:** Federation Watt (SouthernLights' working title; retired placeholder, removed from the GDD on 2026-08-29)
- **Definition:** The single national money system in common use in Tepenia by the late Second Interwar Period. Tepenians took it into orbital infrastructure after Amundsen Tower was completed, and it fractured again when the Tower fell. The author holds the currency absolutely unnamed and unsettled, to be worked out later (2026-10-30): because the people of the various subnets came from different parts of the world, there were likely several currencies around the outer regions of Tepenia early on, which eventually settled into one unified currency. SouthernLights' energy-backed Federation Watt is "most definitely wrong" (author, 2026-10-30).
- **Status:** project canon for its shape (DR-3; author, 2026-10-30). Working: its name, unit and backing, which the author has marked for later work (2026-10-30); they wait until all 38 cities finish ULM, CST and RWBEM, and any answer must also work in orbit.
- **Used in:** InnerTepeniaGDD; CurrentNovelDocs (reference copy); SouthernLights; TorontoFellOutGDD (basis copy only).
- **Sources:** InnerTepeniaGDD `Worldspace/National_Economy_and_Currency.md`; `TODO.md` (national currency name and mechanics); SouthernLights `Worldspace/Core-Systems.md` (as the Federation Watt).
- **Open:**
  - A siligel or coolant commodity standard is ruled out.
  - DR-3 says the currency has no name at any stage and no earlier placeholder may be reintroduced.
  - SouthernLights `Worldspace/Core-Systems.md` still describes the Federation Watt, an energy-backed scrip redeemable for grid capacity (also in the copies at CurrentNovelDocs `reference/Worldspace/National_Economy_and_Currency.md` and TorontoFellOutGDD `Original-Basis-Materials/`), and GDD `Worldspace/Parallel_Economies_Survey.md` (§1 and §5) still calls energy or charge the confirmed currency; marked for correction.

### National Institutes (medicine, trauma and robotic care)
- **Category:** organization
- **Definition:** Three national training institutes that together are Tepenia's entire source of trained medical and care staff, because care must be staffed locally and no clinician can be brought in from outside: the Esperanza Institute of Medicine, the Belgrano Institute of Medicine and the Sinheung Institute of Cybernetics and Robotic Care. Training is residential, with rolling intake tied to the seasons, and graduates form a nationwide alumni network.
- **Status:** project canon (developer ruling, 2026-09-01).
- **Used in:** InnerTepeniaGDD (Esperanza, Belgrano, Sinheung, Division of Industry); CurrentNovelDocs (one mention of "Esperanza-trained"); DerivationMechanisms.
- **Sources:** InnerTepeniaGDD `Worldspace/Locations-and-Levels/Outside-World/Tepenian-Federation/Locations/Cities/National_Medical_and_Care_Institutes.md`.
- **Open:** satellite campuses are settled as feeder schools, but where they go is not decided; curricula, faculty and admissions are not set.

- **Definition:** Out of fiction. The Inner Tepenia list of 22 industries no Tepenian city can do without, each keyed to humans, residents or robots. - **Status:** project canon.
- **Used in:** InnerTepeniaGDD (Division of Industry; ULM); DerivationMechanisms (one mention).
- **Sources:** InnerTepeniaGDD `Worldspace/Locations-and-Levels/Outside-World/Tepenian-Federation/Locations/Cities/Division_of_Industry/00_Necessary_Industries_Register.md`.

### Neo-race
- **Category:** group or people
- **Definition:** A genuinely new, localized people formed when origin populations settle a specific place for generations: a real "third thing," distinct from every origin population, on the model of Taiwanese, Singaporean, Québécois and Afrikaner. In the Tepenian universe each one forms in one Tepenian city over the Second Interwar Period and is named for that city (for example Zhongshanese, Sinheungese, Rotheran, Marambian, Belgranian, Sanaian), never for its largest founding nation.
- **Status:** project canon (GDD method); most of the names are still to be synthesized.
- **Used in:** InnerTepeniaGDD; TepenianUniverseTimeline; CurrentNovelDocs; TheCryptographHelixDD; DerivationMechanisms.
- **Sources:** InnerTepeniaGDD `Neo-Races-and-Cultures/README.md`; TheCryptographHelixDD `TODO.md`; `factions/mafias/z-template/README.md`.
- **Open:** the harvest filed it both as an out-of-fiction method term (GDD) and as an in-world group (Cryptograph Helix).

### not-Doctor-Usanagi
- **Category:** game term
- **Definition:** In Inner Tepenia, a named implant doctor in each of seven districts (Aquarius, Scorpio, Cancer, Pisces, Aries, Central Hub, Virgo), named after Fallout: New Vegas's Doctor Usanagi. Each can permanently add +1 to a MACHINE stat; total implants are capped by Engine, each stat can be implanted only once, and each doctor also sells two to nine implants of their own. Players in an unfavorable reputation tier are refused service.
- **Status:** project canon (limits 2026-07-27; districts 2026-07-28); working for the doctors' own implants.
- **Used in:** InnerTepeniaGDD.
- **Sources:** InnerTepeniaGDD `Game-Mechanics/Character-Creation/Permanent_MACHINE_Stat_Increases.md`.

### Nuclear War of 2083
- **Category:** era or event
- **Also called:** the 2083 war; the global nuclear war; the Great War; Global Great War of 2083 (Gilgamesh film premise, adoption open)
- **Definition:** The global nuclear war that began on September 26, 2083 and broke the old world order: countries collapsed and borders were redrawn, producing the successor nations, and it opens the First Interwar Period. The first sentient robot, En.Ki.Du, was created at U.R.U.K. the same year, in roughly the half year before the war, while Hawaii was still part of the United States (author, 2026-10-30).
- **Status:** Timeline canon for the date and nature; causes and combatants beyond that are TBD.
- **Used in:** TepenianUniverseTimeline; InnerTepeniaGDD; CurrentNovelDocs (worldbuilding; all novels; the Gilgamesh film); TorontoFellOutGDD (set 14 years later; the Fell Out is Toronto's part in the war); TheCryptographHelixDD; SouthernLights; TepenianHawaiian; DerivationMechanisms; most Tepenian projects.
- **Sources:** TepenianUniverseTimeline `Reference/World_History_Reference.md` (The Nuclear War of 2083); `README.md`; `Timeline Eras/1 The First Interwar Period/Timeline.md` (Opening Image); TorontoFellOutGDD `Worldspace/World_History_Reference.md`.
- **Open:**
  - **Combatants.** The Timeline README says the USA and China spearheaded it after a misunderstanding in Uganda; `World_History_Reference.md` lists its causes and combatants as TBD.
  - **Names.** CurrentNovelDocs usually says "the 2083 war"; *Streetside Harmonies*, song prompts and the GDD's `Worldspace/Modern_Hawaiian_Language.md` say "the Great War"; the Gilgamesh premise says "the Global Great War of 2083" (adoption open, 2026-10-25).
  - **Date.** An old *Streetside Harmonies* email dates it September 16 instead of 26.

### Nyakkyo
- **Category:** place
- **Also called:** the Still Point (gloss)
- **Definition:** Ice Cold Buddhism's central sacred site at Dome Fuji and the pilgrims' goal: a small, unheated, deliberately plain structure at the dome's exact surveyed high point, with no altar or icon, where devotees sit alone in the coldest stillness the faith can arrange.
- **Status:** working (working name; megasheet proposal).
- **Used in:** InnerTepeniaGDD; SouthernLights; CurrentNovelDocs (reference copy); DerivationMechanisms.
- **Sources:** InnerTepeniaGDD `Worldspace/Locations-and-Levels/Outside-World/Tepenian-Federation/Locations/Cities/City_Megasheets/Mawson_Subnet/Dome_Fuji/README.md` (The Central Sacred Site); `Dome_Fuji_Full_Extrapolation.md`; `Dome_Fuji_Physical_Infrastructure_Attributes.md` (#10); SouthernLights `Worldspace/Environments-Federation-Cities.md` (Dome Fuji).
- **Open:** the Mawson-Byrd-Amundsen reference lists it as vignette content, not canon. The "Still Point" gloss comes from non-canon Background-Lore files (`Dome_Fuji_Course_of_Events_Suggestions.md`, #4; `Dome_Fuji_04_Naming_The_Still_Point.md`). Dome Fuji's other named spaces are the Robing Hall, the Archive Vault of ice cores, the Old Wing and the Waystones.
