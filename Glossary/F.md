### Fabrication-synthesis chamber
- **Category:** technology
- **Also called:** fabrication chamber; chamber; Cradle chamber (Background-Lore); robot-synthesis chamber
- **Definition:** The apparatus in which a new robot is physically built, which takes real time, and activated into sentience; its look follows the film *Ex Machina*. Chambers are made in a few cities (Byrd and Sinheung today, Mountain Pass and Denison before the war) by the Cradle network to the current-generation schematic drafted at Neumayer, then shipped nationwide, so almost every city has them (Dome Fuji is the confirmed exception). They are durable, so robots can still be built in the game's present; hardware generations are counted in Marks (see Gen/Mark).
- **Status:** project canon for the function; the name is a placeholder. The Timeline records the makers and the Neumayer schematic.
- **Used in:** InnerTepeniaGDD (widely; Byrd, Sinheung, Neumayer, Mirny, Dome Fuji); TepenianUniverseTimeline; CurrentNovelDocs; SouthernLights; DerivationMechanisms
- **Sources:** InnerTepeniaGDD `Worldspace/Robot_Biology_and_Culture/Robot_Physiology_and_Cultural_Practices.md` (Notes, "Robot creation mechanism"); `ULM/DEVELOPER_RULINGS_LOG.md` (DR-55, Q-66); `Cities/Specs/Byrd subnet/Byrd.md` (Economy & Industry); `Cities/Specs/Mirny subnet/Sinheung.md`; TepenianUniverseTimeline `Reference/Amundsen_Station_Archive_and_Trucking_Network.md`; `TODO.md`; DerivationMechanisms Robot Universals Ch. 14; SouthernLights `Worldspace/Robot-Biology-and-Culture.md` (The Cradle)
- **Open:**
  - A proper name is still open.
  - **Count per city.** The physiology file speaks of one chamber per city; DR-55 rules that every city except Dome Fuji has an array of them. The Neo-Races Byrd catalog says that after the war only two places can still make a robot from nothing, which reads against the rule that existing chambers in almost every city still work.
  - Makers: Zhongshan is a candidate; Belgrano and Concordia were proposed but paused; Fort McMurdo was removed 2026-07-07 (volcanic siting concern). The chamber in Calethina's lab in Concordia came from Mountain Pass.

### Faction bases
- **Category:** method term
- **Definition:** Out of fiction: in the Textbooks factions research, what a faction can be built around. The author's four base-level generatives are genetics (family, race), geography (origin, residence), hierarchy (class, rank) and ideas (politics, religion, ethics, uses of technology and nature); twelve candidate bases (interest, vocation, temperament, allegiance, shared experience, contract, common enemy, secret knowledge, style, kind of being, outside assignment, institution) and three patterns are research topics, not yet rules.
- **Status:** project canon for the four (author, 2026-10-25); working for the candidates and patterns.
- **Used in:** Textbooks
- **Sources:** Textbooks `factions/unorganized_ideas/Faction bases - the author's four and candidate additions (2026-10-25).md`; `factions/research/00 - Research topics - faction bases and patterns.md`

### Faction Devotion Endings
- **Category:** game term
- **Also called:** Faction-Devotion ending (Toronto Fell Out category name)
- **Definition:** Inner Tepenia: twelve secret endings (FD-1 to FD-12) for maximum standing with one of Concordia's cross-district factions and completing its questline, plus a contribution threshold and an in-world trigger object; they are about commitment to a cause rather than a place and are independent of district standing. - **Status:** working: 3 confirmed, 9 tentative because their factions were Grok suggestions; Toronto Fell Out's version is blocked on factions.

### Falkland Treaty
- **Category:** law or rule
- **Definition:** The treaty signed at Stanley, Falkland Islands, on June 21, 2564 between the United Assembly of Nations and Maggie Aarden for the robots, ending the War of Upper Earth on the victors' terms. It founded the Federation of Tepenia in Antarctica and exiled all robots there as a class, with human allies free to follow at the cost of their legal personhood on Upper Earth; it records the robots' defeat, a victor's settlement presented as a negotiation. Its anniversary is Tepenian Independence Day, and its date ends the First Interwar Period and opens the Second.
- **Status:** Timeline canon for the core facts; the full text is a first draft (2026-07-08: preamble plus seven Titles), not locked.
- **Used in:** TepenianUniverseTimeline; InnerTepeniaGDD (widely); CurrentNovelDocs (worldbuilding; The Saga of Maggie Aarden); TheCryptographHelixDD; SouthernLights; TorontoFellOutGDD; TepenianHawaiian; DerivationMechanisms

### Fallout Precedence Law
- **Category:** method term
- **Also called:** Fallout Precedence (perk version)
- **Definition:** Out of fiction: a binding design law that old-school Fallout (1, 2 and New Vegas) is the base reference for tone, quests, companions and RPG structure, and where they differ New Vegas wins unless a named exception is recorded. Inner Tepenia's exception is New Vegas mechanics that depend on real-time 3D, where Fallout 1/2's equivalent is the baseline (VATS becomes NODE); Toronto Fell Out's one named exception is Fallout 4's free third-person camera; Outer Tepenia adopts another game's feature only where Fallout has none, and the law does not govern space flight or zero-g.
- **Status:** project canon (binding design law)
- **Used in:** InnerTepeniaGDD; TorontoFellOutGDD; Outer Tepenia series; CurrentNovelDocs
- **Sources:** InnerTepeniaGDD `General-Overview-Notes/Fallout_Precedence_Law.md`; `General-Overview-Notes/Game_Design_Principles.md`; `Game-Mechanics/Universal_Rules.md`; TorontoFellOutGDD `Game-Mechanics/Universal_Rules.md`; `General-Overview-Notes/Design-Philosophy.md`; Outer Tepenia 1 `Game_Design_Principles.md`
- **Open:** Recorded deliberate departures in Inner Tepenia: deferred level-up spending and quest tracking by dialogue choice; features found only in Fallout 1/2 (such as the Jinxed trait) remain allowed. A direct consequence is no level-scaling. In Toronto Fell Out's perk version, overlapping perks default to the New Vegas or Fallout 4 implementation. Outer Tepenia's version restates its Absolute Laws 2 and 3.

### Federation of Tepenia
- **Category:** polity
- **Also called:** Tepenia (the usual short name); Tepenian Federation; the Federation; New Antarctica (alternate name; the Timeline README and Laws of Robotics)
- **Definition:** The sovereign, robot-majority nation in Antarctica, usually called Tepenia, founded by the Falkland Treaty on June 21, 2564 to receive the robots and human allies exiled from Upper Earth; every real Antarctic research station site became one of its cities. Its first century or so covered building the cities, the subnet highways, the Arcanet and Hwy 22 before Amundsen Tower. Fort McMurdo was its capital until the Long Night War, and present-day Tepenia has no capital.
- **Status:** Timeline canon; the structure of its government is undecided and left to a future Tepenian Constitution.
- **Used in:** every Tepenian-universe repo, among them TepenianUniverseTimeline; InnerTepeniaGDD; CurrentNovelDocs (worldbuilding; The Saga of Maggie Aarden); SouthernLights; TheCryptographHelixDD; TepenianHawaiian; DerivationMechanisms
- **Sources:** TepenianUniverseTimeline `Reference/Falkland_Treaty/Falkland_Treaty_Draft_v1.md` (Title I, the formal name); `Reference/World_History_Reference.md` (The Tepenian Federation); `README.md`; `Reference/Laws_of_Robotics.md` (New Antarctica); InnerTepeniaGDD `.../Locations/Cities/Overview.md`; `Cities/National_Capital_Candidates.md`; `DONE.md` (flag and seal); `Neo-Races-and-Cultures/_Method/Robot_Culture_Open_Questions_Tracker.md` (New Antarctica); CurrentNovelDocs `worldbuilding/history/World-History-Excerpt.md`
- **Open:**
  - The name Tepenia is also used for its people's identity, its era and the universe's flagship setting.
  - Its flag is the "wide circuit"; government variants use a diamond-and-circuit background and a tesseract seal (GDD `DONE.md`).
  - The GDD rules out any present-day "robot rights movement," since robots already hold full equality in Tepenia (2026-07-29).
  - Song lyrics use generated foreign forms (Тепения, 特佩尼亚, テペニア) found in no canon file, and some call Tepenia gone, while the GDD keeps the Federation name.
