# Glossary: J

### Jack-in
- **Category:** technology
- **Definition:** In Inner Tepenia, the Bridge Unit's physical interface: a cable or link drawn from the hand, connected to a terminal, access point, antenna or Arcanet node, then pulled back. It always plays as a short visible sequence. It is the in-world trigger for hacking and the delivery method of Signal Weapons.
- **Status:** Project canon (2026-07-12); the AP cost and range are open.
- **Used in:** InnerTepeniaGDD; TorontoFellOutGDD (copy); DerivationMechanisms.
- **Sources:** InnerTepeniaGDD `Game-Mechanics/Core-Mechanics/Hacking_and_Traceability_System.md`.

### Jack-in LAN parties
- **Category:** culture and society
- **Definition:** Social events in Concordia's Circuit (Gemini) district where robots connect through their Arcanet or Bridge Unit interface and humans through VR or AR headsets to play one shared game as equals. The district megasheet cites them as the reason human-robot life there is unusually easy.
- **Status:** Working: from the 2026-07-29 "Community Infrastructure & Social Life" brainstorm list; the Gemini megasheet builds on it.
- **Used in:** InnerTepeniaGDD.
- **Sources:** InnerTepeniaGDD `Districts/District_Canon_Reference.md` (District 9, "Community Infrastructure & Social Life"); `Districts/District_Megasheets/09_Gemini/Gemini_Full_Extrapolation.md`; `Storyline/Side-Content/District_Under_Questlines.md`.

### Janbogo
- **Category:** place
- **Definition:** A Tepenian coastal city on the site of Jang Bogo Station at Terra Nova Bay on the Ross Sea. It was founded by (Unified) Korea, and its name adapts "Jang Bogo". It is the hub of the Janbogo subnet, and its polynya gives year-round sea access. Tea, hospitality and togetherness are part of its culture. Damaged in the Long Night War, it was deliberately left standing as witness to the destruction of Zukelli, 8 km away; it sent refugees to Concordia and is the last coastal city still supplying it, by Hwy 183, with delays.
- **Status:** Timeline canon. Founders and city detail are project canon (Founding Register, DR-22).
- **Used in:** TepenianUniverseTimeline; InnerTepeniaGDD (widely; DLC 6); CurrentNovelDocs; SouthernLights; TorontoFellOutGDD; the Outer Tepenia series; DerivationMechanisms.
- **Sources:** TepenianUniverseTimeline `Reference/World_History_Reference.md` (Janbogo); `Timeline Eras/2 The Second Interwar Period/Timeline.md`; InnerTepeniaGDD `Cities/Specs/Janbogo subnet/Janbogo.md`; `Cities/Founding_Register.md` (DR-22).
- **Open:**
  - ⚠️ **Playability.** The Timeline says the player never visits Janbogo; the GDD's DLC 6 plan makes the Janbogo region playable, with the city partly operational.
  - Majyao's song prompt (CurrentNovelDocs doll-music extraction) has her flee to Concordia after the war; the GDD's Janbogo spec says before or during it.

### Janbogo subnet
- **Category:** place
- **Also called:** Janbogo/Ross subnet; Ross subnet (some Timeline research notes)
- **Definition:** The Arcanet subnet of the Ross Sea and Dumont d'Urville Sea coasts, named for its hub city Janbogo. Its cities are Janbogo, Zukelli, Cape Adare, Fort McMurdo, Scott, Denison and Dumont d'Urville, and Concordia (author, 2026-10-30), whose Gemini district (the Circuit) physically holds the subnet's data nexus. Its coast is the region of Inner Tepenia's DLC 6; it has no nickname by design, and its broad-scale culture label is East Sinitic.
- **Status:** Timeline canon; Concordia's membership is the author's ruling (2026-10-30).
- **Used in:** TepenianUniverseTimeline; InnerTepeniaGDD (widely); SouthernLights; CurrentNovelDocs (reference copy); TorontoFellOutGDD (basis copy); DerivationMechanisms.
- **Sources:** TepenianUniverseTimeline `Worldspace/Locations/README.md`; `Reference/World_History_Reference.md` (Janbogo); InnerTepeniaGDD `Cities/Overview.md`; `Cities/Station_to_City_Map.md`; `Locations/Per-Subnet_Overview_Reference_Data.md`.
- **Open:**
  - The Timeline's `Worldspace/Locations/README.md` still says Concordia is not part of a subnet, and SouthernLights' `Worldspace/Environments-Federation-Cities.md` calls it "the sixth" subnet; marked for correction.
  - Dumont d'Urville and Denison are Janbogo cities even though they lie near the Mirny subnet.
  - The DLC overview once called Mirny "Concordia's own subnet" (corrected 2026-07-04).
  - The per-subnet overview questions how well the East Sinitic label fits the subnet's Australian and New Zealand founders.

### Japan-founded Tepenian city
- **Category:** method term
- **Definition:** (Out of fiction) The required form of words for a city's origin: name the founding stock, then say the people are Tepenian with a local culture of their own, rather than calling it a Japanese (or other national) city. ### Jeju-do
- **Category:** place
- **Also called:** Jeju-Do (spelling)
- **Definition:** Jeju Island as a three-power condominium of Korea, Japan and the Sinian Federation, submitted by Korea as neutral ground and home of the International Court of Diplomacy from 2267. Korean, Japanese and Mandarin are equally valid before its law, but Korean prevails in any conflict of translation. Maps after the Court's founding show the island in its own color.
- **Status:** Timeline canon. The author first stated the condominium and the Korean precedence on 2026-09-23, and they were migrated to the Timeline's East Asia record.
- **Used in:** TepenianUniverseTimeline; InnerTepeniaGDD; CurrentNovelDocs (worldbuilding); SouthernLights; TheCryptographHelixDD; DerivationMechanisms.
- **Sources:** TepenianUniverseTimeline `Worldspace/Locations/Earth/Upper_Earth/Asia_East/README.md`; `Reference/World_History_Reference.md`; CurrentNovelDocs `worldbuilding/history/Jeju-do - Neutral Ground and Language Precedence.md`.
- **Open:** Whether the condominium survives the Falkland Treaty is open.

### Jeju-do allocation
- **Category:** era or event
- **Also called:** Jeju-do partition; Jeju-do diplomatic allocation
- **Definition:** The pre-exile diplomatic settlement at the International Court of Diplomacy in which Japan, Korea and China settled their Larsemann Hills claims. Shirayuki (the Bharati site) went to Japan and Sinheung to Korea, chiefly to block an uncontested Chinese claim. China's claim to Zhongshan predates it and was confirmed.
- **Status:** Timeline canon for Shirayuki's allocation to Japan (`No_Subcontinentals.md`). Project canon for the rest (Founding Register, DR-44).
- **Used in:** InnerTepeniaGDD; TepenianUniverseTimeline; CurrentNovelDocs; TheCryptographHelixDD; SouthernLights; DerivationMechanisms.
- **Sources:** TepenianUniverseTimeline `Reference/No_Subcontinentals.md`; InnerTepeniaGDD `Cities/Founding_Register.md` (Shirayuki, Sinheung); `Neo-Races-and-Cultures/Mirny_Subnet/Shirayuki/Shirayuki_Catalog.md`; `Neo-Races-and-Cultures/Mirny_Subnet/Sinheung/Sinheung_Catalog.md`.
- **Open:** ⚠️ **Timing.** The Timeline's draft Falkland Treaty (Art. II.3) says station assignments would be agreed after signing and need not follow a station's former nation (see International Court of Diplomacy).

### Jeju-do ruling
- **Category:** law or rule
- **Also called:** robot-personhood ruling
- **Definition:** The International Court of Diplomacy's ruling of April 27, 2318 that granted robots full legal personhood. It came in the case of Gyeong-ja Yun, a robot nightclub DJ whose Mafioso boss exploited her unpaid labor. Robots held that personhood on Upper Earth until the Falkland Treaty withdrew it in 2564; in the Timeline's structure the ruling is the Midpoint of the First Interwar Period.
- **Status:** Timeline canon for the ruling and its date. Project canon (author, 2026-10-11) that its effects spread slowly to other countries; each country's year is open.
- **Used in:** TepenianUniverseTimeline; InnerTepeniaGDD; CurrentNovelDocs (worldbuilding; every New North America novel); TheCryptographHelixDD; SouthernLights; TorontoFellOutGDD; DerivationMechanisms.
- **Sources:** TepenianUniverseTimeline `Reference/World_History_Reference.md`; `Timeline Eras/1 The First Interwar Period/Timeline.md` (Midpoint); CurrentNovelDocs `worldbuilding/history/Author Rulings 2026-10-11 - Jeju-do Diffusion and Tales from the New Southwest.md`; TheCryptographHelixDD `series-overview/universe-timeline.md`.
- **Open:**
  - ⚠️ **How fast it spread.** The newer CurrentNovelDocs ruling (2026-10-11) says it binds Korea, Japan and the Sinian Federation at once, but its effects reach other countries with robot populations only a generation or two later (roughly 2320 to 2380), and robots are legal property until then. The Timeline says robots held personhood on Upper Earth for all 246 years, with no delay outside the three countries.
  - The Timeline leaves its beat sub-type (False Victory or False Defeat) open; the Maggie Aarden planning file argues for False Victory.
