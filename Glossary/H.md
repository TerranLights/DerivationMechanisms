### Hacking
- **Category:** game term
- **Definition:** Inner Tepenia: a Calculation skill for breaking into systems, one of several descended from Fallout's Science. It is the skill side of the hacking either-or gate (see Hacking and Traceability System).
- **Status:** project canon
- **Used in:** InnerTepeniaGDD
- **Sources:** InnerTepeniaGDD `Game-Mechanics/Character-Creation/Skills.md`; `Game-Mechanics/Core-Mechanics/Hacking_and_Traceability_System.md`
- **Open:** It was split from the former skill "Arcanet Navigation & Hacking," the name Outer Tepenia 1 still uses.

### Hacking and Traceability System
- **Category:** game term
- **Definition:** Inner Tepenia: hack targets have security levels 0 to 6, each opening to a Calculation threshold or a Hacking threshold, with both together unlocking extra options (adapted from Cyberpunk 2077's access points). Every hack is traceable by default and can bring suspicion, alerts and reputation loss; only investment reduces the trace, Level 4 to 6 systems stay traceable, reading data leaves a lighter trace than changing it, and hacks nobody sees or traces add no Infamy.
- **Status:** project canon (traceable-by-default is a hard rule)
- **Used in:** InnerTepeniaGDD; Outer Tepenia 1 (fork)
- **Sources:** InnerTepeniaGDD `Game-Mechanics/Core-Mechanics/Hacking_and_Traceability_System.md`

### Hall of Archives
- **Category:** organization
- **Also called:** International Archive (GDD and TepenianHawaiian name); Doris Morikawa's Hall of Archives; data archive library (song prompt)
- **Definition:** A pre-war knowledge-preservation institution in the Sovereign Republic of Hawaii, a politically and economically neutral zone for scientific research, that cataloged and preserved much of pre-war human knowledge against a global catastrophe; its Head Librarian, a title known to Tepenians, was Doris Morikawa. The GDD and TepenianHawaiian say it later became a major data source for building the Arcanet in Antarctica.
- **Status:** Timeline canon for its role (Doris Morikawa's character file); project canon for the Arcanet link under the name International Archive (developer, 2026-10-13); its fate in the wars is open. Both names are true (author, 2026-10-30): either one is the official name and the other the name people generally know it by, or one names the institution and the other its building in Hawaii. Both framings are valid; neither is chosen yet, and neither is which name is which.
- **Used in:** TepenianUniverseTimeline; InnerTepeniaGDD; CurrentNovelDocs (Hawaii profile; Research Unions file; doll-music extraction); SouthernLights; TepenianHawaiian
- **Sources:** TepenianUniverseTimeline `Worldspace/Characters/Dolls/Doris Morikawa/README.md`; `Personal_Background/Formative_History.md` (same folder); InnerTepeniaGDD `Worldspace/Modern_Hawaiian_Language.md` §1; TepenianHawaiian `CANON.md` C-1; CurrentNovelDocs `worldbuilding/nations/Sovereign Republic of Hawaii - National Character Profile.md`; SouthernLights `Storyline/Writers-Room-Hooks.md`
- **Open:**
  - **Name.** Which framing applies (official name and common name, or institution and building) is not yet decided, and neither is which name is which (author, 2026-10-30).
  - **Fate.** The Timeline and GDD READMEs leave open whether it survived the wars and whether its records reached Tepenia, against the GDD developer note and TepenianHawaiian C-1 that it fed the Arcanet. A link to the Great Corruption was dropped as too speculative (2026-07-20).

### Halley
- **Category:** place
- **Definition:** The Halley subnet's namesake city on the Halley Station site, on the floating, moving Brunt Ice Shelf: the only city designed from the start to relocate, its ski-mounted modules moved inland ahead of the drift, with no docks or airstrip by design, and an identity of "Built to Move." A connecting road off Hwy 7 is its only road link. It was damaged in the Long Night War but stayed partially operational, and it is the centerpiece of DLC 5.
- **Status:** Timeline canon (name, subnet); project canon for its founders (DR-22, partial: the UK in a central role) and design.
- **Used in:** InnerTepeniaGDD (DLC 5); TepenianUniverseTimeline; SouthernLights; CurrentNovelDocs (reference copy); TorontoFellOutGDD (basis copy); DerivationMechanisms
- **Sources:** InnerTepeniaGDD `Cities/Specs/Halley subnet/Halley.md`; `Cities/Founding_Register.md`; `.../Tepenian-Federation/Locations/Infrastructure/Highways.md` (developer ruling 2026-10-03); TepenianUniverseTimeline `Reference/Amundsen_Station_Archive_and_Trucking_Network.md`; `Worldspace/Locations/README.md`; SouthernLights `Worldspace/Environments-Federation-Cities.md` (Halley)
- **Open:**
  - **Founders.** The Register (2026-10-01, partial ruling) gives the UK in a central role; the older subnet reference (`Halley_Subnet_Reference.md`, 2026-09-02) describes a South African-led founding wave with an American majority.
  - It names the subnet without being its technical nexus, which is at Sanay.
  - **Hwy 59.** The Halley spec calls Halley its "origin point" and the Timeline runs Hwy 59 from Halley; `Highways.md` starts it from a Hwy 7 ramp between Halley and Santa Luce.

### Handwave Register
- **Category:** method term
- **Definition:** Out of fiction: OOLSD's numbered list of the only things allowed to break known physics in the Tepenian universe; each entry states what is waved and what is not, and everything else obeys physics. Its one accepted handwave is H-1, the nanotech gel brain: how it gives a robot full consciousness is waved, but her human-shaped body still obeys mass, heat and radiation. It also logs developer physics rulings (RL-n, for example that robots need radiation shielding as humans do) and candidate handwaves (C-1 to C-167), tagged "bend" (physics would have to give) or "cost" (physics holds, but a mechanism, mass, power or duty cycle must be shown).
- **Status:** project canon for the rule, H-1 and the RL rulings (developer, 2026-10-06); working for the candidates, none accepted (next number C-168).
- **Used in:** CurrentNovelDocs (OOLSD); DerivationMechanisms (copy); Hybrid-Anthro_Culture (as a model); InnerTepeniaGDD (one reference)
- **Sources:** CurrentNovelDocs `worldbuilding/Object_Oriented_Location_Synthesis/04_Handwave_Register.md`; `02_Design_Brief.md` §8
- **Open:** Candidate batches from tranche 6 onward carry no bend or cost tag.

### Hardcore Mode
- **Category:** game term
- **Definition:** Inner Tepenia: the optional harder mode governed by the developer's 2026-09-04 ruling that every Hardcore system must change how the player plays, not add another survival meter; the reference case is splitting siligel into distinct classes. It makes companions die permanently, gives ammo and siligel weight, has siligel heal gradually, and adds component wear, memory integrity and cold penalties (such as reduced siligel benefit in the cold). Toronto Fell Out instead keeps Fallout: New Vegas's Hardcore Mode, name and concept unchanged.
- **Status:** project canon (governing standard, 2026-09-04); working (several systems await rework; full design pending)
- **Used in:** InnerTepeniaGDD; Outer Tepenia 1 (fork); TorontoFellOutGDD; CurrentNovelDocs (reference copy); DerivationMechanisms
- **Sources:** InnerTepeniaGDD `Game-Mechanics/Core-Mechanics/Hardcore_Mode.md`; `Storyline/DLC_Overview.md` (Cross-DLC Survival Gifts); TorontoFellOutGDD `Reference/Open-Worldbuilding-Decisions.md`; Outer Tepenia 1 `Game-Mechanics/Core-Mechanics/Hardcore_Mode.md`
- **Open:** Power Consumption fails the standard as written (it is framed like hunger and thirst), and the thermal, Coldshock and blizzard rules are flagged as weak. The Outer Tepenia copy is still titled for Inner Tepenia.

### Held Overflow Ledger (redistribution pool)
- **Category:** method term
- **Definition:** Out of fiction: a census section that parks population removed from a city, by a Gate 11 extent correction or a developer trim, until it is reassigned, so national totals still add up; it is bookkeeping, never an in-world migration. The whole pool became the founding population of the 38th city, Relung Panen, which inherits nothing from the source cities.
- **Status:** project canon (opened 2026-09-05; 947,954 in Census I, raised to 971,870 on 2026-10-28 by DR-68)

- **Sources:** InnerTepeniaGDD `Cities/Official_Population_Census.md` (§D-OVERFLOW; Redistribution)
- **Open:** `Cities/Overview.md` says Relung Panen was founded as the census's redistribution pool, which can read as an in-world event; the census forbids that reading. The census line that Relung Panen's composition is TBD is superseded by DR-34 and DR-61 (Indonesia and Malaysia).
