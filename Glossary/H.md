# Glossary: H

### HyperHedral
- **Category:** robot-fabrication megacorporation
- **Definition:** HyperHedral Industries, one of the post-war megacorporations that design, fabricate, produce, program and distribute sentient robots during the First Interwar Period. It is based primarily in the People's Democratic Republic of Cascadia, and it makes robots to order, with options.
- **Status:** Timeline canon for the name, base and role. "Makes robots to order" is project canon from the novels.
- **Used in:**
  - **TepenianUniverseTimeline:** the megacorp records.
  - **CurrentNovelDocs:** *Streetside Harmonies* (it supplied Valerie), the Ric Alarcón backstory (he worked for it in northern Baja California, then defected), and the worldbuilding notes.
  - **InnerTepeniaGDD:** the location methodology's corporation roster.
  - The doll-music lyrics.
- **Sources:** TepenianUniverseTimeline `Megacorps/README.md` and `Megacorps/HyperHedral/README.md`; CurrentNovelDocs `Streetside Harmonies/` and `Ricardo Alarcón Backstory (title TBD)/`.
- **Open:** whether the company exists in the Second Interwar Period is not stated in the Timeline.
