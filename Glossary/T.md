# Glossary: T

### T1 to T8 (pre-trip tools)
- **Category:** method term
- **Definition:** (out of fiction; Universal Location Methodology) Eight checks bundled with the Pre-Trip Inspection and run on every step, phase and city: T1 handoff accounting, T2 sweep receipt, T3 position-coverage ledger, T4 what the phase's axis had no use for, T5 the Lover faculty run early, T6 quotation audit at each phase close, T7 bare pointers in summary cells, and T8 three-subagent read consensus.
- **Status:** project canon (developer ruling, 2026-09-11)
- **Used in:** InnerTepeniaGDD; DerivationMechanisms
- **Sources:** InnerTepeniaGDD `.../Universal_Location_Methodology/PRE-TRIP_INSPECTION_RECIPE_TRIAL.md` (tool table); `.../Universal_Location_Methodology/00_RUNBOOK.md` (Step 4)

### T8 (three-subagent read consensus)
- **Category:** method term
- **Also called:** three-subagent read consensus; three-reader consensus
- **Definition:** (out of fiction; Universal Location Methodology) The read check in which three independent subagent readers each read a step's or phase's required sources in full and prove it with a PROOF block, followed by a mechanical check against the sources and a cross-check of the readers against one another. Content is written only if all three agree, and the write merges the readers' finds rather than picking one. It has been binding on every step, phase and city since 2026-09-11.
- **Status:** project canon (developer rulings, 2026-09-11 and 2026-09-28)
- **Used in:** InnerTepeniaGDD (ULM; every city pass folder); CurrentNovelDocs; DerivationMechanisms
- **Sources:** InnerTepeniaGDD `.../Universal_Location_Methodology/00_RUNBOOK.md` (Step 4, the T1–T8 binding block); `.../Universal_Location_Methodology/T8_Standing_Brief_Lines.md`; `.../Universal_Location_Methodology/Tools/triple_read_verify.py`; `.../Universal_Location_Methodology/T8_Standard_Entries.json`
- **Open:** in city folders, `NNb_T8_Rounds_….md` files hold the round records (a round is one read cycle), and hidden `.t8_*` files hold briefs, the outputs of readers A, B and C, checkpoints and `.t8_required_*` reading lists.

### TANA
- **Category:** organization; navigation authority
- **Definition:** The Tepenian Astrometric & Navigational Authority, the in-universe body responsible for the solar system's navigation reference frame, the Solar Positioning System. It:
  - keeps the frozen-epoch definitions of the two reference axes as fixed constants;
  - periodically re-measures where the Invariable Plane's pole and the star Regulus actually are;
  - publishes the correction offsets between the frozen lines and their true positions, on a regular cycle, as an "Almanac";
  - runs Space Traffic Control sites, staffed by Space Traffic Controllers.
- **Status:** project canon in the Cryptograph Helix design docs.
- **Used in:** the Cryptograph Helix novel series (TheCryptographHelixDD), including a TANA space traffic controller character.
- **Sources:** TheCryptographHelixDD `world/navigation.md`; `characters/tana_controller.md`; `TODO.md`.
- **Open:** the Almanac's update cycle is not set. A stale copy of the Almanac is a planned plot beat; see Stale Navigational Almanac.

### Temirötkel
- **Category:** place
- **Also called:** Sayowa (former name, until 2026-10-03); Temirotkel (file-name spelling); темірөткел (Cyrillic)
- **Definition:** A Mawson-subnet city on the mainland Sôya Coast of Lützow-Holm Bay, with East Ongul Island (the Syowa Station site) as its heavily industrial port; the name is Kazakh for "Iron Crossing." Founded by Kazakhstan, later supported by the Confederacy of Intermarium Nations and industrialized by Kazakh industrialists, it is a fabrication and trucking city shaped like a long loading dock, beside the Temirötkel Junction, and it supplied the material that built Mawson and the Larsemann cities. It was damaged in the Long Night War.
- **Status:** project canon (renamed `DR-37`, 2026-10-03; founders `DR-30`, `DR-62`). The Timeline lists only the old name, Sayowa.
- **Used in:** InnerTepeniaGDD (DLC 4; megasheets; Background-Lore; records); DerivationMechanisms (method copies); TepenianUniverseTimeline, SouthernLights, CurrentNovelDocs (reference copy) and TorontoFellOutGDD (as Sayowa)
- **Sources:** InnerTepeniaGDD `.../Locations/Cities/Specs/Mawson subnet/Temirotkel.md`; `.../Cities/City_Renames_Alias_Table_2026-10-03.md`; `.../Cities/Founding_Register.md`; TepenianUniverseTimeline `Worldspace/Locations/README.md`; SouthernLights `Worldspace/Environments-Federation-Cities.md` (Sayowa)
- **Open:**
  - **Rename.** The Timeline, SouthernLights, CurrentNovelDocs and TorontoFellOutGDD still use Sayowa (a Tepenianized form of Syowa/Shōwa); the Timeline's history reference also spells the station "Syowa."
  - **Old founding.** Japanese founders are ruled out, but Background-Lore chains (#2 "A Name Two Ways," #10 "The Diluted Founding") and other passages built on the Syowa/Japanese founding are revisit items.
  - **War damage.** The GDD has it damaged in the war; SouthernLights' Sayowa was spared in 2812 as too critical to destroy. SouthernLights also describes Sayowa as the crossroads city that sends migrants to the Hub by design.
  - **Demonym** open; Sayowan belonged to the old name.

### Temirötkel Junction

### Tepenian
- **Category:** group or people
- **Also called:** Tepenians (plural)
- **Definition:** The demonym and adjective for Tepenia and its people, robots and humans alike, used at every scale. In Act 1 of the Second Interwar Period (2564 into the 2600s) the people are still their founding nations living in Antarctica; from Act 2 (the late 2600s or early 2700s on) they are Tepenian in name and identity, and national origin is only ancestry. The prescribed phrasing is "a Japan-founded Tepenian city," not "a Japanese city."
- **Status:** Timeline canon (developer rulings, 2026-09-06)
- **Used in:** every Tepenian-universe repo
- **Sources:** TepenianUniverseTimeline `Timeline Eras/2 The Second Interwar Period/README.md`; `Reference/No_National_Stereotypes.md` (correct form of words); InnerTepeniaGDD `Neo-Races-and-Cultures/README.md`; `Worldspace/Locations-and-Levels/Super_Ultra_Megasheet/README.md` (Full Extrapolation IV)
- **Open:** smaller-scale demonyms named in the GDD include Concordian, Peninsulans, Mawsonite (placeholder) and Byrdian.

### Tepenian Constitution
- **Category:** law or rule
- **Definition:** Tepenia's future founding document, to which the Falkland Treaty deliberately leaves the country's form of government. It is to be written entirely by robots yet make humans equal members of society before the law, and the Fundamental Laws of Robotics shape its spirit rather than appearing as clauses.
- **Status:** Timeline canon for its authorship and spirit; the document is mentioned but not drafted.
- **Used in:** TepenianUniverseTimeline; InnerTepeniaGDD; CurrentNovelDocs; SouthernLights; DerivationMechanisms

- **Open:** the GDD calls the Suspended Compact the Federation's founding document; how the two relate is not stated.

- **Category:** law or rule

Petty or culturally offensive crimes bring exile from the offender's home district (offenders tend to resettle in Pisces or Sagittarius); serious crimes bring closely supervised dangerous labor in Virgo or Aries; truly abhorrent crimes (Tier 3) mean being forcibly "volunteered" as an Aquarius test subject.
- **Status:** working (first pass, 2026-07-24)
- **Used in:** InnerTepeniaGDD

### Tepenian currency
- **Category:** unit or measure
- **Definition:** Tepenia's money followed a many-to-one-to-many arc: rough local currencies per subnet in the early Federation, a national system by the late Second Interwar Period, then a re-fracture into regional currencies when Amundsen Tower fell. What it is based on is undecided; siligel or coolant as a commodity standard is ruled out, though siligel can be bartered.
- **Status:** project canon for the shape only (`DR-3`; author, 2026-10-30); working for the basis and name: the author holds the currency absolutely unnamed and unsettled and has marked its design for later work (2026-10-30)
- **Used in:** InnerTepeniaGDD
- **Sources:** InnerTepeniaGDD `Worldspace/National_Economy_and_Currency.md`; `TODO.md` ("National currency name and mechanics")
- **Open:** ⛔ the currency has no name at any stage, and no name or earlier placeholder may be used (developer instruction). See also Subnet currencies.

- **Category:** organization
- **Definition:** The Federation's national government, seated at Fort McMurdo. No government bodies or offices are named, and the Falkland Treaty leaves the form of government to Tepenia.
- **Status:** project canon for the seat (`DR-21`; resolved 2026-07-07); the structure is TBD in the Timeline.
