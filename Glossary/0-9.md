### 100 Miles Davis Boulevard
- **Category:** place
- **Definition:** The address of Palmer City's first customs office, a makeshift reception point for arriving robots and their human partners at one end of Miles Davis Boulevard. It was the first official address in the Federation of Tepenia and became its most historically significant one; the found Antarctica flag was displayed there.
- **Status:** project canon (GDD city spec).
- **Used in:** InnerTepeniaGDD; SouthernLights; CurrentNovelDocs (reference copy); DerivationMechanisms.
- **Sources:** InnerTepeniaGDD `Worldspace/Locations-and-Levels/Outside-World/Tepenian-Federation/Locations/Cities/Specs/Palmer subnet/Palmer_City.md` (Miles Davis Boulevard; Notable Locations); SouthernLights `Worldspace/Environments-Federation-Cities.md` (Palmer City); GDD Palmer City Megasheet.
- **Open:** SouthernLights uses "100 Miles Davis Boulevard" as the name of a street (the historic, lived-in heart of the city, and a dense live-music street alongside the Little Burgundy Quarter), proposed as a location name. The GDD spec uses it only for the address; the street itself is Miles Davis Boulevard, which has its own entry.

### ±3 solar time zone window
- **Category:** method term
- **Also called:** ±3 solar-UTC window
- **Definition:** (out of fiction) The Inner Tepenia census's proximity test: a nation with a small exile pool qualifies for a city's Notable tier only if it lies within 3 solar time zones of the city, while large pools reach any city by size. Distance is measured from the nearest zone where a meaningful share of the nation's eligible population lives, and a nation routed through another nation's gateway uses the gateway's distance.
- **Status:** project canon (GDD rulings log DR-19: the window gates the Notable tier only).
- **Used in:** InnerTepeniaGDD (Cities; ULM); DerivationMechanisms.
- **Sources:** InnerTepeniaGDD `Cities/Upper_Earth_Immigration_Composition.md` (How factor 1 is measured; How the solar time zone window works); GDD rulings log DR-19.
- **Open:**
  - The same file records a known inconsistency between Davis and Zhongshan over how Russia is treated.
  - It is distinct from the founders' time-zone proximity rule.
