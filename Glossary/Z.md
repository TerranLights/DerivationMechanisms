### Zhongshan
- **Category:** place
- **Definition:** A Mirny-subnet city and one of the Tri-Cities, in the Larsemann Hills on Prydz Bay, on the Zhongshan Station site and named for Sun Yat-sen. It was founded by Chinese exiles of the Sinian Federation who chose exile with the robots, and it is the only city whose founding nation stayed demographically first throughout. It is known for an unhurried fused culture, a non-prying social norm, ceremonial tea and the Long Record archive, and was damaged in the Long Night War but is partly operational.
- **Status:** Timeline canon for the name, subnet and Chinese founding (the Jeju-do court confirmed its claim to continuous Chinese, then Sinian, habitation). Project canon for the founders as ruled in the GDD (DR-21).
- **Used in:** InnerTepeniaGDD (DLC 7); TepenianUniverseTimeline; SouthernLights; CurrentNovelDocs (reference copy; worldbuilding); TheCryptographHelixDD; TorontoFellOutGDD (basis copy); DerivationMechanisms.
- **Sources:** InnerTepeniaGDD `Cities/Specs/Mirny subnet/Zhongshan.md`; `Cities/Founding_Register.md` (DR-21); TepenianUniverseTimeline `Reference/No_National_Stereotypes.md`; `Reference/Amundsen_Station_Archive_and_Trucking_Network.md`; SouthernLights `Worldspace/Environments-Federation-Cities.md` (Zhongshan).
- **Open:**
  - The CurrentNovelDocs reference copy of its culture sheet still says the Sinian Federation "drove" the War of Upper Earth, wording the GDD corrected on 2026-08-30.
  - Its local-culture sheet calls the founders Sinian defectors, against the ruled Chinese founding.
  - Its demonym: Zhongshanese (own entry), while the city spec still says TBD.

### Zodiac (district labels)
- **Category:** method term
- **Definition:** (out of fiction) The GDD's design shorthand for keeping Concordia's thirteen districts distinct, one zodiac sign each: Cancer, Taurus, Leo, Scorpio, Aries, Capricorn, Aquarius, Libra, Gemini, Pisces, Sagittarius, Virgo, and the Hub (True Neutral; Ophiuchus in the substrate). The zodiac is never in-fiction: no one in Concordia would recognize it, the districts emerged organically, and players meet only the districts' in-fiction names.
- **Status:** project canon (ruled not in-fiction).
- **Used in:** InnerTepeniaGDD (widely); SouthernLights; CurrentNovelDocs; DerivationMechanisms; copies.
- **Sources:** InnerTepeniaGDD `Concordia-City/Concordia_Second_Interwar_Cultural_Sheet.md` §2; `Concordia-City/Districts/Zodiac_Personality_Substrate/00_Method_and_Sources.md`.
- **Open:** in-fiction names so far are the Sanctuary, the Power Core, the Yards, the Labs, the Government District, the Circuit, the Markets, the Frostlands, the Undergrid and Concordia Central Hub; Taurus, Leo and Scorpio are still to be named.

### Zodiac Lens
- **Category:** method term
- **Definition:** (out of fiction) A Cultural Synthesis Technique proposed by the developer: each of the twelve zodiac signs is used not as a rule but as a point of inspiration, asking what shapes (a person, a place, a thing) that sign would take within one location on its own terms, without referring back to Concordia or assigning the location a sign. In the ULM it runs in Phase 10 and amends Phase 9 later without blocking it.
- **Status:** project canon (developer-proposed 2026-08-31; widely applied in the location passes).
- **Used in:** InnerTepeniaGDD (ULM; CST; city passes); DerivationMechanisms; Hybrid-Anthro_Culture.
- **Sources:** InnerTepeniaGDD `Worldspace/Locations-and-Levels/Cultural_Synthesis_Techniques.md` (Technique — The Zodiac Lens; §4); ULM `03_The_Phase_Spine.md` (Phase 10 §B2).
- **Open:** it is distinct from Concordia's binding one-sign-per-district use of the zodiac and is not part of the City Symbolic Substrate; the DR-8 symbol deferral does not affect it.

### Zodiac Personality Substrate
- **Category:** method term
- **Definition:** (out of fiction) A design substrate mined from eight astrology books that gives each of Concordia's thirteen districts a temperament family, material texture, shadow expression and conflict geometry (twelve signs plus Ophiuchus for the Hub), used alongside the Enneagram reads. Its repeatable use is the Substrate Application Pass, and none of it is in-fiction.
- **Status:** project canon (built 2026-08-29; the out-of-fiction rule is a standing developer correction).
- **Used in:** InnerTepeniaGDD; CurrentNovelDocs; DerivationMechanisms; Hybrid-Anthro_Culture.
- **Sources:** InnerTepeniaGDD `Concordia-City/Districts/Zodiac_Personality_Substrate/00_Method_and_Sources.md`; `Concordia-City/Districts/Phase_Instructions/00e_Substrate_Application_Pass.md`; DerivationMechanisms `Data_Reference/Symbolism/Zodiac/from InnerTepeniaGDD/Zodiac_Personality_Substrate/00_Method_and_Sources.md`.
- **Open:** its shadow sections are deliberately heavy from extraction and must not be applied in proportion. The ULM's symbol register rates it the only RICH symbol system in the project.

### Zodiac Signs Full Attributes
- **Category:** method term
- **Definition:** (out of fiction) A reference catalog of the twelve Western zodiac signs (element, modality, ruling planet, symbol, positive and negative attributes), compiled as creative input for Concordia's districts and later used by the Zodiac Lens. It is not in-world lore.
- **Status:** project canon (reference tool).
- **Used in:** InnerTepeniaGDD; DerivationMechanisms.

### Zukelli
- **Category:** place
- **Definition:** A Janbogo-subnet city on Terra Nova Bay at the Mario Zucchelli Station site, about 8 km from Janbogo; the name condenses Zucchelli. Founded by Australia, it was known for its food and hospitality (the Long Table) and its polynya, and was the southern end of Hwy 183. Because it sheltered Upper Earth soldiers who had fully defected, their home military obliterated it in the Long Night War as a deterrent, deliberately leaving Janbogo standing to witness; no living population remains.
- **Status:** Timeline canon for the name and the destruction. Project canon for the Australian founders (DR-20, DR-44). Working: a rename is ruled but no name is chosen (DR-58).
- **Used in:** InnerTepeniaGDD (DLC 6); TepenianUniverseTimeline; SouthernLights; CurrentNovelDocs (reference copy); TheCryptographHelixDD; TorontoFellOutGDD (basis copy); DerivationMechanisms.
- **Sources:** InnerTepeniaGDD `Cities/Specs/Janbogo subnet/Zukelli.md`; `Cities/Founding_Register.md`; TepenianUniverseTimeline `Timeline Eras/2 The Second Interwar Period/Timeline.md` (END); `Worldspace/Locations/README.md`; InnerTepeniaGDD `Neo-Races-and-Cultures/Janbogo_Subnet/Zukelli/Zukelli_Catalog.md`; SouthernLights `Worldspace/Locations.md`; `Storyline/Writers-Room-Hooks.md`.
- **Open:**
  - **Rename:** the GDD rulings log (DR-58) rules it will get a new name in line with its Australian founders; none is chosen yet.
  - **Founders:** Italian founders are ruled out, but non-canon Background-Lore says Italian exiles set up its earliest civic institutions. The developer has floated re-founding one Australian-founded city instead (FQ-20).
  - The defectors' home nation, and so the nationality of the force that struck the city, is open.
  - Its demonym is open (see Zukellesi).
  - The Timeline names it the leading origin candidate for a gynoid still to be named [SE-031].

### Zukelli Native
- **Category:** game term
- **Definition:** (Inner Tepenia, DLC 6) A special perk from a chain at Zukelli's ruins: after decoding Zukelli's structural language and doing a favor for a guardian, the player gains guaranteed non-hostility with Zukelli natives and their descendants, and special dialogue with anyone tied to Zukelli. It is the first use of the stat-gated interaction highlight.
- **Status:** project canon (confirmed 2026-07-07); the favor's details are to be decided.
- **Used in:** InnerTepeniaGDD; CurrentNovelDocs; TorontoFellOutGDD.
- **Sources:** InnerTepeniaGDD `Cities/Specs/Janbogo subnet/Zukelli.md`; `Game-Mechanics/Perks/Special_Unique_Perks.md`; `Game-Mechanics/Core-Mechanics/Interaction_Highlight_System.md`.

### Zukelli structural language
- **Category:** game term
- **Definition:** (Inner Tepenia, DLC 6) The color and pattern markings Zukelli's residents left on covered connections and heating conduits. A player with 10 Calculation, 10 Investigation, and at least 6 Engine and 6 Nerve can partly decode them and use that knowledge to talk down a guardian at the ruins; decoding it leads to the Zukelli Native perk.
- **Status:** project canon (confirmed 2026-07-07).
- **Used in:** InnerTepeniaGDD.
- **Sources:** InnerTepeniaGDD `DONE.md` (Zukelli Ruins — Structural Language Decoding Mechanic); `Cities/Specs/Janbogo subnet/Zukelli.md` (Gameplay Notes).
