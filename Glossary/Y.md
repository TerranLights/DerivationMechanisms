# Glossary: Y

### The Yards
- **Category:** place
- **Also called:** Capricorn (design label)
- **Definition:** The in-fiction name of Concordia's District 6, the heavy-industry district that built the insulated domes and makes robot frames, robot components and siligel. Its fabrication yards form an outer arc that sits partly outside the domes, and it runs a literal spire meritocracy in which floor level marks status.
- **Status:** project canon.
- **Used in:** InnerTepeniaGDD; SouthernLights; TorontoFellOutGDD; DerivationMechanisms; copies.
- **Sources:** InnerTepeniaGDD `Concordia-City/Districts/District_Canon_Reference.md` (District 6); SouthernLights `Worldspace/Environments-Concordia-Districts.md` (6. Capricorn).
- **Open:** the GDD records it as the district's in-fiction name; SouthernLights calls it an in-world nickname.

### The Year of Silence
- **Category:** era or event
- **Definition:** The one time Virgo's (the Undergrid's) workforce worked strictly to rule, not quite a strike: no improvisation, no covering for others, no overtime, only what contracts specified. The city began failing within two weeks and Libra gave in, with its promises about half kept; the memory remains Virgo's main leverage.
- **Status:** project canon (confirmed non-malicious 2026-07-20). The Timeline names it among Concordia's district events but does not index it, because it is undated.
- **Used in:** InnerTepeniaGDD; TepenianUniverseTimeline; SouthernLights; DerivationMechanisms; copies.
- **Sources:** InnerTepeniaGDD `Concordia-City/Districts/District_Canon_Reference.md` (District 12); `Storyline/Side-Content/District_Main_Questlines.md` (District 12); TepenianUniverseTimeline `Timeline Eras/2 The Second Interwar Period/Timeline.md`.
- **Open:** the pre-war Virgo Megasheet (2026-07-16, a proposal) dates it to the final decades of the Second Interwar Period; the canon reference and the Timeline leave it undated.

### The Year of Silence Archive
- **Category:** culture and society
- **Definition:** An Undergrid space that preserves the details of the Year of Silence.
- **Status:** working (2026-07-29 Community Infrastructure & Social Life brainstorm list); since used as an under-questline starting point.
- **Used in:** InnerTepeniaGDD.
- **Sources:** InnerTepeniaGDD `Concordia-City/Districts/District_Canon_Reference.md` (District 12, the Undergrid, Community Infrastructure & Social Life); `Storyline/Side-Content/District_Under_Questlines.md` (Virgo).

### y-template
- **Category:** method term
- **Definition:** (out of fiction) The skeleton folder from which new novel and film projects are stamped. It holds `background notes`, `doll`, `legal-and-production`, `reference`, `structure` (with the Dev-Road-Map), `text`, `unsorted_ideas` and `videos`.
- **Status:** project canon (template).
- **Used in:** CurrentNovelDocs; InnerTepeniaGDD; DerivationMechanisms.
- **Sources:** CurrentNovelDocs `y-template/`; `Ricardo Alarcón Backstory (title TBD)/Author Statements (2026-10-20).md` (folder stamped 2026-10-20).

### Yue
- **Category:** polity
- **Definition:** One of the nine Han successor states that federated as the Sinian Federation.
- **Status:** Timeline canon.
- **Used in:** TepenianUniverseTimeline; InnerTepeniaGDD; CurrentNovelDocs.
- **Sources:** TepenianUniverseTimeline `Worldspace/Locations/Earth/Upper_Earth/Asia_East/README.md`.
