### MACHINE stats
- **Category:** game term
- **Also called:** MACHINE
- **Definition:** The seven core character stats of Inner Tepenia, its version of Fallout's SPECIAL, each rated 1 to 10: Might, Agility, Calculation, Humanity, Investigation, Nerve and Engine. Every stat starts at 5 and the player moves 5 free points (40 in total, minimum 1, maximum 10); NPCs ignore this budget, and stats grow permanently only through Intense Training and implants. The system carries over identically and bindingly to Outer Tepenia 1, Outer Tepenia 2 and New Centauri.
- **Status:** Project canon (the 5+5 model is confirmed). Might and Nerve were marked TENTATIVE in the June 2026 roadmap.
- **Used in:** InnerTepeniaGDD; the Outer Tepenia series; TorontoFellOutGDD (translated to MAPLE-BUD); TepenianUniverseTimeline (Doll stat profiles in `to-be-integrated/`); CurrentNovelDocs (reference copy); TheCryptographHelixDD; SouthernLights; DerivationMechanisms.
- **Sources:** InnerTepeniaGDD `Game-Mechanics/Character-Creation/MACHINE_Stats.md`; `Game-Mechanics/Character-Creation/Character_Creation_Overview.md`; `README.md` (MACHINE Stats); Outer Tepenia 1 `Game_Design_Principles.md` (Series-Wide Systems).
- **Open:**
  - Older Storyline files use "Computation" for C (`Storyline/Minmax-Builds/`) and "Machine" for M (`Storyline/Endings/Secret-Endings/Fast_Minmax_Endings.md`).
  - The GDD version adds city-designated baseline stats; the Outer Tepenia copy lacks them.
  - The Timeline excludes MACHINE stat profiles from its own character files by scope, though Doll stat profiles appear in its `to-be-integrated/` material.
  - `Storyline/Main-Story/Major_Stabilization_Paths.md` uses "the MACHINE" for a central authority or system; it is mentioned, not defined, and is not these stats.

### Machu Picchu Airport
- **Category:** place
- **Definition:** Tepenia's year-round international passenger airport on King George Island, next to the real Machu Picchu Base and linked to Ushuaia; it is where the Machu Picchu Border & Customs Authority actually processes arrivals. It is closest to Contrapunto (formerly Sejong) and near enough to Pergamino (formerly Juan Carlos) that they share a map marker. It did not exist when the Treaty was signed in 2564.
- **Status:** Timeline canon.
- **Used in:** TepenianUniverseTimeline; InnerTepeniaGDD (Airports, Highways, specs, Storyline, Background-Lore); CurrentNovelDocs (reference copy); SouthernLights; DerivationMechanisms.
- **Sources:** TepenianUniverseTimeline `Reference/World_History_Reference.md` ("Machu Picchu Border & Customs Authority"); `Worldspace/Locations/Juan_Carlos.md`; InnerTepeniaGDD `Infrastructure/Airports.md` (Confirmed Airports).
- **Open:**
  - The Timeline still places it "near Sejong", the old name of Contrapunto (DR-43).
  - ⚠️ **"The only international airport."** A developer clarification (2026-09-02) made it the only one, but that holds for one era only. DR-51 (deferred) says the early era had only Marambio's airport; DR-81 (2026-10-29) adds a cargo airport at Relung Panen, and DR-82 adds the Winter Intake Airport. How many international airports exist in each era is open.
  - SouthernLights calls the gateway "Machu Picchu Base".
  - The GDD's port research notes that in the real world Punta Arenas, not Ushuaia, is the air gateway to King George Island.

### Machu Picchu Border & Customs Authority
- **Category:** organization
- **Also called:** Tepenian National Customs; Machu Picchu Border Authority; Machu Picchu customs gateway (SouthernLights)
- **Definition:** Tepenia's national customs and immigration body, a fully Tepenian institution and not an Upper Earth one, controlling entry for arrivals through the Ushuaia gateway. It processes arrivals at Machu Picchu Airport on King George Island, while non-immigrant visitors are routed through a separate corridor to a sealiner bound for Palmer City. Its records began at Juan Carlos (now Pergamino), Tepenia's first bureaucratic archive.
- **Status:** Timeline canon (established 2026-07-05).
- **Used in:** TepenianUniverseTimeline; InnerTepeniaGDD (Pergamino, Contrapunto, Palmer City; Airports; Ports); CurrentNovelDocs (reference copy); SouthernLights; DerivationMechanisms.
- **Sources:** TepenianUniverseTimeline `Reference/World_History_Reference.md` (Machu Picchu Border & Customs Authority); `Worldspace/Locations/Juan_Carlos.md`; InnerTepeniaGDD `Cities/Specs/Palmer subnet/Pergamino.md` (Character & Culture; Open Questions); `Infrastructure/Airports.md`.
- **Open:**
  - **Renamed cities.** The Timeline still uses Juan Carlos (renamed Pergamino, DR-41) and Sejong (renamed Contrapunto, DR-43), both GDD renames of 2026-10-03.
  - The Contrapunto catalog calls the airport Upper Earth's arrival gateway; the Timeline stresses that the Authority is Tepenian.
  - Whether Pergamino shares the border function with Contrapunto is open.
  - Older texts call the arrival point "Machu Picchu Base".

### The Manager
- **Category:** game term
- **Also called:** Foreman (Construction Site title); Pit Boss (Casino title)

### MAPLE-BUD
- **Category:** game term
- **Definition:** Toronto Fell Out: the game's attribute system, its answer to Fallout's SPECIAL, which replaces the GDD's MACHINE stats in all adapted material. MAPLE is Might, Agility, Perception, Luck and Endurance; BUD is Brains (intelligence), Utility (crafting, repairing and jury-rigging) and Determination (the player's effect on companions). Each of the 21 skills hangs on one attribute, except that Luck has no skill and instead adds half its value, rounded up, to every skill.
- **Status:** Project canon.
- **Used in:** TorontoFellOutGDD.
- **Sources:** TorontoFellOutGDD `to-be-integrated/not-SPECIAL.md`; `Game-Mechanics/Character-Creation/Skills.md`.

### Marambio
- **Category:** place
- **Definition:** A Palmer-subnet city on Seymour Island, on the Marambio Base site off the southeast Peninsula, founded by Argentina. It was the Peninsula's air and sea logistics hub: the subnet's domestic aviation hub and its receiving port for South American shipping, reached from Hwy 1 by the Picnic Passage causeway, with a 34-million-year-old fossil record. It was destroyed in the Long Night War by a single strike on its airfield and shipyards, because its small, flat island was a single point of failure.
- **Status:** Timeline canon for the name and subnet. Project canon for the founders (DR-19, DR-22).
- **Used in:** InnerTepeniaGDD (DLC 3); TepenianUniverseTimeline; SouthernLights; CurrentNovelDocs (reference copy); TorontoFellOutGDD (basis copy); DerivationMechanisms.

### Mark
- **Category:** technology
- **Definition:** A generation of the fabrication-synthesis chamber's hardware and design schematic. The current generation, Mark IV, was designed at Neumayer and is what Sinheung and Byrd build today; Sinheung guards a Mark IV schematic archive (the Sinheung Standard). Different Marks were built to different specs and can give robots different attributes, never a better or worse rank. - **Status:** Project canon, and part of the Timeline's Robot Universals as Gen/Mark. The number IV is a placeholder.

### Mars L1 magnetic shield
- **Category:** technology
- **Definition:** A proposed artificial magnetosphere for Mars: a superconducting magnetic dipole station at the Mars-Sun L1 point that deflects the solar wind, so a thickened atmosphere is not stripped away. The staging timeline treats it as the fast, cheap first step of terraforming, possibly with only partial coverage when *The Cryptograph Helix* opens. It is based on a real NASA workshop proposal (Green et al. 2017).
- **Status:** Working: PROPOSED, not verified; who built it is open.
- **Used in:** CurrentNovelDocs (worldbuilding history).
- **Sources:** CurrentNovelDocs `worldbuilding/history/Mars Terraforming - Ocean and Magnetosphere Timeline (staging).md`.
