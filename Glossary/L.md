### LAW 0
- **Category:** method term
- **Also called:** LAW 0 (Depth Over Speed); depth over speed
- **Definition:** (Out of fiction) The standing law of all worldbuilding, culture and location work: depth over speed. Contemplate before writing, research instead of recalling, chase each finding to its third-order effects, and never treat completion as a goal in itself. The closing test asks whether a person could live a whole life in the place and whether that life would be unlike life anywhere comparable; its research corollary is LAW 0-R.
- **Status:** Project canon (binding developer law; restated 2026-08-31 and stated in full at the top of every ULM file).
- **Used in:** InnerTepeniaGDD; CurrentNovelDocs; DerivationMechanisms; Hybrid-Anthro_Culture.
- **Sources:** InnerTepeniaGDD `CLAUDE.md` (LAW 0); `Universal_Location_Methodology/README.md`; `Universal_Location_Methodology/00_RUNBOOK.md`; `Districts/Phase_Instructions/00_RUNBOOK.md`.

### LAW 0-R
- **Category:** method term
- **Also called:** LAW 0-R (Research Fully); research fully
- **Definition:** (Out of fiction) LAW 0 applied to research: research a place fully. One search of a pick shows that it exists, not what it holds, so a source is not exhausted because it has been searched once. Recalled facts are not input data, and there is no time limit or search budget.
- **Status:** Project canon (developer instruction, 2026-09-06); stated in full in every research file.
- **Used in:** InnerTepeniaGDD (RWBEM, ULM, city research logs); CurrentNovelDocs; DerivationMechanisms.
- **Sources:** InnerTepeniaGDD `Worldspace/Locations-and-Levels/Real-World_Basis_Extrapolation_Method.md` (LAW 0-R); `Cities/Research_Logs/README.md`; `Universal_Location_Methodology/00_RUNBOOK.md` (Step 3).

### Law of No Forced Fit
- **Category:** method term
- **Also called:** No Forced Fit; NO FORCED FIT (the GDD `CLAUDE.md` heading); empty-slot law (the Founding Register and the rulings log)
- **Definition:** (Out of fiction) The ULM's binding law: never force a location or community into a roster category it does not naturally occupy (faith or religion, faction, symbol, founder), either by assigning a roster member that does not fit or by inventing one to fill a blank. An empty slot is a result, not a gap: the pass says nothing fits, leaves the slot explicitly open, and writes what the place actually has (a stake, a social contract, a debt, a standard of worth). Rosters are open, so their size is never the test.
- **Status:** Project canon (developer ruling, 2026-09-06).
- **Used in:** InnerTepeniaGDD (ULM; Founding_Register; audits; passes; CLAUDE.md); DerivationMechanisms; CurrentNovelDocs; Hybrid-Anthro_Culture; TorontoFellOutGDD (one mention); TepenianUniverseTimeline (two mentions).
- **Sources:** InnerTepeniaGDD `Universal_Location_Methodology/00_RUNBOOK.md` (THE LAW OF NO FORCED FIT); `Universal_Location_Methodology/03_The_Phase_Spine.md` (Phase 6 B); `CLAUDE.md`; `Cities/Founding_Register.md` (Cape Adare); `Universal_Location_Methodology/DEVELOPER_RULINGS_LOG.md` (DR-44).
- **Open:** for founders, Cape Adare is mixed with no dominant national community, an empty slot by ruling (DR-44).

### Law of One Location
- **Category:** method term
- **Also called:** ONE LOCATION law; ONE LOCATION (short form in pass files)
- **Definition:** (Out of fiction) The binding rule that in a ULM, CST or RWBEM pass each location is built on its own terms and never compared with another city: no ranking, no other city as a baseline, and other locations' conclusions are not even read. Relations to other places, such as what flows where, stay legal; the test is to delete the other city's name and see whether the claim survives. Differentiation against sibling locations happens only as a final check on the finished corpus.
- **Status:** Project canon (developer rulings, 2026-09-06).
- **Used in:** InnerTepeniaGDD (ULM; city Pre-Trip sheets, passes, DOCKET); CurrentNovelDocs; DerivationMechanisms; Hybrid-Anthro_Culture.
- **Sources:** InnerTepeniaGDD `Universal_Location_Methodology/00_RUNBOOK.md` (THE LAW OF ONE LOCATION; THE UNIT IS ONE LOCATION); `Universal_Location_Methodology/ULM_Piece_Index.md` §6; `CLAUDE.md`.
- **Open:** It generalizes the earlier rule that Concordia is not a yardstick (§C.8d) and is why DOCKET rows for other passes are left blank. Its scope is ULM, CST and RWBEM only: Concordia's districts keep their own read-before-write differentiation table.
