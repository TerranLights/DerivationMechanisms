### Undercurrent
- **Category:** method term
- **Definition:** (out of fiction) The author's own addition to the Enneagram: an optional second, weaker full profile (type, wing and instinctual subtype) beneath a character's main type, which can be any of the other eight types. It is present and occasionally influential but never load-bearing, and is identified only where evidence shows up, never hunted for. The working balance is about 80% main type to 20% Undercurrent.
- **Status:** Timeline canon (developer-originated concept, 2026-08-11; binding universe-wide). The full mechanics are in the GDD.
- **Used in:** TepenianUniverseTimeline; InnerTepeniaGDD; CurrentNovelDocs (the root seed file, Starlit Engines, The Saga of Maggie Aarden, character sheets); TheCryptographHelixDD; SouthernLights; DerivationMechanisms.
- **Sources:** TepenianUniverseTimeline `Reference/Enneagram_Undercurrents.md`; InnerTepeniaGDD `Worldspace/Enneagram/Undercurrents.md`; DerivationMechanisms `Characters/Enneagram/from InnerTepeniaGDD/Undercurrents.md`.
- **Open:**
  - Scoped to dolls for now, but not limited to them in concept.
  - Eirwyn Cardoss is the first case near parity (about 55/45), against the 80/20 baseline.
  - Other files also use "undercurrent" in its ordinary English sense; check context.

### The Undergrid
- **Category:** place
- **Also called:** Virgo (design label); Concordia Undergrid; Janbogo Undergrid (older layout name); The Depths (older layout name)
- **Definition:** The in-fiction name, recorded as its Official Name, of Concordia's District 12: the underground maintenance and service district of tunnels, heating loops, waste recyclers, structural reinforcement and siligel distribution that keeps the city running unseen, with a self-sufficient, low-profile, practical culture. GDD logistics files also use the name for the underground transit, maintenance and storage network beneath all districts, which holds some housing and can be walked without power, though its lighting needs power.
- **Status:** project canon (GDD district canon); the Timeline mentions it as the Virgo district's name.
- **Used in:** InnerTepeniaGDD; TepenianUniverseTimeline; SouthernLights; CurrentNovelDocs; Outer Tepenia 1 (copied mechanics); TorontoFellOutGDD (imported material); DerivationMechanisms.
- **Sources:** InnerTepeniaGDD `Concordia-City/Districts/District_Canon_Reference.md` (District 12); `Worldspace/City_Logistics.md` (Physical Structure; Internal Transportation); SouthernLights `Worldspace/Environments-Concordia-Districts.md` (12. Virgo); `Worldspace/Core-Systems.md`; TepenianUniverseTimeline `Worldspace/Characters/Dolls/Naizelle d'Edjordoś/README.md`.
- **Open:**
  - **Two senses:** the named district and the citywide under-level network.
  - **Older names:** the preliminary layout calls the district "Janbogo Undergrid (The Depths)", and the Veilkeepers sketch places their archives in the "Janbogo Undergrid"; the hostility file says "Concordia Undergrid".
  - The Deep Level lies below it (own entry).

### Universal Location Methodology
- **Category:** method term
- **Also called:** ULM
- **Definition:** (out of fiction) The author's procedure for developing any single location of any type and scale (a district, city, polity, station, corridor, ruin or network locus) from inputs it cannot generate itself. It runs as twelve Steps (−1 to 10), which contain the eleven Phases (0 to 10, inside Step 4) and the seventeen QA gates (inside Step 7). It was generalized from the Concordia district methodology and is the first and current pass for Inner Tepenia's 38 cities.
- **Status:** working: its README calls it a working draft (since 2026-08-30), partially validated. Many of its rules are developer rulings.
- **Used in:** InnerTepeniaGDD (ULM; Cities, the 38-city run in `Cities/City_Development_Passes/`; Concordia; `CLAUDE.md`); DerivationMechanisms (copy, `Environment/ULM [Universal Location Methodology]`); CurrentNovelDocs (Object-Oriented Location Synthesis; the worldbuilding plan of action); Hybrid-Anthro_Culture (a ULM adapter); TepenianHawaiian (decision notes).
- **Sources:** InnerTepeniaGDD `Worldspace/Locations-and-Levels/Universal_Location_Methodology/README.md`; `00_RUNBOOK.md`; `ULM_At_A_Glance.md`; `ULM_Piece_Index.md`.
- **Open:** the GDD copy stays the working copy; the DerivationMechanisms copy, made 2026-10-29, can drift from it.

### Unmarked Discovery Content
- **Category:** method term
- **Also called:** The Cradle Precedent; Unmarked discovery
- **Definition:** (out of fiction) A binding design law: lore as large, as cross-regional or as tied to the player character's origin as the Cradle network must never be a quest of any kind. It gets no marker, journal or log entry, or XP, and is left in the world for diligent players to piece together through environmental storytelling, incidental dialogue and cross-referencing. ### Upper Earth
- **Category:** place
- **Also called:** Upper-Earth (adjective)
- **Definition:** The setting's term for the whole surface of the planet outside Antarctica, so called because it lies geographically above Antarctica; the name is geographic, not cosmological, and never means orbit or off-world colonies.
