# Glossary: S

### Siligel
- **Category:** robot biology; robot food
- **Definition:** The robot equivalent of food. Its first function is **energy replenishment**, similar to recharging a battery: the robot's engine is the power plant, and siligel is what it draws on. Its second function is **maintenance and repair** of internal systems.
- **Status:**
  - **Project canon:** its role as robot food, and energy first, repair second (author ruling, 2026-09-04).
  - **Working:** its composition and whether it comes in several classes.
- **Used in:** InnerTepeniaGDD (widely; the game's Hardcore Mode treats it mechanically); CurrentNovelDocs (reference material and *Streetside Harmonies*); the Outer Tepenia series; TorontoFellOutGDD; SouthernLights.
- **Sources:** InnerTepeniaGDD `Worldspace/Robot_Biology_and_Culture/Robot_Physiology_and_Cultural_Practices.md`, section "Siligel — Robot Food"; `Game-Mechanics/Core-Mechanics/Hardcore_Mode.md`.
- **Open:**
  - **Classes.** The author raised the idea of several classes of siligel that do different things. It hasn't been ruled on.
  - **Composition.** A research proposal awaits the author's ruling: `Siligel_Composition_Research.md` in the same GDD folder. It explains siligel as fluoride chemistry renewing silicon surfaces, which would also explain why it is lethal to humans. It is not canon.
