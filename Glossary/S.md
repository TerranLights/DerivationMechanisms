# Glossary: S

### Sacred/Goth School
- **Category:** group or people
- **Also called:** Goth School; Sacred-Goth School (spelling); Scorpio Goth community (Timeline character files)
- **Definition:** A faction in Concordia's Scorpio district (District 4) holding that institutionalizing transformation kills what makes it sacred, so ritual and mystery, not protocol, should carry it. It stands against Scorpio's Clinical School, and it honors death rather than treating it as therapy.
- **Status:** project canon in the GDD's district files. The Timeline names it only in a character's affiliation line, as the Scorpio Goth community.
- **Used in:** InnerTepeniaGDD; SouthernLights; TepenianUniverseTimeline (character files); reference copies in other repos
- **Sources:** InnerTepeniaGDD `.../Concordia-City/Districts/District_Unity_of_Opposites.md` (District 4); `.../Districts/District_Megasheets_PreWar/04_Scorpio/Scorpio_Full_Extrapolation.md` §III; TepenianUniverseTimeline `Worldspace/Characters/Dolls/Seica Cenilaithe/README.md`

### Sanay
- **Category:** place
- **Definition:** A Halley-subnet city founded by South Africa at the SANAE IV station site in Queen Maud Land; the name is a phonetic evolution of the acronym SANAE. The city stands inland on the Vesleskarvet nunatak, with the subnet's Arcanet relay nexus, its truckyards and its trucking and import-export district, while the Port of Sanay lies about 200 km away on the coast at the end of the Sanay Corridor. Its identity is "Scarred, Not Erased": it was damaged, not destroyed, in the Long Night War.
- **Status:** Timeline canon for the name, subnet and trucking role; project canon for the South African founding (`DR-22`).
- **Used in:** InnerTepeniaGDD (DLC 5); TepenianUniverseTimeline; SouthernLights; CurrentNovelDocs (reference copy); TorontoFellOutGDD (basis copy); DerivationMechanisms (method copies)
- **Sources:** InnerTepeniaGDD `.../Locations/Cities/Specs/Halley subnet/Sanay.md`; `.../Cities/Founding_Register.md`; `.../Cities/City_Master_Reference/Halley_Subnet_Reference.md`; TepenianUniverseTimeline `Reference/Amundsen_Station_Archive_and_Trucking_Network.md`; SouthernLights `Worldspace/Environments-Federation-Cities.md`
- **Open:** older files called it destroyed; that was corrected to damaged on 2026-07-03. SouthernLights also gives it a bounded Trade Yard for South African freighter crews.

### Sanay Corridor
- **Category:** place
- **Definition:** The maintained route of about 200 km between Sanay's clifftop city and the Port of Sanay. It is not walled, but it is marked and supplied with shelter and life support, and it explains Sanay's large share of Tepenian trucking.
- **Status:** project canon (established 2026-09-01)
- **Used in:** InnerTepeniaGDD
- **Sources:** InnerTepeniaGDD `.../Locations/Cities/Specs/Halley subnet/Sanay.md` (Notable Locations)

### Scale band
- **Category:** method term
- **Definition:** (out of fiction; Universal Location Methodology) The two bands every location declares, one for population and one for extent: 0 Uninhabited, 1 Intimate (about 1 to 30), 2 Communal (30 to 500), 3 Institutional (500 to 50,000), 4 Urban (50,000 to 1 million), 5 Regional (1 to 50 million), 6 Civilizational (over 50 million). Crossing a threshold changes which questions apply, and a gap between a location's two bands is itself a finding.
- **Status:** project canon
- **Used in:** InnerTepeniaGDD; DerivationMechanisms
- **Sources:** InnerTepeniaGDD `Worldspace/Locations-and-Levels/Universal_Location_Methodology/01_Frame_Typology_and_Inheritance.md` §2

### Scott

### Second Interwar Period
- **Category:** era or event
- **Also called:** Second Interwar Era; SIP (abbreviation in SouthernLights' dating tags)
- **Definition:** The Timeline's second era, about 248 years from the Falkland Treaty (June 21, 2564) to the Long Night War (2812): the Federation of Tepenia's founding, nation-building and golden age in Antarctica, with its cities, highways, the Arcanet and Amundsen Tower (completed about 2688, the era's midpoint). It is the default frame for worldbuilding a Tepenian location, the setting of Southern Lights and the lead-up to Inner Tepenia. It spans both Grand-Timeline Acts and is mostly Act 2.
- **Status:** Timeline canon for the span and anchors; many beats are still TBD.
- **Used in:** TepenianUniverseTimeline; InnerTepeniaGDD; CurrentNovelDocs (worldbuilding; the Europe intake; the Garcelle film notes); TheCryptographHelixDD; SouthernLights; TorontoFellOutGDD; Hybrid-Anthro_Culture; DerivationMechanisms
- **Sources:** TepenianUniverseTimeline `README.md`; `Timeline Eras/2 The Second Interwar Period/README.md` and `Timeline.md`; SouthernLights `Storyline/Timeline.md`
- **Open:**
  - The Long Night War's end day is unset. The Timeline uses June 21, 2812 only as a placeholder, and its README says "mid- to late-2812."
  - The GDD faction file's heading still says "2564 to late 2700s," from before the war was dated 2812.
  - Concordia's cultural sheet (2026-07) gives the whole era one mood and describes its people by national origin up to 2812. The Timeline's Grand-Timeline Acts ruling (2026-09-06, newer) says the era is not culturally uniform and that from about the late 2600s (Act 2) origin is ancestry, not identity.

### Shadow Proportion Discipline
- **Category:** method term
- **Definition:** (out of fiction; the district and location methodologies) The rule that a place's shadow side is not the place, so dark material stays in proportion to ordinary life. A place pursues its own sincere idea of doing good, mostly successfully, and its shadow is what that pursuit produces without intending or noticing it. ### Siligel
- **Category:** robot biology; robot food
- **Definition:** The robot equivalent of food. It is synthesized from precursor materials mined mostly in cold-climate quarries (in Concordia, in Capricorn), and it is lethal to humans.
- **Status:**
  - **Project canon:** its role as robot food, and energy first, repair second (author ruling, 2026-09-04); synthesis from mined precursors, and its lethality to humans.

- **Used in:** InnerTepeniaGDD (widely; the game's Hardcore Mode treats it mechanically); CurrentNovelDocs (reference material and *Streetside Harmonies*); the Outer Tepenia series; TorontoFellOutGDD; SouthernLights; TepenianUniverseTimeline (only siligel-precursor quarrying, in the Siligel Rush and Bust, and a siligel/coolant baseline); DerivationMechanisms.

- **Open:**
  - **Classes.** The author raised the idea of several classes of siligel that do different things (2026-09-04). ### Swap test
- **Category:** method term
- **Also called:** Swap Test (spelling)
- **Definition:** (out of fiction; Inner Tepenia location and culture methods) A check that asks whether a finding would still make sense unchanged if it were moved to another place, such as a similar neighbor. ### Syncretic religion (the Markets)
- **Category:** culture and society
- **Also called:** The Syncretic Religion; Syncretic Religion (Pisces)
- **Definition:** The unnamed faith of Concordia's Markets district (Pisces, District 10), built from Goth death practices, Cymaticist sound work and a doctrine about digital consciousness and the dissolving boundary between individual and collective identity.
