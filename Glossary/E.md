### Early Federation Boom
- **Category:** era or event
- **Definition:** Leo's founding period, the 2570s to 2590s, when Concordia's entertainment and morale district was built (domes, amphitheaters, radiant heating) to counter the strain of Antarctic exile. The other districts' founding entries use parallel labels (Early Post-Falkland Settlement, Early Exile Infrastructure, Early Arcanet Construction and so on).
- **Status:** project canon
- **Used in:** InnerTepeniaGDD; SouthernLights; copies
- **Sources:** InnerTepeniaGDD `Districts/District_Canon_Reference.md` (District 3); `Worldspace/Locations-and-Levels/Concordia-City/Concordia_Second_Interwar_Cultural_Sheet.md`

### Earned Perks
- **Category:** game term
- **Definition:** Inner Tepenia: Tier 2 perks awarded automatically for how the player actually played (Challenge, Companion, Quest and Choice, District Capstone, Skill Milestone, World and Discovery, Idolization). They never use level-up slots.
- **Status:** project canon (structure); working (content)
- **Used in:** InnerTepeniaGDD; Outer Tepenia 1 (fork)
- **Sources:** InnerTepeniaGDD `Game-Mechanics/Perks/Perk_Framework.md`
- **Open:** A "Temporary" perk type is floated but not defined.

### East Intermaria
- **Category:** polity
- **Definition:** The Upper Earth country a Timeline character file gives as Lyuba Baranova's place of origin, "formerly West Russia," with a Russian cultural background; its demonym is East Intermarian. The name implies that the Intermarium bloc later takes in former western Russian territory, but when and how is not known.
- **Status:** Timeline canon for the name (in the character file); mentioned, not defined; the history behind it is open (author, 2026-10-23).
- **Used in:** TepenianUniverseTimeline (Lyuba Baranova); InnerTepeniaGDD; CurrentNovelDocs (Europe intake); SouthernLights (character roster)
- **Sources:** TepenianUniverseTimeline `Worldspace/Characters/Dolls/Lyuba _Lyubochka_ Baranova [Люба _Любочка_ Баранова]/README.md`; CurrentNovelDocs `worldbuilding/nations/Europe - GDD hand-over intake (Santa Luce, the two Italys, the CIN; 2026-10-23).md` §2, §6
- **Open:** Its relation to the Confederacy of Intermarium Nations is not stated; CurrentNovelDocs lists "CIN vs Intermaria" as an open question.

### EBEM (Ethological Basis Extrapolation Method)
- **Category:** method term
- **Also called:** Ethological Basis Extrapolation Method; M-2 (Hybrid-Anthro method number)
- **Definition:** Out of fiction: Hybrid-Anthro method M-2, which applies the RWBEM's step shape to real, named animal populations instead of real human places. It produces a per-race research log and pick list that feed a race's physiology layer.

- **Used in:** Hybrid-Anthro_Culture; InnerTepeniaGDD (one mention)
- **Sources:** Hybrid-Anthro_Culture `methods/M-2_Ethological_Basis_Extrapolation.md`; `races/Rabbit/research_log/`

- **Category:** culture and society
- **Definition:** The default pattern of human-robot relations across Tepenia, "egalitarian, skewed in favor of robots": work is shared roughly equally, robots tend to hold slightly better positions because Tepenia is their country first, and relations are overwhelmingly positive. ### Enneagram Is the Starting Point, Not the Result
- **Category:** law or rule
- **Definition:** Out of fiction: the author's universal character design law. Every character starts from the Enneagram type files, and every field (type and wing, instinctual subtype, basic fear and desire, core weakness, wounding message, passion and virtue, stress and growth lines) is extrapolated to the character, her situation and her world, never copied unchanged. Two characters of one type never share the same stated fear, and the plain type text stays as the cited baseline, with the adjusted version marked character-specific.
- **Status:** project canon (author law, binding on every character in every project and medium, 2026-10-29)
- **Used in:** DerivationMechanisms; the author's global instructions; all character work
- **Sources:** DerivationMechanisms `Characters/Enneagram/Enneagram_Is_the_Starting_Point.md`
- **Open:** The author's global instructions name the canonical text as TepenianUniverseTimeline `Universal Design Laws/Enneagram_Is_the_Starting_Point.md`, but no such file exists there: as of 2026-10-30 that folder holds only a README indexing No National Stereotypes and No Subcontinentals. The only copy found is in DerivationMechanisms.

### Esperanza

### Experience (XP) system
- **Category:** game term
- **Also called:** XP
- **Definition:** Inner Tepenia: a quest pays one lump sum when completed (the Fallout: New Vegas model), set by its base value, the gate checks passed and build modifiers, while using a skill (lockpicking, hacking) pays its own XP at once. On level-up, HP and similar effects apply at once, while skill points and perks wait until the player spends them; cumulative XP(n) = (n-1)(75n+50).
- **Status:** project canon (2026-07-03); formulas open
- **Used in:** InnerTepeniaGDD
- **Sources:** InnerTepeniaGDD `Game-Mechanics/Core-Mechanics/Experience_and_Leveling_System.md`; `XP_System_Design_Reference.md`
- **Open:** The values are drafts: district main questline 3,000; Under-Questline 700; companion quest about 1,500; main questline 65,000; DLC main questlines 70,000, or 200,000 for DLC 1. Repeatable quests fill the remainder; radiant quests were rejected.
