### Palmer City
- **Category:** place
- **Definition:** Tepenia's first city and the hub of the Palmer subnet, on the Palmer Station site on Anvers Island. It was settled on June 21, 2564, the day the Falkland Treaty was signed, by exiled robots, their human partners and human supporters, united by their relationship to robots rather than by nationality. Pre-war Tepenia's cultural and entertainment capital and neutral ground ("built purely for joy"; jazz, casinos, Upper Earth tourism), likened to Las Vegas, Atlantic City, New Orleans and St. Petersburg in one, it was the site of the killing (about 2762) that set off the Long Night War, and was destroyed in that war (2812).
- **Status:** Timeline canon.
- **Used in:** TepenianUniverseTimeline; InnerTepeniaGDD (widely; DLC 3); SouthernLights; CurrentNovelDocs (including the doll-music extraction); TorontoFellOutGDD (basis copy); DerivationMechanisms.
- **Sources:** TepenianUniverseTimeline `Reference/World_History_Reference.md` (city table; Machu Picchu Border & Customs Authority); `Timeline Eras/2 The Second Interwar Period/Timeline.md` (Set-Up, Break into Three, END); `Worldspace/Locations/README.md`; InnerTepeniaGDD `Worldspace/Locations-and-Levels/Outside-World/Tepenian-Federation/Locations/Cities/Specs/Palmer subnet/Palmer_City.md`; `Cities/Founding_Register.md` (DR-16); SouthernLights `Worldspace/Locations.md`; `Worldspace/Environments-Federation-Cities.md`.
- **Open:** it is the cultural capital, never the political one (that was Fort McMurdo). Its demonym is Palmerian. Song prompts (Shenzva, Villena) call it a neutral city or neutral zone with Upper Earth, which agrees with the GDD.

### Palmer subnet
- **Category:** place
- **Also called:** "American" (nickname)
- **Definition:** The Arcanet subnet of the Antarctic Peninsula, the South Shetlands and the South Orkneys, hubbed at Palmer City and nicknamed "American"; its broad-scale culture label is Latin-American. Its eight cities are Palmer City, Puerto Abrigo, Rothera, Esperanza, Marambio, Contrapunto, Pergamino and Signy (peripheral, with weak connectivity). It is the region of Inner Tepenia's DLC 3 and the corridor through which arrivals from Upper Earth came via Ushuaia; only Signy came through 2812 undamaged.
- **Status:** Timeline canon.
- **Used in:** TepenianUniverseTimeline; InnerTepeniaGDD; SouthernLights; CurrentNovelDocs (reference copy); TorontoFellOutGDD (basis copy); DerivationMechanisms.
- **Sources:** TepenianUniverseTimeline `Worldspace/Locations/README.md`; `Reference/World_History_Reference.md` (Machu Picchu Border & Customs Authority); InnerTepeniaGDD `Worldspace/Locations-and-Levels/Outside-World/Tepenian-Federation/Locations/Cities/Overview.md`; `Cities/City_Master_Reference/Palmer_Subnet_Reference.md`; `Locations/Per-Subnet_Overview_Reference_Data.md`; `Locations/Per-Subnet_Metacategories.md`; SouthernLights `Worldspace/Environments-Federation-Cities.md`.
- **Open:**
  - **City names.** The GDD renamed three cities on 2026-10-03: Juan Carlos to Pergamino, Port Lockroy to Puerto Abrigo, Sejong to Contrapunto. The Timeline's location list, SouthernLights and Background-Lore still use the old names; the Timeline's Second Interwar file already uses Pergamino and Puerto Abrigo.

### Parallel economies
- **Category:** culture and society
- **Definition:** A draft survey's term for anything in Concordia that works as an economy beside the formal currency, judged by four tests: a scarce desired resource, real production and circulation, a way to value or exchange it, and structural power effects. Candidates include repair and parts, research access, political influence, attention, trust and vouching, and devotion and pilgrimage; the black market is the confirmed case.
- **Status:** working (research draft, not canon).
- **Used in:** InnerTepeniaGDD.
- **Sources:** InnerTepeniaGDD `Worldspace/Parallel_Economies_Survey.md`.
- **Open:** its energy-or-charge currency baseline (§1 and §5) contradicts DR-3 and the author's ruling that the currency is unnamed and unsettled (2026-10-30); marked for correction (see National currency (Tepenia)).

### Personality Module
- **Category:** robot biology
- **Definition:** The psychological seed set when a robot is fabricated: an underlying weighting of concepts that determines her value tendencies, roughly like an Enneagram specification, not a finished personality. It is separate from her Build and her Language Module. ### Primary, Significant and Notable tiers
- **Category:** method term
- **Also called:** Primary tier; Significant tier; Notable tier; Tier 1/2/3; T1/T2/T3
- **Definition:** Out of fiction. The three national-origin tiers in a Tepenian city's human population. Primary is the dominant community that shapes the city's main language and identity; Significant are distinct, visible communities with a meaningful presence in the city's culture; Notable are present and recognizable but only background texture. Nations are ranked by Gini-adjusted effective population, split at the largest ratio gaps, and weighted 8, 3 and 1 shares per nation; the tiers are used in the census, the city specs and the Neo-Races catalogs.
- **Status:** project canon (demographic method).
- **Used in:** InnerTepeniaGDD (census; specs; local cultures; culture template; ULM; Neo-Races); CurrentNovelDocs (reference copy); SouthernLights; TorontoFellOutGDD; DerivationMechanisms.
- **Sources:** InnerTepeniaGDD `Worldspace/Locations-and-Levels/Outside-World/Tepenian-Federation/Locations/Cities/Official_Population_Census.md` (Section A tier definitions); `Cities/Upper_Earth_Immigration_Composition.md` (The tier system); `Universal_Location_Methodology/DEVELOPER_RULINGS_LOG.md` (DR-12, DR-19); `Neo-Races-and-Cultures/_Method/City_Catalog_Template.md`.
- **Open:**
  - DR-12: for location work the tiers and city totals are the information, and no finding may rest on a difference inside one tier. DR-19: the ±3 window gates the Notable tier only. The Notable tier is optional in Neo-Races Phase 1c, and Byrd's and Palmer City's full Notable rosters are deferred.
  - Whether the tiers are also in-world or design-only is not established.
  - Not to be confused with the ULM's input tiers (Tier 0 to 3) or the Division of Industry's three-tier split. Background-Lore often contrasts a founding nation "diluted to Notable tier" with a later Primary majority.

### Puerto Abrigo
- **Category:** place
- **Also called:** Port Lockroy (former name, until 2026-10-03)
- **Definition:** A Palmer-subnet city on Goudier Island, beside the Port Lockroy harbor; the name is Spanish for "sheltered harbor," and it was founded by Chile. Its 1944 Operation Tabarin buildings, later a museum, were absorbed into the exile city as a living palimpsest, and it became the Peninsula's postal hub ("Rothera moves the materials; Puerto Abrigo moves the words"), with a communal stone wall that grows panel by panel. It was damaged but spared in 2812, as too strategically minor to be a priority target.
- **Status:** Timeline canon for the city under its old name, Port Lockroy. The new name is project canon (GDD DR-40, 2026-10-03); the founders are project canon (DR-34).
- **Used in:** InnerTepeniaGDD; TepenianUniverseTimeline; DerivationMechanisms (method copies); SouthernLights, CurrentNovelDocs and TorontoFellOutGDD (only as Port Lockroy).
- **Sources:** InnerTepeniaGDD `Worldspace/Locations-and-Levels/Outside-World/Tepenian-Federation/Locations/Cities/Specs/Palmer subnet/Puerto_Abrigo.md`; `Cities/City_Renames_Alias_Table_2026-10-03.md` §7; `Cities/Founding_Register.md`; `Cities/City_Vision_Notes/Puerto_Abrigo.md`; TepenianUniverseTimeline `Worldspace/Locations/README.md`; `Reference/Amundsen_Station_Archive_and_Trucking_Network.md`; SouthernLights `Worldspace/Environments-Federation-Cities.md` (Port Lockroy).
- **Open:**
  - **Rename.** Renamed from Port Lockroy on 2026-10-03 (GDD DR-40); the real base, harbor and bay keep the name Port Lockroy. The Timeline's location list, SouthernLights, CurrentNovelDocs and the GDD's megasheets and Background-Lore still use Port Lockroy; the Timeline's Second Interwar Timeline already uses Puerto Abrigo.
  - **Population.** The 2026-09-02 Palmer reference gives Census I as 128,887, but `Official_Population_Census.md` reduced it on 2026-09-05 (Census II 691). Background-Lore assumes the older, larger, UK-founded city.

  - Its earlier claim to be the oldest continuously occupied site was withdrawn (DR-28). Its demonym is open; the Neo-Races catalog and the Port Lockroy megasheet use the working name "Lockroyan," which belongs to the former name.
