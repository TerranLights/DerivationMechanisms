### Canon Gap Resolution Method
- **Category:** method term
- **Also called:** CGRM
- **Definition:** (Out of fiction) The InnerTepeniaGDD's system for acquiring canon that does not exist yet, turning an unknown into an answer with recorded provenance; it is separate from the synthesis methods that consume canon (ULM, district passes, megasheets) and reaches them only through shared canon. It triages gaps, picks one of seven acquisition paths, deposits results in the right tier, runs verification gates and logs everything; gaps get project-wide IDs (CGRM-001 and on), and the facts it adds carry tags like [CGRM date · Path n · source]. Its laws: A, an open gap is not a defect; B, where a fact lands matters as much as whether it is true; C, the method is not its test cases; D to G govern bulk mode.
- **Status:** project canon (built 2026-08-31)
- **Used in:** InnerTepeniaGDD (CGRM; city Overview, Inspirational-Influences, Division_of_Industry; ULM); DerivationMechanisms
- **Sources:** InnerTepeniaGDD `Worldspace/Canon_Gap_Resolution_Method/00_RUNBOOK.md` (opening definition; Laws A to C), `Worldspace/Canon_Gap_Resolution_Method/00_Design_Proposal.md` and `Worldspace/Canon_Gap_Resolution_Method/02_Acquisition_Paths.md`
- **Open:** the runbook still calls it "partially grounded, not yet run," but `Resolution_Log.md` records closed gaps (CGRM-019, the core/outer city model; CGRM-015, no inspiration picks for highways).

### Cape Adare
- **Category:** place
- **Definition:** A Janbogo-subnet city on the Ross Sea coast at Cape Adare, beside a huge Adélie penguin rookery and Borchgrevink's 1899 hut, the oldest human structure on the continent. Its founding is ruled mixed, with no single dominant national community (`DR-44`); its identity is "firstness," with St. Carsten as its founding Saint, and it is a vast city with a small-town feel. - **Status:** Timeline canon (name, subnet); project canon for the founders (`DR-44`)

### Census I and Census II
- **Category:** unit or measure
- **Also called:** Census I; Census II
- **Definition:** The Tepenian Federation's two official population counts. Census I is the pre-orbital count, taken after the founding period and long-run immigration equilibrium and before Amundsen Tower was built: each city's peak surface population (32,026,600 in all: 15,623,523 humans and 16,403,077 robots), which design work uses everywhere because it is the largest size each city must hold. Census II is the Orbital Era snapshot taken just before the Long Night War, after decades of migration to orbit through the Tower: the same people relocated rather than born or dead, with about 21.9 million on the surface across 34 cities and about 10.1 million in orbit; it is pre-war and cannot record war outcomes, and there is no Census III.
- **Status:** project canon (`DR-4`; the census was rescaled by `DR-68` on 2026-10-28, with national totals unchanged). The Timeline notes the boundary between the two.
- **Used in:** InnerTepeniaGDD (widely: cities, ULM, Division_of_Industry); TepenianUniverseTimeline (Second Interwar README); CurrentNovelDocs (reference copy); SouthernLights; DerivationMechanisms
- **Sources:** InnerTepeniaGDD `Worldspace/Locations-and-Levels/Outside-World/Tepenian-Federation/Locations/Cities/Official_Population_Census.md` (Sections B and C) and `Worldspace/Locations-and-Levels/Universal_Location_Methodology/DEVELOPER_RULINGS_LOG.md` (`DR-4`, `DR-16`, `DR-23`, `DR-69`); TepenianUniverseTimeline `Timeline Eras/2 The Second Interwar Period/README.md`
- **Open:**
  - **Boundary.** The Timeline places the Census I to II boundary at the Tower's completion (~2688); the census file says Census I predates the Tower's construction.
  - **Rulings.** `DR-4` chose Census I as the governing figure, overriding a unanimous T8 reader recommendation for Census II; `DR-16` makes the move from I to II history, not an input to a ULM pass; `DR-23` freezes Census II's national total until the cities finish the ULM; `DR-69` puts the space workforce only in Census II.
  - Counts of locations differ (38 cities and entities in the GDD; 37 locations in SouthernLights). Some files still carry pre-`DR-68` figures, and Background-Lore city ranks ("rank 27th of ~32") were found stale and fixed.
  - SouthernLights flags its war-loss total (~2,874,000) as a likely undercount. The census file's sections were relettered (old II and III became B and C).

### Cultural Synthesis Techniques
- **Category:** method term
- **Also called:** CST
- **Definition:** (Out of fiction) The GDD's generative toolkit for building the culture of any specific place (Concordia's districts, the outer cities, DLC locations, places in future projects): eighteen named techniques plus one extension, each a question with a structure and a divergence table, never a result to copy. The RWBEM supplies the raw material and the CST supplies the operations; it is governed by Characteristic Plausibility and the Anti-convergence rule, and its techniques include the Bounded Personal Franchise, the Surviving Witness, the Negative Image, Borrowed Form, the Unrecognized Instrument, the Zodiac Lens and the Composition Merge.
- **Status:** project canon (an adopted toolkit, in use). By ruling it runs across all cities only after every city's ULM pass is finished (`DR-14`, `DR-23`); the per-city CST run is in progress.
- **Used in:** InnerTepeniaGDD (CST; cities; ULM); DerivationMechanisms; CurrentNovelDocs; Hybrid-Anthro_Culture (a CST adapter)
- **Sources:** InnerTepeniaGDD `Worldspace/Locations-and-Levels/Cultural_Synthesis_Techniques.md`, `Worldspace/Locations-and-Levels/Universal_Location_Methodology/Disciplines/Cultural_Synthesis_Techniques.md` (ULM reading) and `Worldspace/Locations-and-Levels/Universal_Location_Methodology/CST_Progress.md`; DerivationMechanisms `Environment/CST [Cultural Synthesis Techniques]/`

### Cymatics Reverence
- **Category:** culture and society
- **Also called:** Cymaticism; Cymaticist reverence; Cymatics
- **Definition:** A mostly robot religion that reveres sound and vibration, and so music, rooted in robots' wider-than-human range of hearing and vibration sense. Its larger claim is that controlled vibration of spacetime itself could in principle move planets; it is diffuse, with no home city, and humans may join. Its adherents are the Cymaticists.
- **Status:** working: a placeholder name in early development; whether its central claim is pursued as science or held as a sacred, non-literal belief is deliberately undecided. SouthernLights treats its existence as canon.
- **Used in:** InnerTepeniaGDD (Robot_Religions; Ariun Nuur and Mirny robot culture; Concordia's Leo district; perks); CurrentNovelDocs; SouthernLights; Outer Tepenia series (perk, skill and damage-type names); TorontoFellOutGDD; DerivationMechanisms
