### Random variation passes
- **Category:** method term
- **Definition:** Out of fiction. The Inner Tepenia census's final calibration: each city's human population was cut by a random 1 to 5 percent and its robot population moved up or down by a random 1 to 2 percent, so the figures look observed rather than calculated.
- **Status:** project canon.
- **Used in:** InnerTepeniaGDD (cities); DerivationMechanisms.
- **Sources:** InnerTepeniaGDD `Worldspace/Locations-and-Levels/Outside-World/Tepenian-Federation/Locations/Cities/Upper_Earth_Immigration_Composition.md` (The random variation passes); `Universal_Location_Methodology/DEVELOPER_RULINGS_LOG.md` (DR-12).
- **Open:** DR-12: the adjustments carry no meaning, and no finding may rest on them.

### Rapid Intake
- **Category:** method term
- **Also called:** seed-to-profile process
- **Definition:** Out of fiction. A deliberately light alternative to the character pipeline for standing up hundreds or thousands of minor characters: copy a folder template, fill in a seed template (TBD is a valid answer), and transfer each seed field straight into the profile with no inference.
- **Status:** project canon.
- **Used in:** TepenianUniverseTimeline; DerivationMechanisms; InnerTepeniaGDD.
- **Sources:** DerivationMechanisms `Characters/Character_Development_Methodology/from TepenianUniverseTimeline/Rapid_Intake/seed-to-README-process.md`; `seed-template-init.txt`; `z-template/`.

### Rastra
- **Category:** technology
- **Also called:** las Arrastradoras (Belgrano's official term)
- **Definition:** Tepenia's standard heavy overland vehicle and the most common one in the country: a large, enclosed, heated cabin on a tracked chassis, descended from the Soviet Kharkovchanka. Belgrano built the first native haulers, las Arrastradoras, for the founding-era expedition to Byrd; Neumayer redesigned the vehicle for distance; and over generations non-Spanish-speaking cities wore the name down to "Rastra." Rastras carried the crews who built the Arcanet, remain the means of continental travel (convoy haulers, Concordia's inter-district shuttles, the Frostlanders' communally owned shelters), and are now built at Byrd; the name was later reused for small spacecraft such as tugs and shuttles.
- **Status:** Timeline canon for the origin and name. Project canon for the lineage and role; a later-era cruise speed of about 45 km/h was ruled (DR-63, 2026-10-28). Early-era speed, range, capacity and fleet details are open.
- **Used in:** InnerTepeniaGDD (widely; DLC 1, where Michelle Stanton teaches the player to repair one); TepenianUniverseTimeline; CurrentNovelDocs (doll-music extraction); SouthernLights; TorontoFellOutGDD; DerivationMechanisms.
- **Sources:** TepenianUniverseTimeline `Reference/Amundsen_Station_Archive_and_Trucking_Network.md`; InnerTepeniaGDD `Worldspace/Locations-and-Levels/Outside-World/Tepenian-Federation/Locations/Infrastructure/Rastra_Mechanics.md`; `Worldspace/Locations-and-Levels/Outside-World/Tepenian-Federation/Locations/Cities/Specs/Byrd subnet/Byrd.md`; `Worldspace/City_Logistics.md`; `General-Overview-Notes/aspects_of_consideration.md` (Rastras); `Storyline/DLC_Overview.md`; SouthernLights `Worldspace/Core-Systems.md`.
- **Open:**
  - **Wheels or tracks.** TepenianUniverseTimeline `Worldspace/Characters/Dolls/Michelle Stanton/README.md` describes a Rastra as an 8-wheeled vehicle; other files give it a tracked chassis.
  - **Origin.** The early GDD overview note calls Rastras "post-Soviet-style" vehicles used to build the Arcanet, and older files say "Kharkovchanka convoy"; the later Belgrano origin (2026-07-03) traces them to the Byrd Expedition. The cross-city patterns file credits Neumayer with the original design, uncredited, while Belgrano built them.
  - The English version of the song "A Todo Motor" translates Rastra as "truck."

### The Rationing of Grief
- **Category:** era or event
- **Definition:** Libra's emergency directive of about 2818 capping how long districts could fund bereavement care ("productive grief timelines"). Cancer filed an exemption through Libra's own process and kept working in good faith, but Libra's overloaded automatic compliance system flagged it as noncompliant and cut its allocation before anyone read the request; the cut was never revisited.
- **Status:** Timeline canon (corrected 2026-08-07).
- **Used in:** TepenianUniverseTimeline; InnerTepeniaGDD; SouthernLights; CurrentNovelDocs; TorontoFellOutGDD; copies in other repos.
- **Sources:** TepenianUniverseTimeline `Timeline Eras/2 The Second Interwar Period/Timeline.md`; InnerTepeniaGDD `Worldspace/Locations-and-Levels/Concordia-City/Districts/District_Canon_Reference.md` (District 1); `Districts/Deep_Dives/01b_Cancer_Rationing_of_Grief_Alternatives.md`.
- **Open:** earlier versions said Cancer simply refused the directive and was punished; both the Timeline and the GDD corrected this on 2026-08-07, but SouthernLights `Storyline/Timeline.md` (2026-07-10) still has the older version.

### READER files (climate READER)
- **Category:** method term
- **Definition:** Out of fiction. Inner Tepenia's per-city climate files giving monthly mean temperatures from the British Antarctic Survey's READER data set (WMO 1991 to 2020 normals), each with its source and data period. They are the authoritative climate input for ULM Phases 1 and 3.
- **Status:** project canon (reference data).
- **Used in:** InnerTepeniaGDD (real-world reference data; city specs; City Master Reference; passes; research logs; ULM).
- **Sources:** InnerTepeniaGDD `Worldspace/Locations-and-Levels/Outside-World/Tepenian-Federation/Locations/Cities/City_Master_Reference/README.md` (Reference/Real-World table); `Reference/Real-World/Climate Data/READER/`.
- **Open:** most files are named for the real station, not the city (`Aboa.md` is Santa Luce; `Contrapunto.md` is King Sejong), so a search by city name can wrongly find nothing. Most files are temperature only. The folder was wrongly declared empty for two months because it holds only a subfolder.

### Reader/deriver isolation
- **Category:** method term
- **Definition:** Out of fiction. ULM technique §C.2 for learning where facts are without learning what they say: isolated subagent readers fetch and return material under a strict return contract, so the deriving session is not contaminated.
- **Status:** project canon.
- **Used in:** InnerTepeniaGDD; DerivationMechanisms.
- **Sources:** InnerTepeniaGDD `Worldspace/Locations-and-Levels/Universal_Location_Methodology/00_RUNBOOK.md` §C.2.
- **Open:** warm passes retrieve a named file at a named range instead (DR-6).

### Readiness Check
- **Category:** method term
- **Definition:** Out of fiction. ULM Step 10, run before a pass is declared complete or handed off: verify, do not assert. It checks the contamination surface for the next subject, path and structure integrity, and record integrity (claims match files; findings are implemented).
- **Status:** project canon.
- **Used in:** InnerTepeniaGDD; DerivationMechanisms.
- **Sources:** InnerTepeniaGDD `Worldspace/Locations-and-Levels/Universal_Location_Methodology/00_RUNBOOK.md` (Step 10); `Universal_Location_Methodology/Stepwise_Execution/01_Spine/S12_Step_10_THE_READINESS_CHECK.md`.

### Rebirth Ritual
- **Category:** game term
- **Definition:** In Inner Tepenia, the re-spec method of Scorpio (the Veil): extremely risky and able to advance Identity Fragmentation by several states at once. ### Recognition Cascade
- **Category:** culture and society
- **Definition:** The chain of uncredited labor in Concordia: Cancer, Taurus and Leo undervalue Aries' dangerous power work, and Aries in turn undervalues Virgo's maintenance work, which has no one further down to pass it to. It is an instance of the game's most repeated civic theme, consequential work without visible credit; the revised main quest makes it a resolvable player choice.
- **Status:** project canon (a cross-reference finding); its use in the main quest is tentative.
- **Used in:** InnerTepeniaGDD; SouthernLights; copies in other repos.

### Red Spiral
- **Category:** organization
- **Also called:** Red Nebula (former name)
- **Definition:** Tepenia's medical organization of first responders, doctors and other medics, in the line of the Red Cross and Red Crescent; the author describes it as the universe's counterpart to Fallout: New Vegas's Followers of the Apocalypse. A non-geographical faction headquartered in Concordia's Cancer (medical) district and operating across all districts, it lives by codes of duty built on the Hippocratic Oath, first aid and medical triage. The medic Ayako Hayashi is an esteemed member, not its leader.
- **Status:** project canon (the developer's medical concept; author ruling, 2026-10-30). There is no full design document yet, and its leader is not chosen. The Timeline mentions it only in character files.
- **Used in:** InnerTepeniaGDD; TepenianUniverseTimeline (character files); CurrentNovelDocs (doll-music extraction); SouthernLights; Outer Tepenia; TorontoFellOutGDD.
- **Sources:** author ruling, 2026-10-30; InnerTepeniaGDD `General-Overview-Notes/aspects_of_consideration.md` (The Red Spiral); `Worldspace/Characters/Dolls/Still-Present_-_In-Game/recruitable/Ayako Hayashi/README.md`; TepenianUniverseTimeline `Worldspace/Characters/Dolls/Ayako Hayashi/README.md`; SouthernLights `Worldspace/Characters.md`.
- **Open:**
  - InnerTepeniaGDD `Storyline/Endings/Secret-Endings/Faction_Devotion_Endings.md` (FD-2) still frames it as a revolutionary political faction that holds Concordia's governance broken beyond reform and wants to replace it. The author confirmed the medical reading on 2026-10-30.
  - Its logo files were once named "Red Nebula" (GDD `DONE.md`).
  - Whether Ayako Hayashi founded it is uncertain. Her song gives her a different role: helping robots find purpose through art and philosophy.
