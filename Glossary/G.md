### Gel brain
- **Category:** robot biology
- **Definition:** A robot's actual brain: a real, localized organ of malleable "nano-architecture" gel, visually patterned on the film *Ex Machina*, whose data is always in motion rather than fixing one self. It cannot be cleanly read out, copied or backed up, so uploading a robot mind is as unrealistic as uploading a human one, and irreparable damage to it (or to the energy source) is death. Its "glowy-blue" color is a fixed physical consequence of its material, not a personal trait.
- **Status:** Timeline canon (Robot Universals Part I, moved to DerivationMechanisms 2026-10-30); project canon in the GDD (confirmations 2026-07-09 and 2026-08-07).
- **Used in:** TepenianUniverseTimeline (summary); InnerTepeniaGDD; CurrentNovelDocs (Streetside Harmonies; reference); SouthernLights; DerivationMechanisms; Hybrid-Anthro_Culture
- **Sources:** DerivationMechanisms `Characters/Robot-specific/Robot_Universals/1_-_Part_I_Ground-Level_Fundamentals/02_The_Gel_Brain/README.md`; InnerTepeniaGDD `Worldspace/Robot_Biology_and_Culture/Robot_Physiology_and_Cultural_Practices.md` ("Physiology Basics"); TepenianUniverseTimeline `Reference/Robot_Universals_Summary.md`; CurrentNovelDocs `worldbuilding/Object_Oriented_Location_Synthesis/04_Handwave_Register.md` (H-1)
- **Open:** How it yields full consciousness is the one accepted handwave (H-1). GDD `Worldspace/Characters/Dolls/Doll_Visual_Design_Canon.md` adds that a gel brain is configured for the build's function and can wear out, degrading memory, processing and eventually personality. The siligel research proposal (not canon) explains the blue as silicon surface states.

### General Population Discipline
- **Category:** method term
- **Definition:** Out of fiction: the rule that any finding about a place's architecture, senses, fashion, religion, daily life or robot culture describes its general population by default, not one profession's, ritual's or context's version; a narrow version must be presented as a scoped exception on top of a genuine general answer. The ULM notes it inverts below about thirty people (Band 1), where there is no general population.
- **Status:** project canon (binding since 2026-08-16)
- **Used in:** InnerTepeniaGDD; DerivationMechanisms
- **Sources:** InnerTepeniaGDD `Districts/Phase_Instructions/00b_General_Population_Discipline.md` (original); `ULM/Disciplines/00b_General_Population_Discipline.md`

### Gen/Mark
- **Category:** robot biology
- **Also called:** Gen (the robot-side term); Gen-Mark
- **Definition:** A robot's generational identity. Mark, which has its own entry, is the generation of fabrication-chamber hardware and design specification (currently Mark IV, a placeholder number, designed at Neumayer); Gen is the robot's own generation, the robot-side counterpart, and the GDD stresses that the two terms are not interchangeable. Robots recognize and note it as a biographical fact, independent of their city because chambers ship nationwide, and it marks a difference in kind, never rank or hierarchy.
- **Status:** Timeline canon (Robot Universals Part III, Ch. 14; full text in DerivationMechanisms); project canon in the GDD (2026-07-09, corrected 2026-08-06/07).
- **Used in:** InnerTepeniaGDD; TepenianUniverseTimeline; CurrentNovelDocs (Starlit Engines; Maggie Aarden); DerivationMechanisms; Hybrid-Anthro_Culture

### Geography and access (founding rule)
- **Category:** method term
- **Also called:** DR-19 (its ruling, cited in about 250 files)
- **Definition:** Out of fiction: the governing founding rule that a nation may be a city's founder only because it could practically reach the site (geography, access, time-zone proximity), never because it built or ran the real research station there; the station, its operator and its start year are facts about inherited infrastructure only. The Register adds that a founding is never justified by comparison with another city, and recorded mechanisms such as the Jeju-do court allocation (Shirayuki, Sinheung) stand alongside geography (DR-44). See also GPS-purposes-only rule.
- **Status:** project canon (DR-19, DR-24)
- **Used in:** InnerTepeniaGDD (Cities; ULM; all city templates; CLAUDE.md); TepenianUniverseTimeline (No_National_Stereotypes); DerivationMechanisms
- **Sources:** InnerTepeniaGDD `ULM/DEVELOPER_RULINGS_LOG.md` (DR-19, DR-24, DR-44); `Cities/Founding_Register.md` (rules 2 and 3)

### Geography Is Character
- **Category:** method term
- **Definition:** Out of fiction: a binding world-design principle that a Tepenian city's culture, architecture, economy and psychology come first from its geography (climate, latitude, elevation, coast or inland, neighbors), so city design starts with geography. As a faction technique it derives a community's identity from the concrete conditions people lived under (climate, labor, density, isolation) by asking what survival required, what belief that produced, and what the group would do together once displaced; the GDD applies it to refugee groups from the Tepenian cities.
- **Status:** project canon (binding design principle; Toronto Fell Out's chosen faction method, though no factions exist there yet)
- **Used in:** InnerTepeniaGDD; TorontoFellOutGDD; CurrentNovelDocs (`reference/` copy); Outer Tepenia series
- **Sources:** InnerTepeniaGDD `General-Overview-Notes/Game_Design_Principles.md` ("Geography Is Character"); `Worldspace/Factions/Hometown-based_Faction_Possibilities.md`; TorontoFellOutGDD `Worldspace/Factions/Hometown-based_Faction_Possibilities.md`

### Great Lakes DMZ
- **Category:** place
- **Also called:** DMZ (New North America); the DMZ
- **Definition:** A demilitarized strip on the post-2083 map of North America, a narrow band through southern and central Ontario between the Great Lakes and Quebec; it is not a nation and has no Falkland Treaty signatory status. Toronto Fell Out is set inside it in 2097, shortly after the 2083 war: Toronto was never claimed or rebuilt and remains a quarantined ruin behind a guarded perimeter. The game is set in the Tepenian universe, and its human protagonist is deliberate: robots technically exist by then, but that is not generally known, and they are absent from most of the world, so there are none in the Toronto area (author, 2026-10-30).
- **Status:** Timeline canon for the DMZ (a map label); project canon that Toronto Fell Out is set there (author, 2026-07-29) and that the game is Tepenian (author, 2026-10-30); what conflict or settlement produced the DMZ is open.
